0. Current behavior (baseline)

Single note every 3–7 seconds with a 20% chance of rest. This is what the Library harpsichord does today — included here for A/B comparison.

1. Grace note + hold

A quick appoggiatura a step above the main note, landing on the main note ~80ms later, held long. Classic baroque ornamentation — two notes per cluster.

♩♩♯♯♯♯♯♯ · · · · · · ♩♩♯♯♯♯♯♯ · · · · ·

2. Rolled chord

Three or four scale tones rolled up or down with ~80ms between notes, like a harpsichordist arpeggiating a chord. Then a long breath.

♩♩♩♩♯♯♯♯ · · · · · · · · ♩♩♩♯♯♯♯ · ·

3. Trill and rest

Quick alternation between two adjacent scale tones (5–7 notes at ~55ms spacing), then a long silence. Very baroque, ornate.

♩♩♩♩♩♩ · · · · · · · · · · · ♩♩♩♩♩ · ·

4. Question & answer

Two rising notes (question), a short breath, then three descending notes (answer) with the last held long. A conversational phrase, then silence.
