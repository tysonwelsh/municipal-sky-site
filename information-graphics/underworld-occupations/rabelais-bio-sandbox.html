François Rabelais

c. 1483/1494 – 1553 · French Renaissance humanist, physician, and satirist

  • A Franciscan friar who left the cloister to study medicine in Lyon and Montpellier.
  • Practiced as a physician while writing under the anagrammatic pseudonym Alcofribas Nasier.
  • Author of Gargantua and Pantagruel: a bawdy, encyclopedic, riotously satirical five-book cycle (1532–1564).
  • Repeatedly condemned by the Sorbonne for obscenity and heresy; protected by powerful patrons including King François I.

Book 2, Chapter 30 of Pantagruel sends the scholar Epistemon on a brief tour of Hades, where the great men of antiquity have been reduced to menial trades. The chapter is the data source for everything that follows.
