Coordination number @1.8 ÅiNeighbours within 1.8 Å of each atom, counted against full periodic images — an atom bonded across the cell boundary still counts. The bonds drawn in the cloud are intra-box only, so a CN 3 atom near a face may show fewer than three lines. CN 3 is graphitic sp², CN 4 diamond-like sp³; CN 1–2 are chain ends and CN 5+ over-coordination. Click a class to isolate it, shift-click to add. Density and mean CN below are computed from the relaxed cell.

Radial distribution function g(r)iThe density of atom pairs at separation r, normalised so a uniform structure sits at 1. Computed with periodic images out to the smaller of 10 Å and half the cell's thinnest width. The first peak is the bond length, the second the first-neighbour shell; where the curve settles to 1 the structure has lost all order. Dashed line: the 1.8 Å bond/CN cutoff. Dotted lines: graphite (1.42 Å) and diamond (1.54 Å) first-neighbour distances. Brush a range of r to highlight every atom pair at that separation in the cloud — available to 5 Å, capped at 120,000 pairs — and click outside the band to clear. With a sequence active, the other stages draw as gray ghost curves.

Bond lengths < 1.8 ÅiEvery intra-box bond shorter than the 1.8 Å cutoff, binned at 0.02 Å; bonds crossing the box boundary are excluded. Bars carry the same ramp as the Bond strain toggle — red compressed, neutral at the median, blue stretched — scaled symmetrically about the median, so colour reads as strain magnitude rather than absolute length. Brush a range to select those bonds in the cloud; click outside the band to clear.

Rings by sizeiShortest-path (Franzblau) rings of size 3–10 on the periodic bond network: a cycle counts as a ring only if no shortcut exists between any two of its atoms. Solid bar: rings lying entirely inside the box, drawn in the cloud. Faded bar: rings crossing the box boundary — counted here, but not drawable. The 6-ring is the graphitic reference state; 5- and 7-rings are the characteristic defects of a disordered network. Click a size to isolate those rings, shift-click to add.
