Live engine tap. PLAY launches the actual Library track from prosperos-jukebox-audio.js. The spiral now taps the engine's master output, so every audible event (drone, harpsichord, music box, hum, ambient events) feeds the FFT and appears on the coil.

3D Pitch Spiral. One full turn per octave, 96 spectral samples per turn. The baseline traces a constant-radius cylinder; each sample lifts upward by its current spectral magnitude. Octave labels (C1–C7) are fixed on the left, anchored to each octave's vertical range.

Honest mapping. Peak height is dB-normalized linearly in the window [-75 dB, -10 dB]. No per-octave tilt, no spectral blur, no gamma lift. Bins between samples are linearly interpolated — no invented smoothness. The drone will dominate visually because it dominates the signal; quieter events stay short and brief because they are.

Pulse Constellation floor. Below the spiral, on a horizontal plane, the constellation viz from the main app is laid out. Each active drone partial sits at its pitch-class angle (radius = octave) with a growing waveform halo whose ring expands across the cycle and whose shape is the actual sound wave slowed 60×. A connecting line shows the interval between the two partials. Looking down through the spiral (default view) lets you see the constellation as a floor; spinning lets you walk around the whole structure.

Drag the canvas to rotate (horizontal = yaw, vertical = pitch). AUTO toggles slow continuous rotation; VIEW resets the camera.
