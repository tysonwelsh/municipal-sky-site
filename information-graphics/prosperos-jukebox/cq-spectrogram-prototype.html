Constant-Q Spectrogram

~ honest log-frequency view of the live mix ~

VOL READY

X axis: time, ~30-second window, scrolling left. Y axis: pitch on a log scale, 12 bins per octave (one bin per semitone). Octave gridlines at A1–A7. Color: spectral magnitude in dB (see swatch).

Drone partials show up as steady horizontal lines. Markov-driven layers (harpsichord, ghost tones, chimes, whistle) trace moving pitch ladders. Ambient events appear as transient streaks. Noise events (breeze, wind, leaf rustle) show up as broad vertical bands rather than discrete pitches.
