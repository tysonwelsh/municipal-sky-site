Waterphone Sandbox

~ for Sycorax's Spell ~

Three different synthesis approaches to a waterphone-like sound. Real waterphones produce inharmonic partials (frequencies that don't follow integer multiples of the fundamental), with slow pitch drift from sloshing water and a long bowed swell. Each approach below gets at that character differently. Listen, compare, pick a favorite — or hybrid bits from several.

1. Bowed Steel Bowl

Additive · inharmonic partials

Six sine oscillators at non-integer frequency ratios (1, 1.41, 2.07, 2.95, 4.13, 5.62 — borrowed from real waterphone spectra). Each partial has its own slow pitch LFO (≈0.3 Hz, ±0.5%) for the water-shimmer wobble. Upper partials add subtle amplitude tremolo for metallic shimmer. Long bowed swell envelope.

ready

2. Wailing Spirit

FM · dense modulation

A sine carrier at the fundamental, frequency-modulated by another sine at ×1.41 (√2 — pleasingly inharmonic). Modulation index swells from zero, peaks during sustain, then fades — generating a dense, wavering metallic spectrum. Includes slow downward glissando, a deep water wobble, and a sub-octave sine for body.

ready

3. Sea Bell

Hybrid · partials + bloom

A combination of voices 1 and 2. The inharmonic partial structure from #1 provides the ringing upper register that rises above the drone, with stronger shimmer-tremolo on the top three partials. The swelling FM modulator from #2 layers on top, blooming into the sustain. A shared deep water wobble (≈0.6 Hz) breathes through the lower partials and the FM carrier together, while a mild downward glissando ties it all together. Sliders below tune all five knobs live.
