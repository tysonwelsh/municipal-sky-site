SECTION 03

About — Long-form Prose

I'm Tyson. This site collects creative explorations, digital experiments, and the occasional poem. The visual language here is what I'd call throwback digital functionalism: clean lines, monospaced chrome, and a refusal to pretend the page is anything other than rendered text on a glowing rectangle.

Form follows function. Color is reserved for signal — a single ink-red and ink-blue do all the work. Decoration that doesn't carry information is removed. Inline links get a hairline underline and nothing more; the medium is honest about being a hyperlinked document.

What you'll find here

  • Information graphics, mostly built in d3 and SVG.
  • Short essays and poems.
  • Small chatbots and language experiments.

What you won't find

Drop shadows, rounded corners, gradient hero sections, animation: bounce, or any other ornament that doesn't earn its pixels.

Data & Technical

Numerical data and technical content render in monospace by default. The .data-panel component is intended for small reference tables — file sizes, run times, parameter dumps — that should look like terminal output, not like a feature card.
