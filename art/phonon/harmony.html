Phonon × harmony — two integration mockups

Both use the irradiated-graphite-T9 mode spectrum (6 picked peaks). They demonstrate opposite directions of control: in Level 1 the app's harmony steers the material; in Level 3 the material tunes the app's instruments. Starting one stops the other.

Level 1

Anchored transposition — the drone follows the harmony

The spectrum's dominant peak is declared the fundamental; choosing a root transposes the entire spectrum by one ratio (all internal ratios untouched — the data stays rigid). New roots crossfade in the way an overlapping-cycle drone would take a modulation: the old chord dies in the old key.

Material intonation — the instruments play the material's scale

The peak ratios are octave-folded into a scale (below). A seeded music-box improvises in that tuning over the untransposed drone — the melody instrument is tuned by graphite. Flip the toggle to hear the same melody quantized to 12-TET and back: the difference is the material's intonation.
