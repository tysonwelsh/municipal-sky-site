## Top 5 — scout these for videos first
1. **https://filmmirasim.ktb.gov.tr/** — the only site in the beat where I confirmed an open HLS manifest against a pre-1960 state film archive, with era-browse built in. Highest ratio of (deep-cut × era fit × readable × licence) in the register.
2. **https://www.aparat.com/** — the deep-cut motherlode for Persian: NIRT idents, test cards, 1970s ads, sign-offs, uploaded by Iranians for Iranians. Named extractor, so it is the cheapest real material in the beat. Licence is per-upload — pick items that are plainly state-broadcast ephemera.
3. **https://modernegypt.bibalex.org/** — 2 004 documentary videos, 1799–1981, in Egypt's own national library, catalogued in Arabic. Confirm playback on one item first; if it plays, this is the Egyptian newsreel tier at source.
4. **https://www.kan.org.il/lobby/archive/** — IBA 1968–2017 + Educational TV 1966–2018, free by the broadcaster's own statement. Needs a browser session (Cloudflare), so budget a real browser for it, not a scraper.
5. **https://51.com.kw/** — the Gulf entry that actually exists: Kuwait's Ministry of Information serving its own 1977-onward tape behind a free signup. Nothing else in the Gulf put its own broadcast past online.

Runner-up worth one look: **https://jfc.org.il/** for the free tier of יומני כרמל newsreels and the home-movie collections.

## Search notes

**What worked, per language.**
- *Turkish.* The productive path was not the broadcaster but the **ministry**: `film arşivi` + `ktb.gov.tr` reached filmmirasim, which is the single best find in the beat. `TRT arşiv` now resolves to tabii — the standalone archive site is gone, and every Turkish "nostalgia archive" result above that is a TikTok or YouTube compilation. There is **no Turkish equivalent of fernsehmuseum.info**: the kapanış/jenerik collector culture lives entirely on YouTube and TikTok. Do not spend a video round looking for one.
- *Persian.* `آرشیو` alone is useless (it means any news archive). What worked: the artefact names — `آرم شبکه`, `پایان برنامه`, `تصویر آزمایشی`, `تبلیغات قدیمی`, `تلویزیون ملی ایران`. These are Aparat/Namasha search terms, not site-discovery terms: **run them inside Aparat, not in a search engine**. Telewebion is IRIB's own but its archive only starts at 1393/2014 — the pre-revolution tape reaches the public through Aparat uploads and through IRIB's own retrospective programme «خاطره».
- *Hebrew.* `ארכיון השידור` reaches Kan; `ארכיון הסרטים הישראלי` reaches jfc.org.il. Note the split: **Kan and jfc host their own video; the Spielberg archive and the State Archives publish to YouTube.** That split is the single most useful fact for an Israel video round.
- *Arabic.* The hard lesson of this beat: **Arab broadcasters did not build archive portals — their archive communities are on YouTube and Facebook.** Egypt, Morocco, Jordan, Kuwait, Iraq and Algeria all have famous, active `أرشيف …` pages; all of them are excluded by rule. The native holdings that *are* online sit in **libraries and archives, not broadcasters**: Bibliotheca Alexandrina (Egypt), the Palestinian Museum, UMAM (Lebanon), NLA (UAE), Darah (Saudi). Kuwait's 51 is the one broadcaster-side exception.

**Dead ends worth recording.** Med-Mem (medmem.eu) is gone, and with it the only pooled online home for EPTV, RTT, Télé Liban, SNRT, ERTU and JRTV clips — that single death explains most of the Maghreb and Levant gaps above. Iraq's archive was looted in 2003 and is being restored offline. Sudan's is being digitised offline in Bergen. Afghan Film was dissolved in 2025. Télé Liban's archive is being proposed for UNESCO *Memory of the World* and is promised on "a platform, soon" — recheck in a year.

**Before a video round goes in.** (a) Kan needs a real browser — Cloudflare 403s every scripted client, and yt-dlp names `generic:impersonate` as the workaround. (b) palarchive.org 429s quickly; space requests by seconds. (c) filmmirasim's manifests are plain HLS on port **8443** and the generic extractor will not find them from the page URL — read the `playlist.m3u8` out of the HTML. (d) bibalex's ASP.NET stack throws 500s under any load, and the collection path is `DocumentaryVideos`, not `DocumentaryFilms`. (e) On Aparat and Namasha the licence is per-upload: prefer items that are self-evidently state-broadcast ephemera over a collector's rip of a feature.

**Audio-only, noted and not registered (type 10 deferred).** `trtdinle.com/genre/trt-arsiv` is TRT's archive-radio front end and looks substantial; `iranseda.ir` is IRIB's radio archive; Kan carries the Kol Israel archive (1917–2017) as audio on its own site. All three are worth the audio pass when it comes.
