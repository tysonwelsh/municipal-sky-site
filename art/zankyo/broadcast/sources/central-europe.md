## Top 5 — scout these for videos first
1. **https://www.oldradio.cz/seznamtv.htm** — raw `.mpg` files of the 1953 ČST monoskop, a station opening and 1980s znělky sitting on a static Czech collector site. Deepest cut in the beat, and the only source here that needs no player at all.
2. **https://filmhiradokonline.hu/** — the whole Hungarian newsreel century in the National Film Institute's own player, indexed and stable. Densest single vein; the player hides its stream in JS, so send a scout who can read one.
3. **https://repozytorium.fn.org.pl/** — FINA's repository serves plain `fragment.mp4`, and carries Polska Kronika Filmowa beside documentary and animation. The easiest real fetch in the beat, and a native home for the pool's `dziennik-telewizyjny-1984` neighbours.
4. **https://arhiva.mrt.com.mk/** (with https://play.mrt.com.mk/c/7) — a whole small country's broadcast memory, digitised, on its own host, essentially absent from YouTube. Cloudflare in front: go browser-shaped.
5. **https://rtsplaneta.rs/sr_lat/category/137/programski-arhiv** — Belgrade Television back to 1970 (*Novogodišnji kolaž 1970*) with `.mp4` in the page, plus the *Trezor* strand at rts.rs as a map of what RTS has already cleared.

Runner-up worth one look: **https://obljetnica.hrt.hr/glasovi-vremena/spikeri-kroz-povijest-hrt-a** — HRT's announcers indexed by person and decade, HTML5 audio, 1926 onward.

## Search notes
- **The pattern of this whole beat:** the national film archives here are, almost without exception, catalogue-only or on YouTube (Albania, Serbia's Kinoteka, Croatia's Kinoteka, Romania's ANF, Bosnia's Kinoteka). The *broadcasters* and the *newsreel studios*, by contrast, have built their own players (Hungary's Filmhíradók Online, FINA's repository, MRT, RTS Planeta, ČT, BNT). Search the broadcaster and the newsreel first, the cinematheque last.
- **Terms that worked.** PL: `kronika filmowa`, `Polska Kronika Filmowa`, `repozytorium cyfrowe`, `archiwum` — `plansze`/`testcard` led only to the Fandom wiki. CZ: `monoskop`, `znělky`, `Československý filmový týdeník`, `archiv` — `monoskop` is the single best Czech word, it went straight to oldradio.cz. SK: `Slovenský filmový ústav`, `filmy online`, `archív`. HU: `filmhíradó`, `Magyar Világhíradó`, `ingyen nézhető`, `archívum` — `adás vége`/`monoszkóp` belong to the Videa/Indavideo layer, not the institutions. RO: `jurnal de actualități` + `Sahia` is the only pair that finds digitised newsreel; `arhiva TVR` finds press releases. BG: `кинопреглед`, `филмов архив`, `видеоархив`. SR/HR/BS: `архива`, `Трезор`, `стare emisije`, `špica`; `obljetnica` (anniversary) is a surprisingly good Croatian keyword — broadcasters build commemorative microsites and put the ephemera there. SI: `baza slovenskih filmov`, `arhiv`. MK: `МРТ архива`, `дигитализирана архива`. SQ: `arkiva`, `kinostudio` — both lead to YouTube.
- **"Anniversary microsite" is a genre.** obljetnica.hrt.hr and play.mrt.com.mk's "50 ГОДИНИ МРТ" are both broadcaster birthday sites, and both hold exactly the continuity material the station wants. Worth trying `<broadcaster> + obljetnica / godišnjica / évforduló / výročí / rocznica / годишнина` in every remaining beat.
- **Bot walls to expect.** `filmovyprehled.cz` returns 403, `sfu.sk` returns 466, `kinoteka.mk` refused the connection, `arhiva.mrt.com.mk` is behind a Cloudflare challenge (yt-dlp needs `--extractor-args "generic:impersonate"`), `ceskatelevize.cz/porady/…` 403s scripted fetches and the old `/porady/` URL shape now returns **410 Gone** — the `CeskaTelevize` extractor wants current `/ivysilani/` item URLs. None of these are refusals of a human browser; a video scout should come in browser-shaped and slow.
- **ČT's archive is decade-bucketed**, which is unusually convenient: `/ivysilani/kategorie/4732-archiv/` with children `4734-60-leta`, `4736-80-leta`, `4739-televize` (programmes *about* television), plus a standalone `13325200960-reklamy-80-leta` (1980s Czechoslovak advertising) and the `/vse-o-ct/historie/videoarchiv/` history shelf from 1953. That is the best-organised archive entry point in the beat.
- **Players are nearly all script-resolved.** Only `repozytorium.fn.org.pl` (`fragment.mp4`), `rtsplaneta.rs` (`.mp4`) and `oldradio.cz` (`.mpg`) put a media URL in the server HTML. Everywhere else — Ninateka, KronikaRP, filmhiradokonline.hu, TVR+, BNT, obljetnica.hrt.hr — the page is an SPA or resolves the stream in JS, so `yt-dlp` generic returns "Unsupported URL" even where the video is plainly free. Read that column as *"no extractor and no naive fallback"*, not as *"unreachable"*.
- **Legal-deposit walls.** NAVA (hu) and BSF (si) both gate most of their holdings behind institutional access — NAVA-pontok in Hungarian libraries, a Slovene library card at bsf.si. Both are out of scope by the no-login rule, but both are worth keeping as *maps*: they tell you a title exists and who holds it.
- **Audio (type 10, deferred) tripped over in passing, noted in one line:** Český rozhlas's archive, Polskie Radio's `Archiwum`, and BNR's "Златен фонд" all have native front ends; oldradio.cz is half a radio site and would serve the audio tier as well as the video one.
- **What the pool already has vs what is here:** the pool's `cst-vysilani-znelky`, `ct2-testcard-1994` and `dziennik-telewizyjny-1984` all now have native homes in this register (oldradio.cz / ČT iVysílání, and FINA + KronikaRP + cyfrowa.tvp.pl respectively). `jrt-jugoslavija-spice` has a native home at RTS Planeta and, if MNE Play opens, at RTCG.
