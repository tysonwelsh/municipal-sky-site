# Southeast Asia — native sources register (`sea`)
Scout: Claude Opus 5, 2026-09-14.
Languages: Vietnamese, Thai, Indonesian, Malay, Filipino/Tagalog, Khmer, Burmese,
Lao, Iban, Tetum/Portuguese (Timor-Leste).
Territories: Vietnam, Thailand, Indonesia, Malaysia, Philippines, Cambodia,
Myanmar, Laos, Brunei, Timor-Leste.
Probe tool: `~/anaconda3/bin/yt-dlp -s --no-playlist` (2026.08.19), one page per site.

**The shape of this locale, in one paragraph.** Southeast Asia keeps its old
television in three places and almost never in a fourth. (a) *State archives that
catalogue but do not stream* — ANRI in Jakarta, Viện phim Việt Nam and Lưu trữ
quốc gia III in Hanoi, the National Archives of Thailand: rich finding aids, real
1950s–70s newsreel series, no player. (b) *One archive per country that actually
put video on the web*, and it is usually not the broadcaster: Bophana in Phnom
Penh, Arkib Negara Malaysia's registration-gated Online Finding Aid, the Thai Film
Archive's in-building VOD, Indonesiana.TV. (c) *Commercial OTT platforms with a
shallow archive tier* — Vidio, RCTI+, CH3Plus, HPlus, RTMKlik — where the
1980s–90s survives as drama and sinetron, not as continuity. The fourth place,
the collector who rips his own VHS to a native host, barely exists here: SEA's
ident-and-sign-off collectors are on YouTube, Facebook and TikTok almost without
exception, and Pantip / Kaskus threads embed those. So the deep-cut tier in this
beat is *institutional*, not *fannish*, and the single most exploitable fact in
this register is that Arkib Negara Malaysia gives every registered user a free
**90-second video preview** of any digitised holding — which is longer than a
ZANKYŌ window needs.

## Top 5 — scout these for videos first
1. **https://ofa.arkib.gov.my/portal/** — 2 746 digitised hours of Malaysian state newsreel and broadcast, and a **free 90-second preview per item** that is longer than any window ZANKYŌ cuts. The legal path is already built. (Read its `Disallow: /` robots.txt first: human browsing only, and the owner should decide whether a hand-driven visit is acceptable.)
2. **https://memory.bophana.com/archives** — 2 300+ videos of 1960s Khmer newsreel, Sihanouk's own films and post-1979 TVK, free, trilingual, robots-open, and with no YouTube twin. The highest ratio of strangeness to effort in the beat.
3. **https://anri.go.id/** — *Gelora Indonesia*, 1951–1976, a weekly Indonesian national newsreel catalogued item by item. No player yet, so this is a written request, not a fetch; but it is the largest untouched newsreel run in Southeast Asia.
4. **https://indonesiana.tv/** — the Directorate-General of Culture's own streaming channel, carrying the state restoration programme's 1950s features (*Darah dan Doa* 1950, *Dr Samsi* 1952) plus cultural documentary. A real native player, publicly free, and the cleanest licence story in the beat.
5. **https://www.fapot.or.th/** — tens of thousands of Thai newsreels, ads, home movies and educational films, reachable only by going to Salaya or writing to them. Worth the letter: nothing in this register is richer.

*Also, if the round has room:* **https://db.sac.or.th/anthroarchives/** (Thai fieldwork film, free, and the one site here that told us in writing how it wants to be read), and **https://www.vidio.com/** searched for `iklan jadul` — it has a working extractor, a genuine Indonesian centre of gravity, and is the one place in this beat where a private collector's tape rip lands on a native host instead of YouTube.

## Search notes
**What worked, per language.**
- **Thai.** `หอภาพยนตร์` + `ภาพยนตร์ข่าว` finds fapot immediately; the important discovery is that its VOD is a *building*, not a website (`ระบบ VOD` appears only in the library page copy). `จดหมายเหตุ` is the word that separates record-archives (nat.go.th, SAC) from film archives (fapot). `ย้อนหลัง` (catch-up) pulls commercial OTT; `รายการเก่า` pulls the genuinely archival tier — that exact phrase is what surfaced Thai PBS's old-programme index.
- **Vietnamese.** `tư liệu` and `lưu trữ` are the load-bearing words, not `phim cũ`. `tra cứu phim lưu trữ` goes straight to the Vietnam Film Institute catalogue. `hình hiệu` and `kết thúc phát sóng` returned essentially nothing outside YouTube — Vietnamese ident culture is not on a native host.
- **Indonesian.** `arsip` + `audiovisual` is the pair that works; `jadul` (old-timey) is the collector word and lives on Vidio, TikTok and Kaskus. The single best string of the whole beat was **`daftar arsip film PPFN seri Gelora Indonesia 1951-1976`**, which returns ANRI's own inventory PDF — an item-level list of a 25-year national newsreel run.
- **Malay.** `arkib` (archive) is the key; `tontonan dalam talian` (online viewing) separates the portals that stream from the ones that only describe. `Filem Negara Malaysia` is the name to search, not "Malaysian newsreel".
- **Khmer.** `បណ្ណសារ` works, but in practice every road leads to Bophana; TVK has no archival web presence at all.
- **Filipino/Tagalog.** `lumang patalastas` and `pagsasara` returned nothing native — Philippine broadcast nostalgia is wholly on Facebook and YouTube. The institutional words (`FDCP`, `Philippine Film Archive`, `RTVM`) are what pay.
- **Burmese / Lao.** Near-total absence. `မှတ်တမ်း` (record/documentary) surfaced Save Myanmar Film; Lao returned only LNTV's YouTube.

**What a video scout should know before going in.**
1. **The 90-second rule.** Arkib Negara Malaysia's free preview is 90 s of video and 120 s of audio per item. That is the single most useful number in this register — but the site's robots.txt is `Disallow: /`, so put it in front of the owner as a *human browsing* decision, not an automated fetch.
2. **Three sites ship anti-bot.** juanflix.com.ph and anri.go.id sit behind Cloudflare challenges; rtmklik 403s a bare fetch. None of them should be pushed past — the round's etiquette rules that out, and the impersonation flag is not in scope.
3. **db.sac.or.th publishes a machine-readable policy.** It names ClaudeBot explicitly, allows it with a **20-second crawl delay**, and sets a `Content-Signal` header that permits indexing but **forbids AI-training use**. Honour both. It is the only site in the beat that spelled this out, and honouring it costs nothing.
4. **Two rows are time-sensitive.** JuanFlix halted in September 2025 with a relaunch announced for early 2026 — check it is serving. IVAA's archive portal was mid-redevelopment with a 7 October 2025 reopening date. Both could be live now or still dark.
5. **Save Myanmar Film is a YouTube front.** Its own site is a WordPress shell; the one probe resolved straight to a YouTube video id. It is still the best door to the Myanmar National Film Archive as an *institution* — but the video is not native, and Myanmar therefore has no native host in this register beyond the unreachable MRTV.
6. **Three hosts were unreachable from this machine** and should be retried before anyone concludes they are dead: anri.sikn.go.id (connection refused), mrtv.gov.mm (connection refused), vnews.vnanet.vn (network unreachable — looked like an IPv6-only route).
7. **The type-5 tier does not exist here.** Twelve searches in five scripts for ident, sign-off, test-card and old-advertisement collectors turned up Pantip threads, Kaskus threads, TikTok hashtags and Facebook pages — all of them embedding YouTube or Facebook video. If the owner wants SEA ident culture, it has to come from the broadcasters' and archives' own holdings, or it comes from YouTube. That is a real finding about this locale, not a gap in the search.
8. **The best material in this beat is not on the web at all**, and three institutions are worth a letter rather than a fetch: the Thai Film Archive (tens of thousands of newsreels and ads in an in-building VOD), Sinematek Indonesia (2 750 films, no catalogue), and Mowelfund (16/35 mm and Super 8, digitising now). ANRI is the fourth and the largest.
9. **Type 10 sighting, noted and not registered** (audio is deferred): **RRI — Radio Republik Indonesia**, on air since 1945, whose historical recordings sit inside ANRI's 59 000-reel audiovisual transfer alongside the TVRI and PPFN material. When the audio pass happens, ANRI is one door to both.
10. **Cross-beat handoffs.** *far-languages*: db.sac.or.th (Thai minority-language fieldwork film), sarawakiana.com (Iban), lntv.gov.la (Khmu-language LNTV broadcasts). *romance-west*: uc.pt/camstl is a Timorese archive hosted in Portugal and may fit their etiquette better than this beat's.
