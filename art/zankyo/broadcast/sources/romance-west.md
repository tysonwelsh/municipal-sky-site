## Top 5 — scout these for videos first

1. **https://patrimonio.archivioluce.com/** — the giant, and it is already machine-readable: generic pulls an m3u8 straight off a detail page. Seventy years of Italian state newsreel, 70 000 films, with titles, fanfares and announcer voice that cut to 4–12 s windows without argument. One site could carry a dozen reels.
2. **https://memoire.ciclic.fr/** (via `/embed/<id>`) — 14 986 amateur films, HLS+DASH readable through the embed, free, no login. The highest deep-cut-per-effort ratio in the beat: nobody has re-uploaded a 1958 pétanque contest at Dordives.
3. **https://cinememoire.net/** — publishes direct MP4s in a `videositemap.xml`. Marseille and the former French colonies, 1930s on. Trivially readable and genuinely unreachable elsewhere.
4. **https://www.rtve.es/play/** (NO-DO at `rtve.es/filmoteca`) — the native home of a station the pool already carries via a mirror, with a working extractor, plus the TVE *carta de ajuste* and *cierre de emisión* the pool lacks. Retry the probe: it 503'd twice under light load, not a licence or access problem.
5. **https://pasouoquepasou.crtvg.gal/** — a minority-language broadcaster's own archive portal with .m3u8 in the page, off YouTube, in Galician. Type 8 and type 1 at once, and the cheapest way to add a language the pool has none of.

## Search notes

**What worked, per language.**

- **French.** The productive path was not "archive" but the *institution type*: `cinémathèque régionale` + `films amateurs` + a region name. France has a federation of regional film archives (FCAFF / the "Amorce" network) and they nearly all run the same CMS — URLs shaped `slug-NNNN-0-0-0.html`. Ciclic (Centre-Val de Loire), Cinémathèque de Bretagne, MIRA (Alsace), Normandie Images and Ciné-Archives are all on it. **Finding one finds the family**: a video scout should sweep the FCAFF member list and `cinematheque-documentaire.org/le-reseau-national` for the ones I didn't reach (Savoie, Nouvelle-Aquitaine, Occitanie, Hauts-de-France). Warning: the CMS itself is **not** yt-dlp-readable, and several members outsource streaming to Vimeo — check the player before registering.
- **French, continuity specifically.** `mire`, `fin des programmes`, `interlude` mostly lead to *forums* (forum.retrotechnique.org, tvnt.net) and blogs (vivelapub.fr), not to hosted video. The forums are worth reading as a **finding aid** — they name the exact mires (Hourriez "escargot" 1959–74, Marly horse, Niçoise, TDF 1975) — then search those names inside INA and Dailymotion. Don't register the forums.
- **Italian.** `cinegiornale` and `archivio storico` go straight to Luce; `film di famiglia` / `cinema amatoriale` finds Home Movies and Superottimisti; `cinema d'impresa` finds the Ivrea industrial collection. The big trap: Italy's best industrial archive (CIAN, 82 000 reels) publishes **only to YouTube**. The `nuvolar.it`-hosted "La mia Olivetti" is the one non-YouTube door found into it — look for more project microsites of this shape rather than the parent institution's own domain.
- **Spanish and the co-official languages.** The regional broadcasters are the win and they are unevenly readable: CCMA (Catalan), Crtvg (Galician) and Canalsurmas (Andalusian) all have named yt-dlp extractors; EITB (Basque) does not, and was mid-migration from *Nahieran* to *ETB On* when scouted. The **filmotecas are a dead end as video sources** — Española, Catalunya, Vasca, Andalucía, Valenciana are all either on-site consultation, YouTube, or Vimeo. Search `carta de ajuste` / `cierre de emisión` inside RTVE rather than on the open web, where those terms return only Wikipedia and nostalgia blogs.
- **Portuguese.** `arquivos.rtp.pt` is the whole story and it is a WordPress site with an open sitemap and a genuinely deep collection (1930s on, *Meio Século de Televisão*, *História da RTP*). Note that the `RTP` extractor did **not** cover the `arquivos.` subdomain — a video scout needs to solve that one host, and it unlocks Portuguese continuity wholesale. Cinemateca Digital is Vimeo; SAPO Vídeos is gone.
- **Catalan / Basque / Galician / Occitan / Romansh / Maltese (type 8).** Galician (`pasouoquepasou`) and Romansh (RTR Fundus) are the two genuinely reachable minority signals. Catalan and Basque are reachable through their broadcasters. **Occitan is blocked** (ÒC tele is a YouTube/Vimeo front end) and **Maltese is unreachable** — PBS's archive is not online at all. If the owner wants Maltese in the pool, it will take a request to PBS, not a scout.
- **Swiss-French / Swiss-Italian.** The single best structural find of the beat: **FONSART's participatory platforms** — `notrehistoire.ch` (Romandy, 2009) and `lanostrastoria.ch` (Ticino, 2017) — which pool private 8/9.5/16 mm film *and* seeded RTS/RSI broadcast archives on one free, non-YouTube site. Neither is yt-dlp-readable (SPA; `/entries/<id>` redirects to `/documents/<ulid>`), so a video scout will need to read the document API. Worth the effort: this is material with no other home.

**Dead ends worth not repeating.** Searching for collector communities (`mires`, `monoscopio`, `cartas de ajuste`, `vinhetas`) in Romance languages returns almost nothing hosted natively — unlike the German and Japanese ident-collector scenes, the Romance-west collectors publish to YouTube and to Facebook groups. Type 5 is thin in this beat; spend the video round on types 1, 2, 6 and 7 instead.

**Before going in.** Two sites rate-limited or wobbled under a *single* probe: RTVE returned 503 twice in a row, and `patrimonio.archivioluce.com` 301s its own robots.txt. Ciclic publishes `Crawl-delay: 10`. GP Archives' robots.txt names `INA dlweb` explicitly, which means that site already watches for archive crawlers. Space requests, fetch one page at a time, and expect the two big state archives (INA, RAI) to be geo- and log-aware.

**Audio-only, noted in passing per the brief (type 10, deferred).** INA holds the French radio legal deposit at `ina.fr` alongside the TV; RTP Arquivos carries Emissora Nacional radio from 1936 in the same portal as its television. Both are already registered above for their video, so the audio pass gets them for free.
