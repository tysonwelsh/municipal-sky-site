## Top 5 — scout these for videos first

1. **https://vod.tfai.org.tw/Category/11** — 台影新聞, 1945–1990s, open to the whole internet, under the **Open Government Data Licence v1.0 (Tier A)**, ~485 pages deep, and yt-dlp has never heard of it. A state newsreel archive nobody has stripped. Everything else in this beat is Tier B; this is the one that is free to cut.
2. **Bilibili 收台/測試卡 collectors — 猫娘维佳Weglorya_ and 0851tvchannel** (rep BV1MA411Z7kd, BV1eB4y1h7xC, BV1Mf421Q7oN). The only place in the sinosphere where the caught-signal moment itself — sign-off, test card, ident, trailer, in sequence, from CCTV, TVB, 明珠台 and small provincial stations — has already been excerpted by people who care. **The probe succeeded**, so this is fetchable today.
3. **https://www.hkmemory.hk/** (時代光影：香港廣播史, 港台寫實劇, 香港節) — 90 years of Hong Kong broadcasting in Cantonese, free, native player, SPA-hidden media on slscdn.hkmemory.hk. The pool has six Hong Kong reels and not one of them came from Hong Kong.
4. **http://www.cndfilm.com/** — 中央新影, 42 000 reels / 420 000 minutes of 新闻简报. China's NO-DO, the exact genre the station receives. Carry the TLS warning: the host serves a mis-matched certificate and any verifying fetcher fails.
5. **https://www.nas.gov.sg/archivesonline/audiovisual_records** — Ministry of Culture films of the 1960s–70s in Mandarin **and Hokkien/Teochew/Cantonese**, plus the launch of Television Singapura (15 Feb 1963). State film, so the best Tier-A odds after TFAI, and it answers the pool's lone `sg-rediffusion-hokkien`.

Runner-up worth naming: **https://www.grs.gov.hk/tc/photo_n_video_gallery.html** serves plain `.mp4` files (e.g. `/img/photo_gallery/1.mp4`) with no player in the way — the easiest technical fetch in the whole beat, though the gallery itself is small.

## Search notes

**What worked, by script.** Searching in 正體字 and 简体字 finds *different* sites for the same thing, so run both: 「測試卡」 lands on Taiwan and Hong Kong material, 「测试卡」 on mainland. The single highest-yield term of this beat was **收台** (sign-off) — it is the native community's own word and it goes straight to the collectors. **測試卡 / 测试卡**, **台徽**, **開台/开台**, **收播** are the rest of the caught-signal vocabulary. For archives, **隨選視訊** (VOD) is the term Taiwanese institutions use for a public player, and it is what surfaced `vod.tfai.org.tw` where 「典藏」 and 「資料庫」 only surfaced catalogues. **新闻简报** goes to 中央新影; **科教片** and **教育片** to 上海科影厂 and 中国电影资料馆; **影像资源库** to 上海音像资料馆; **網上藏品集** to the Hong Kong GRS.

**The structural finding.** In this beat the fan/collector tier (§2 type 5) does **not** live on standalone sites the way fernsehmuseum.info or the Russian заставки pages do. It lives on Bilibili, inside UP主 spaces and tag pages. Two collectors carry most of it: **猫娘维佳Weglorya_** (CCTV / TVB / 明珠台, 測試卡 posted as reusable 素材) and **0851tvchannel** (provincial and city stations). A video scout should work their spaces and the 收台 tag rather than hunt for a Chinese equivalent of a test-card fan site — I looked, and the forums that would have hosted one are gone.

**Institutions split their material across hosts.** TFAI alone runs four: `www.tfai.org.tw` (institutional), `vod.tfai.org.tw` (the player — this is the one that matters), `docs.tfai.org.tw` (documentary index) and a wing inside `openmuseum.tw`. Chasing the institutional front page wastes a fetch every time.

**Technical traps to carry into the video round.**
- `vod.cts.com.tw` **fails the TLS handshake** for both yt-dlp and WebFetch (`SSLV3_ALERT_HANDSHAKE_FAILURE`). Use `--legacy-server-connect`.
- `www.cndfilm.com` serves a **mis-matched certificate** (a chinanetcenter/wscdns CDN bundle). WebFetch refuses it outright; plan for HTTP or `--no-check-certificate`, and tell the owner before anything is fetched.
- `sls.hkpl.gov.hk` (ex-MMIS) sits behind a **Queue-it waiting room**; the probe never reaches the site, it lands on a queue token. One patient session, no parallel requests.
- `openmuseum.tw` bounces some object URLs to a **/validUser bot gate**.
- Bilibili's documented 412 did **not** fire for me on a bare `yt-dlp -s --no-playlist`; the only wall was `1080P 高码率` asking for premium. Lower rates resolve fine. Keep the CURATE.md header recipe in reserve.
- CCTV ids look like `VIDE…….shtml` and must be harvested from a real listing page — constructed ids 404, and `jishi.cctv.com` index pages make the CCTV extractor fail on "unable to extract video id".

**Licence shape of the beat.** Almost everything here is Tier B. The two real Tier A openings are **TFAI's VOD under 政府資料開放授權條款-第1版** and **Open Museum, where 96 % of objects carry a 創用CC licence** — but Open Museum's licence is per-object and includes ND, which CURATE.md rules is Tier B, so it must be read object by object. Taiwan's government-open-data posture is the single biggest licence difference between this beat and the rest of the sinosphere: the PRC archives (中央新影, 上海音像资料馆, 中国电影资料馆) are state-owned and all-rights-reserved, and 上海音像资料馆 is an active commercial licensing house. Singapore's Ministry of Culture films are the other likely Tier A, on state-output grounds, but NAS states rights per record — check each one.

**Where the pool's existing sinosphere reels could be re-sourced natively.** `hk-tv-continuity-1979`, `hk-tv-news-1986`, `hk-tvb-pearl-weather-1987`, `hk-tvc-70s-90s`, `hk-atv-dx-1993` → hkmemory.hk and the Bilibili collectors (TVB/明珠台 material is there). `macau-tdm-02`, `macau-tdm-falar-1984` → macaumemory.mo and tdm.com.mo. `tw-ttv-commercials-1982`, `tw-ttv-dx-1991`, `tw-cts-morning-news-1980` → vod.tfai.org.tw and vod.cts.com.tw (ttv.com.tw/news/tdcm tells you what survives but will not hand it over). `cn-tv-set-ads-80s90s` → admuseum.cuc.edu.cn for provenance, Bilibili/Youku for the tape. `sg-rediffusion-hokkien` → nas.gov.sg, and for a mainland Hokkien companion, xmtv.cn / qztv.cn.

**Type 10 sighting, noted and not registered** (audio is deferred): 厦门广播电视台 has broadcast in 厦门话 continuously since **25 Dec 1949** and runs 闽南之声广播; and 中国音网 (a Chinese historical-recordings database) is carried by the National Library at nlc.cn. Both are worth the later audio pass.

**Dead ends.** 荔枝网 as a Guangdong archive does not exist as described — GRT (gdtv.cn) has no archive portal; the Guangdong material worth having is 1950s–80s newsreel held by 中央新影 and provincial archives with no web presence. Mainland *city* stations' 网络电视台 (qztv.cn is typical) are catch-up shells with nothing pre-2000 on them — the old tape is in the station, not on the site. And there is no public mainland equivalent of INA or Beeld & Geluid: 中国电影资料馆 streams only inside its own app, and 上海音像资料馆 sells licences. Everything old and mainland that is actually *reachable* has been put online by a private collector, which is why the Bilibili rows matter more here than in any other beat.
