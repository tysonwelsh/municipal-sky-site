# Japan and Korea — native sources register (`jp-kr`)
Scout: Claude Opus 5, 2026-09-14. Languages: Japanese (ja), Korean (ko).
Territories: Japan, South Korea. North Korea: no NK-run public web host with
playable video exists outside state portals that are dead or unreachable from
here (see rejected); the pool's KCTV reels stay the NK coverage.

Probe: `~/anaconda3/bin/yt-dlp -s --no-playlist` (2026.08.19), one
representative page per site. In the `readable` column —
`generic:ok` = the generic extractor resolved a playable format;
`generic:unsupported` = the page downloaded cleanly but the media sits behind a
JS/iframe player (in nearly every case here that player is fed a plain
progressive `.mp4` over XHR, so a video scout's first move is the network tab,
not another extractor).
**28 of the 30 registered domains are unknown to yt-dlp** — only nicovideo.jp
(`niconico`) and nhk.or.jp (`NhkVod`) have an extractor. Verified by grepping every
domain against `yt-dlp-extractors.txt`; the apparent `ktv` and `adic` hits are only
NRKTV / SAKTV / RadioRadicale.

## Top 5 — scout these for videos first

1. **https://filmisadocument.jp/** — 339 complete Japanese newsreels and cultural films on the national film archive's own player, and the generic extractor already resolves a format. Highest yield per request in the whole beat.
2. **https://archive.much.go.kr/** — KBS's pre-1970s film vault, 1 100+ records, published free and high-quality by the National Museum of Korean Contemporary History. Nothing here has been re-uploaded; the only cost is a watermark.
3. **https://www.ehistory.go.kr/** — 대한뉴스 issues 1–2040 (1953–1994) plus 미공개 대한뉴스, at the producing government's own portal, with an open-data catalogue. Replaces the pool's foreign-mirrored `daehan-news-1970` with a native, Tier-A source.
4. **https://www.kagakueizo.org/** — 1 060+ rescued Japanese industrial, science and education films, 1950s–70s, free and nowhere else. The type-9 motherlode; treat the one-server NPO gently.
5. **https://www.nicovideo.jp/** — tags 放送開始・終了 (1 765), 懐かCM 80年代, カラーバー, テスト放送: the actual home of Japanese sign-offs, test cards and 1980s ad blocks, in Japan's own host, with a working extractor. Enumerate tags in a browser (the tag playlist extractor returns 0 unauthenticated), then fetch watch pages one at a time.

Runners-up worth naming: **archives.seoul.go.kr/movie** for 「여기는 TV서울입니다」 alone, **tokyodouga.metro.tokyo.lg.jp** for 昭和 municipal record film, **kfilm.khistory.org** for 국립영화제작소 문화영화, and **adic.or.kr** for Korean ad breaks back to 1970.

## Search notes

**Japanese — what worked.** The productive axis was *institution words* + *genre words*, not station names. `記録映画` / `産業映画` / `文化映画` / `ニュース映画` reach the film-archive tier; `映像アーカイブ` + `デジタルアーカイブ` + a prefecture or city name reaches the municipal tier reliably (`広島`, `沖縄`, `埼玉`, `上田市`, `仙台` all produced hosts). `８ミリ` is the single best word for the home-movie tier — it pulled 沖縄アーカイブ研究所 and 20世紀アーカイブ仙台 in one query. For the ephemera itself, the reels-3 vocabulary holds up exactly: `放送開始・終了`, `クロージング`, `オープニング`, `試験放送`, `テスト放送`, `カラーバー`, `時報`, `懐かCM`, `VHS録画` are all live Niconico tags with counts attached, so you can size a haul before fetching anything.

**Japanese — dead ends.** `NHKアーカイブス` mostly returns the *on-site* 番組公開ライブラリー (50+ viewing booths, no web playback) rather than the web portal; www.nhk.or.jp also refuses WebFetch outright, so NHK has to be characterised from secondary sources and the existing yt-dlp extractors. Searching for ident/closing *collector* sites in Japanese lands on Fandom wikis and YouTube playlists almost every time — the real collector energy has migrated into Niconico tags, which is where a video scout should go instead of hunting for blogs. Newsreel *producers* (毎日映画社, 日映アーカイブ, テレビ朝日映像アーカイブ, 中日映画社) are all findable and all are licensing desks; their value is telling you what exists and who owns it, not giving you a file.

**Korean — what worked.** `기록영상` is the keyword that unlocks this locale — it appears in the name or navigation of almost every serious archive (현대사 기록영상 아카이브, 국가기록영상관, 영상기록). `아카이브` + an institution (`국가기록원`, `서울기록원`, `국립국악원`, `대한민국역사박물관`) is near-deterministic; Korean public bodies all put their holdings on a `go.kr` host with a JS player and no login, which makes this the friendlier half of the beat by a distance. `옛날 광고` / `추억의 광고` reaches the ad tier (adic.or.kr, tvcf.co.kr); `문화영화` reaches the state-propaganda-short tier and led to kfilm.khistory.org, which is the best-hidden thing found in either language.

**Korean — dead ends.** The broadcasters themselves (KBS, MBC, SBS) have all moved their retro output to YouTube channels or behind a business-registration paywall; do not spend time on `KBS 아카이브` as a phrase. The two exceptions are worth knowing: **bada.kbs.co.kr**, which KBS built deliberately as an open archive with free non-commercial download, and **archive.much.go.kr**, where KBS's film vault is published by a museum instead.

**Technical notes for the video round.**
- Only four of the 30 registered domains have a yt-dlp extractor (niconico, NHK, Naver, Kakao). Twenty-six do not, and of those only three resolved through the generic extractor (filmisadocument.jp, bada.kbs.co.kr, tv.busan.go.kr). The rest are `generic:unsupported` — but in every case the page itself downloads fine, which means the media is behind a JS or iframe player, not behind auth. The KR `go.kr` sites in particular are almost all plain progressive `.mp4` served over XHR; budget one browser-network-tab look per site rather than one extractor hunt per video.
- Niconico's `niconico:tag` extractor returned **0 items** unauthenticated on `/tag/放送開始・終了` even though the tag page shows 1 765 videos. Enumerate in a browser, then fetch watch URLs individually.
- `gongu.copyright.or.kr` answered an automated request with **HTTP 503** — it has a WAF that dislikes tooling. Pace it like a browser.
- `iha.go.kr` presents a TLS certificate yt-dlp's stack rejects (`WRONG_SIGNATURE_TYPE`); `kfilm.khistory.org` has no working HTTPS at all (WebFetch gets ECONNREFUSED on :443, plain http works). Both are reachable in a browser — don't write them off on a probe failure.
- Licence-wise the clean tier is: 공유마당 (explicit PD/CC), bada.kbs.co.kr (free non-commercial), 沖縄アーカイブ研究所 (CC BY), the Korean `go.kr` government archives (공공누리 posture), and the NARA-origin slice of 昭和館. Everything Japanese and institutional (NFAJ, NHK, 科学映像館) is all-rights-reserved on its face.

**Also seen, not registered (real but thin for this round).** `https://www2.nhk.or.jp/school/` — NHK for School, free and already served by the `NhkForSchool*` extractors, but the online catalogue is overwhelmingly post-2000; worth a look only if a pre-1990 series surfaces. `https://www.eizou.pref.saitama.lg.jp/library/` — 彩の国デジタルアーカイブ / SKIPシティ 映像公開ライブラリー, ~5 000 videos and ~24 000 stills run jointly with NHK's 番組公開ライブラリー, but playback is on facility terminals; useful purely as a public index of what NHK's programme library holds. `https://museum.umic.jp/` — 上田市デジタルアーカイブ, a clean worked example of the Japanese municipal tier (cross-search of one Nagano city's museums) if the round-5 scouts want to sample that tier by hand. `https://tv.naver.com/` — Naver TV; native KR platform with a working extractor, but almost nothing pre-2000 survives on it, so it is a fallback for geo-fenced broadcaster clips rather than a source.

**Type 10 (audio, deferred) — one line, as instructed.** http://www.asahi-net.or.jp/~uk9o-tkzw/tvstaann.html is a private collector's **1974–75 tape recordings of Japanese TV stations' sign-on/sign-off music and station-name announcements**, NHK and commercial, with a companion 1980s programme-recording index at `/tvlist.html` — audio only, so out of scope now, but it is the single best JP find for the audio pass and should be handed to whoever runs it.
