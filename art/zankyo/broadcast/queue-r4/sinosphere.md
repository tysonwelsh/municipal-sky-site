## Site notes

### vod.tfai.org.tw (台影新聞) — Tier A, and the best-documented source in the beat
- **Search really works, and it is not linked from the Category pages.** The endpoint is `https://vod.tfai.org.tw/Search?keyword=<urlencoded>` with `&page=N`; 20 results a page, pagination links are in the HTML. `Category/11` itself is 485 pages of listings at `?page=N`.
- **The item page hands over everything the cutter needs, in one fetch**: 出品時間 (year), 長度 (duration), 主題詞 (subject keywords, sometimes including `字幕 無` = no burnt-in subtitles), a **long dated 影片說明 that is effectively a shot list**, the licence sentence, and — the important part — **the direct MP4**: `https://vod.tfai.org.tw/api/getConvertVideojs/<YYYYMM>/<archive-id>.mp4`, plain in the page source. No player to defeat, no yt-dlp extractor needed; `curl` the page, regex `getConvertVideojs`, fetch the file. (I did not download anything.)
- **Licence, verbatim from the item pages:** 「您不需告知即可使用，但需註明出品者與典藏者」— *use without notification, but credit the producer and the holder* — 政府資料開放授權條款-第1版 (OGDL v1.0). The page prints the exact attribution string to copy: 出品者：台灣電影文化公司。<title>。典藏者：國家電影及視聽文化中心。政府資料開放授權條款-第1版。發佈於《國家影視聽中心-影片隨選視訊系統》[url]. **Tier A, 40 s long windows allowed.**
- **Open-web scope is stated on every page**: 新聞片與TFAI影音提供全網域觀看 — newsreels and TFAI AV play for the whole internet; everything else is library-only. Every candidate above is 新聞片 / 台影新聞.
- **The length trap, and it is the one real problem with this source.** 台影新聞 items are single newsreel stories: **most run 0:45–2:10**. A 61-second source cannot give six non-contiguous 12 s windows, let alone a long one. The cutter's options, in order: (a) take the six TFAI items here that run **3:20–9:37** — ids **4678** (9:37), **6798** (8:51), **115** (5:39), **6826** (5:05), **6967** (4:28), **7151** (4:08), **7095** (3:53), **978** (3:50), **4474** (3:47), **7170** (3:37), **4949** (3:21) — for full reels with long windows; (b) cut the one-minute jewels (1172, 3404, 1116, 3603) at **`--window-len 8` with 3–5 windows** and accept a short reel; or (c) ask the owner whether two or three sibling TFAI items may be concatenated into one reel — the archive numbers them in the same 台影新聞史料 volume, so they are literally consecutive reels of the same newsreel, which is a defensible single "source". **This is the one decision I would put to the owner.**
- **Terms that worked, in 正體字**: 電腦, 電子計算機, 原子, 太空, 太空人, 阿波羅, 科學, 展覽, 開播, 抽籤, 摸彩, 開獎, 自動化, 氣象, 通信, 發明, 博覽會, 天文. Terms that returned nothing useful: 愛國獎券 (0 hits), 未來 and 模型 (both pull feature films, all library-only), 電視台 (pulls drama).

### www.bilibili.com — Tier B, and the 科教片 archive lives in four UP主 spaces, not in the search results
- **Search API**: `https://api.bilibili.com/x/web-interface/search/type?search_type=video&keyword=…&page=N`. Needs `buvid3`/`buvid4` cookies (get them free from `https://api.bilibili.com/x/frontend/finger/spi`) plus UA, Referer, Origin, Accept-Language and the `Sec-Fetch-*` trio. Works fine; the results are dominated by 2020s content-farm recuts.
- **The real finding of this round: the sinosphere's science-film archive is in UP主 spaces, and the way in is `recArchivesByKeywords`.** The signed space API (`/x/space/wbi/arc/search`) returns **-352 风控校验失败** and `yt-dlp` on `space.bilibili.com/<mid>/video` returns **412**, even with wbi signing and the `dm_img_*` parameters. But the unsigned, undocumented **`https://api.bilibili.com/x/series/recArchivesByKeywords?mid=<mid>&keywords=<kw>&ps=30`** works with the same cookies and searches *inside one uploader's space*. It intermittently 412s — retry 3–4 times with a 3 s gap and it comes back. **This one endpoint is what found most of the Bilibili half of this queue.** Sweep an uploader with single-character keywords (電/机/科学/自动/宇宙) rather than phrases; it is a substring match on the title.
