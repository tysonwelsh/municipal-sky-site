# `south-asia` — video queue, reels round 4
Scout: Opus 5, 2026-09-14. Languages: Hindi, English (India).
Sites: https://ncaa.gov.in/repository/ (NCAA / DIGITĀLAYA, 21 989 records) and
https://cec.nic.in/cec/ + https://cec.swayam2.ac.in/ (CEC, Countrywide Classroom).

**Headline.** NCAA delivered exactly what the source scout promised and more: the
`Surabhi` collection deposited by Cinema Vision India is a shelf of **unedited
U-matic broadcast masters of a Doordarshan magazine programme, 1990–1994**, and
three of those reels are the theme itself — a 1991 feature on a *programmable
electronic percussion machine*, a 1991 feature on *computer calligraphy*, and a
1991 feature on *computers used to make paintings*. CEC turned out to be two
different things stacked: a modern explainer tier whose subjects are the theme
almost verbatim (the 1947 Harvard Mark II moth, Deep Blue vs Kasparov) and a
112-title self-hosted documentary tier that is almost entirely off theme.

## Site notes

### https://ncaa.gov.in/repository/ — NCAA / DIGITĀLAYA (C-DAC), 21 989 records
- **How search actually works.** The form is a **GET**, not a POST, and the action path carries the page number *and* the page size:
  `GET /repository/search/retriveBasicSearchResult/<page>/<perPage>?searchString=…&metadataSearchType=Metadata&_exactWordSearch=on&mediumName=&recentlyPublished=&resultPerPage=<perPage>`
  Posting to it returns a 302 to `/search/searchHome` and nothing else — that redirect is the trap that makes the site look broken. A session cookie (`NCAA_ID`) from one hit on `/repository/` plus a `Referer` header is enough; no login.
- **The search stems and does not rank by relevance.** "lottery"→pottery, "electronic"→election, "chance"→changing. Query narrow nouns. `exactWordSearch=true` exists as a checkbox if the fuzziness gets in the way. `metadataSearchType=Fulltext` is the other mode and was not needed.
- **Item pages carry their whole catalogue record in the HTML**, in a JS literal — `var metadata={"Administrative":{…},"Descriptive":{…},"Technical":{…}}` — with Source, Collection, Identifier, Title, Medium, ExtentOriginal, Spatial, Temporal, **Created (exact date)**, Rights, Subject, Keywords, Description, Artist, Language, Duration, Frame Rate and Dimension. Parse that literal; the visible page is empty because jQuery injects the table at DOM-ready.
- **The URL only needs the id.** `/repository/search/displaySearchRecordPreview/<ID>/x` resolves fine — the title segment is cosmetic. Handy, because the real titles contain quotes, commas and newlines.
- **How the media resolves (for the cutter).** The page builds
  `https://ncaa.gov.in/repository/stream/ncaaVideoStreamingSourceFile/<ID>/<fileName>/<ID>.mp4`
  which **302s to a tokenised HLS playlist**: `https://ncaa.gov.in/m3u8?rid=<ID>&cid=<token>&tid=<epoch-ms>&pid=/vol2/ncaa-archive-data/Portal_Record/<Dep>/<Carrier>/<ID>/<ID>/<ID>.m3u8`. Verified with `yt-dlp -s`: it resolves and would download, **but only with `--referer` and a browser `--user-agent`** — without them the path 403s. Multi-tape records expose several `previewFileNames`; iterate them.
- **Carrier is the id suffix**, and it is the fastest era filter: `FR` film reel (oldest, rarest — only one in this whole shortlist), `UM`/`UMLB`/`UMHB` U-matic, `BC` Betacam, `VHS`/`SVHS`, `MDV` MiniDV, `AC`/`SP`/`78` audio (skip for video rounds). The `mediumName` parameter takes the human names (`Film Reel`, `U-Matic`, `Betacam`, …) if a carrier-restricted search is wanted.
- **The depositor prefix tells you the register.** `CVI` Cinema Vision India (the **Surabhi** and **Bhoomi** Doordarshan series — the broadcast masters, and the richest seam for this brief), `NID` National Institute of Design (student and diploma films, animation, industrial film, PSA spots), `IGNCA` and `IGRMS` (seminars, lectures, ethnography), `JDCA`, `ICCR`, `SAK`, `CCRT`, `NSS`, `RS`, `SPK`, `KF`, `NAI` (National Archives film reels).
- **`audioVideoMarkingArray` is empty on every one of the 21 items I opened.** DIGITĀLAYA supports timecoded shot marks and publishes a `#markingDiv` for them, but no depositor has populated any. So **every NCAA row here is `timing: audition`** — there are no published timecodes to borrow.
- **The Surabhi tapes are unedited broadcast masters** (Medium: U-Matic, Temporal: "Feature Story For Television Broadcast", ExtentOriginal ≈ 21 min against a ~20-min slot). Worth auditioning their **heads**: colour bars, a slate, a countdown leader and a Doordarshan ident are all plausible there, and none of it is catalogued. That is the round's free theme-6 material if it exists.
- **Rate limits / etiquette.** `robots.txt` returns 403 (no policy served), so the plan's own etiquette applies. I ran ~25 searches and 24 item fetches single-threaded with ~1.5–2.5 s between them over roughly an hour and was never throttled, slowed or blocked. TTFB is 1–3 s. No login, no geo-block.
- **Licence, honestly.** The footer says "Copyright 2016 © All Rights Reserved" and every item carries a per-item `Rights` holder. The government depositors (IGNCA, IGRMS, NID, NAI) are Tier B argue-able A; **`CVI` is a private production house** and `JDCA` a private trust, so those are plainly Tier B — ≤ 6 windows, no whole scene, music windows ≤ 10 s. The T&C permit direct linking without permission and are silent on reuse.
- **What else is there beyond this round's theme.** Enormous depth in performance and ritual — Surabhi's ~5 500 feature-story reels, IGRMS ethnographic tape, Sangeet Natak Akademi, CCRT folk theatre, ICCR foreign-troupe tours (Polish jazz, Korean dance drama, Johnson Mountain Boys bluegrass in India). And the sound archive is the real prize: the `NID-T…-SP` spools include **raw location atmospheres** ("Sounds of Horns, Tea Service Boy, Hawkers, Tea Shop", "Sounds of Crowd, Water, Mela, Child Weeping", "Sounds of Damru & Flute, Share Market, Pinjara, Hammer") — a field-recording seam that would serve a later audio round directly.

### https://cec.nic.in/cec/ — Consortium for Educational Communication (with https://cec.swayam2.ac.in/ as the stub)
- **Go to `cec.nic.in/cec/`, not `cec.swayam2.ac.in`.** The swayam2 host in the register is a one-page brochure (see Passed over). The Drupal site on `cec.nic.in` is where the self-hosted video is.
- **Three content tiers, two of them real.**
  - `/cec/beyond_class` — **112 self-hosted documentaries**, EMMRC productions, 9–32 min each. The list is server-rendered with the title, the duration and a **full paragraph description** in hidden inputs (`<input id="<n>t">` title, `<n>v` description, `<n>p` path), so the whole catalogue parses out of one page load, no JS. Paths are relative: prefix `https://cec.nic.in/cec/sites/default/beyond` to the `/wpresources/<name>.mp4` value. Filenames contain spaces — URL-encode them.
  - `/cec/conceptclass` — 16 short explainers at `https://cec.nic.in/cec/sites/default/concept/slo/[<Subject>/]<name>.mp4`, each with a long prose description in the page. The subject `<select>` lists ~55 subjects (Computer Science, Physics, Statistics, Quantum Mechanics, Multimedia, Forensic Science…), but **posting the filter returns the same 16 items** — either the back end is dead or results render somewhere the fetcher doesn't see. Assume 16 is all that is public.
  - `/cec/curriculum_class` — demo only, five sample subjects (see Passed over).
- **The mp4s are plain, public and un-tokenised.** `HEAD` returns `206` / `video/mp4` with a `Content-Length` and range support; no cookie, no referer, no session. The easiest media to cut in the whole beat — but note `yt-dlp -j` returns null duration/dimensions on them (no container probe over a bare range request), so **size is the only duration proxy without downloading**: the concept files run 55–138 MB and look like 3–8 minutes; the Beyond Classroom durations are printed on the listing page and are reliable.
- **Era.** Concept Class is recent (the Deep Blue script mentions 1997 in the past tense and reads as 2024–25 production); Beyond Classroom is undated but the tape looks 1990s–2000s EMMRC. **None of it is the 1984 Countrywide Classroom U-matic.** The source scout's hope that CEC would yield "a 1986 lecturer with a chalkboard and a Doordarshan slate" is not met by CEC's own public hosts — that material exists (CEC claims 40 000 video programmes / 24 000 e-content modules) but is not served from anything I could reach. **Do not send another scout here expecting 1980s tape.**
- **One live thing worth recording.** `/cec/vyas` embeds the **Vyas higher-education channel's live stream**, `https://playhls.media.nic.in/hls/live/vyas/vyas.m3u8` via `playhls.media.nic.in/webcast/video.html`. That is a live Indian educational-TV channel on an open NIC HLS endpoint — off-brief for an archive round (nothing to cut from a live feed), but if a future round ever wants *a station receiving a station*, this is a real broadcast signal with no login in front of it.
- **Rate limits.** `nic.in` was slower than NCAA (3–8 s TTFB, one page took ~20 s) but never refused. Single connection, pause between requests, as the register warns. No robots.txt is served on the swayam2 host; `cec.nic.in` did not refuse anything I asked for.
- **Licence.** Government of India / UGC inter-university centre content on a `nic.in` host, no licence declared anywhere on the portal and no per-item rights statement. **Tier B, argue-able A.**
- **What else is there beyond this round's theme.** Beyond Classroom is a serious ethnographic and craft library — Koodiyattam in four parts, the Rhythms of Kerala percussion series (Maddalam, Mizhavu, Idakka, Pulluvakudam), six films on Majuli island, Manipuri crafts and foodways, Kerala puppetry in three forms. For a round about ritual, craft or percussion rather than machines, this site is a first stop, and every file is a public mp4.
