**Read this before cutting.** Two findings change how this beat is used:

1. **filmisadocument.jp has no audio at all.** `yt-dlp -F` on its HLS returns
   four *video-only* renditions (192×144 / 320×240 / 480×360 / 640×480) — every
   film in the portal is a silent 1920s–40s transfer and the NFAJ streams no
   audio track, not even a dead one. Its seven candidates below are therefore
   **picture beds, not receptions**: superb pictures, but each needs a paired
   audio decision from the owner (a `--picture`-style inversion, or a silent
   reel). `--propose` will fail its loudness gate on all of them. Ranked below
   the sound sources for that reason, not for quality. See §Decision.
2. **대한뉴스 is Tier B as found, not Tier A.** The source register assumed a
   공공누리 posture. The item pages carry **no 공공누리 badge**; `/etc/copyright`
   says KOGL applies only where the mark is attached and that everything else
   needs prior arrangement through KTV 나누리. (The copyright *page* is KOGL-1;
   the films are not.) Tiered B accordingly — ≤ 6 windows, ≤ 30 s long window.

## Site notes

### filmisadocument.jp — NFAJ 「フィルムは記録する」
- **How search works.** There is no free-text search. `/films` is the whole catalogue — **339 films in exactly 4 pages** (`?page=1..4`), with `&genre=1..15` and `&area=<pref>` filters and a `sort=` (`date_asc`, `time_desc`, …). The 4-page crawl is the entire holding; do that once and search the HTML locally rather than hitting the site repeatedly. Each card carries id, 作品番号, year, region, minutes, sound/colour, producer, genre labels and a truncated synopsis. Detail is `/films/view/<id>` (**not** the ST number — `ST000015` is at `/films/view/15`). `/list/` 404s; the register's "rep: /list/" is stale.
- **Era.** 1904–1944 only (1920s 174, 1930s 134, 1940s 14). There is **no post-war material at all**, so themes 1, 2 and 5 are out of reach here; what it can serve is theme 3 and 4.
- **How the media resolves.** The detail page carries `<iframe src="https://h10.cs.nii.ac.jp/view4/video_view.php?fileName=<32-hex>">`. That page hard-codes HLS masters: `https://h10.cs.nii.ac.jp/stream/nfc/<fileName>/hls/{auto,144,240,360}/master.m3u8`. `yt-dlp -F` on the `auto` master lists **192x144 / 320x240 / 480x360 / 640x480, all `video only`** — and 192×144 is literally the reel's own resolution, so the cutter can pull the exact rendition with no downscale. The `fileName` hashes for the queued films: #9 `stca01e7e61441cabfa47499ce97700def`, #21 `stcd0b914553331fdcf139b379fb7d2ff7`, #28 `st693f94cd4aaf5d93d6db6bf35ad1ccfa`, #29 `st1b012b3bd6351c5a8b79fd7e5cb763c7`, #32 `st167ac570bb1d07b546db73212728f6a4`, #33 `st21191aeda3b5761ee3cb357679503bc8`.
- **No audio.** See header note 1. Every film is 「サウンド: サイレント」 and the stream carries no audio track whatsoever.
- **Rate limits / robots.** No robots.txt block observed (the site returns its own 200 HTML for `/robots.txt`). It is a small museum portal — I used ~20 requests at 3 s apart with no trouble.
- **Also there.** 満洲 three-part series (ST000266/333/334), the Zeppelin LZ-127's 1929 Tokyo visit in three separate films (184/196/203), 防空/帝都の防空 air-defence drills (322/225), 日露戦争 films from 1904–05, and a large folklore and mountaineering tier. 「フィルム映写速度」 is given per film (12/16/18/20/24 fps) — worth reading before cutting, since the transfers are at the stated speed.

### www.kagakueizo.org — NPO 科学映像館
- **How search works.** WordPress. `https://www.kagakueizo.org/?s=<term>` works and is not robots-blocked (robots only disallows `/wordpress/wp-admin/` and `/wp-includes/`). Results are thin — 1 to 7 films per term — so use many narrow terms rather than one broad one. **The terms that produced this queue:** `電子計算機`, `コンピュータ`, `サイバネティックス`, `通信`, `テレビジョン`, `衛星`, `原子力`, `エレクトロニクス`, `自動化`, `宇宙`. Terms that returned nothing useful: `ロボット`, `トランジスタ`, `万国博`, `電子音`, `パルス`. **Better than search: the sidebars.** Every detail page lists ten more films by the same 製作会社 and by genre, and that is how 音響創造 (#5) surfaced — it appears in no search result I ran. Browse `東京シネマ`'s sidebar in particular; it is the best studio on the site.
- **How the media resolves — the cutter needs this.** The page contains `<a id="movie" name="0687/<slug>.mp4">` and nothing else; `/html5video2.js` reads that `name` and injects `<iframe src="https://150.videoplayer.jp/player.asp?client_code=admin&video_file=<name>&serial=12345678&volume_level=100&control_hidden=on">`. **A plain curl to that player.asp returns HTTP 500** (both raw and %2F-encoded), so open it in a real browser and take the mp4 from the network tab. The `video_file` paths for the queued films: #2 `0687/denshikeisanki.mp4`, #3 `0687/cybernetics.mp4`, #5 `0687/onkyousouzou.mp4`, #12 `0687/eiseitsuushin.mp4`, #15 `0687/television.mp4`, #17 `0687/denshi-no-gijutsu.mp4`, #20 `0687/taiyou-to-denpa.mp4`, #24 `0687/genshiryokuhatsuden-no-yoake.mp4`, #30 `0687/taiheiyou-cable.mp4`.
- **Rate limits.** One server, one NPO. I used 5-second pauses throughout and ~25 requests total; nothing rate-limited but treat that as the ceiling.
- **Licence.** No terms page exists. The site's whole stated purpose is 無料配信 of rescued sponsored film, and detail pages sometimes name a rights manager (#5 names 株式会社東京シネマ新社 and says the print is held by the National Museum of Modern Art's film centre). Tier B until cleared per film.
- **Also there.** 1,060+ films across 15 genres and a new one every Thursday. Beyond this round: a very deep coal-mining tier (三池/三井山野), 精密機械工業 technical series, medical microcinematography by 小林米作 (the 東京シネマ cameraman who shot half this queue), and 職務における人間研究 — a series of industrial-psychology films about individual workers that is unlike anything else found in this beat.

### www.ehistory.go.kr — e영상역사관 (KTV / 한국정책방송원)
- **robots.txt disallows `/search`.** Do not use the site-wide search. The 대한뉴스 board's own filter form is a different path and is allowed: POST to `/koreanews/korea_news` with `_csrf` (scraped from a prior GET of the same page, cookie jar required), `search_type=all|subject|cont|news_number`, `search_text`, and optional `search_start_date` / `search_end_date` (decade granularity: 1950…1990). 12 results a page, newest first, total count printed as 총 게시물.
- **The catalogue is item-level, which is the reason to use this site.** 대한뉴스 issues 1–2040 are split into **12,804 individual clips** — each with its own title, duration and page. So a candidate here is *already* a 20-second-to-5-minute single subject; there is no scrubbing through a ten-minute newsreel to find the computer. The view page lists the issue's sibling clips ("클립별 영상 (8)") if you want the rest of the broadcast.

That player is WeNMediaPlayer over **HLS** with the `id` uuid as the file id; the manifest URL is built in JS, so take it from the network tab. ### www.nicovideo.jp
- **robots.txt is kinder than expected.** `Disallow: /search/` applies **only to Googlebot**; for `User-agent: *` just `/api/`, `/my/`, `/related_tag/`, `/recommendations/` and friends. So `/search/<query>` and `/tag/<tag>` are both fetchable. (`GPTBot` is disallowed entirely — I used a Safari UA, which is what the register's "browser-shaped fetch" means.)
- **How to enumerate without the API.** The pages are client-rendered React Router, but the server streams the payload into the HTML as HTML-escaped JSON. Unescape `&quot;` and match `"type":"essential","id":"<sm…>","contentType":…,"title":…,"registeredAt":…` plus the later `"duration":<n>` — that yields 32 results per page with id, title, duration, date and view count, no API call and no login. `?sort=v&order=d` sorts by views, `?sort=f&order=a` by upload date ascending. A parser is at `scratchpad/nicoparse.py` in this scout's session dir if it survives; it is 15 lines.
- **The register's warning is confirmed and bounded.** `yt-dlp`'s **tag/search playlist** extractor returns 0 unauthenticated — but **watch pages work fine**: `yt-dlp -s` on sm17965313 and sm10127138 both resolved `video-h264-360p+audio-aac-64kbps` with no account. So enumerate in HTML (above), then hand watch URLs to the cutter one at a time. 360p/64 kbps is well above 192×144 mono.
- **Search-term notes.** Modern noise dominates by view count on every general term — sort by date ascending or add a period word. `電子立国` is the single highest-value query in this beat: the entire NHK series 「電子立国 日本の自叙伝」 (1991) is up in two-part uploads — 第01回 新・石器時代 (sm17952365/sm17954671), 第02回 トランジスタの誕生 (sm17965313/sm17967188), 第03回 石になった電気回路 (sm17976757/sm17976841), 第04回 電卓戦争 (sm17985874/sm17986742), 第05回 8ミリ角のコンピューター (sm17989448/sm17996182) — plus 新・電子立国's video-game episodes and its finale 「コンピューター地球網」 in three parts (sm3036363/sm3034484/sm3036526). That is roughly seven hours of Japanese broadcast television about computers. `NHK電子音楽スタジオ` returns only 4 items but one of them (`日本の初期の電子音楽集`) is the theme-1 anchor. `大阪万博` and `電子計算機` are dead — see the passed-over table for why.
- **Rate limits.** dwango rate-limits hard on bulk. I kept to ~10 page fetches at 4–6 s and 2 yt-dlp probes at 6 s with no throttling.
- **Licence.** Every item is a user upload of someone else's broadcast or record. Tier B without exception; the NHK documentaries especially — 6 windows, no continuous scene, and the two music items at ≤ 10 s per window.

## Decision for the owner (raised as a flag)

**Does the round accept picture-only sources?** filmisadocument.jp — the highest-yield Japanese site in the beat, and the one the source scout ranked #1 — streams **no audio track at all**, and the same is true of the NARA items on archive.much.go.kr (#14 AFVN, and most of that site's American footage). Seven queued candidates (#9, #14, #21, #28, #29, #32, #33) are affected. Three ways out, and it is not the scout's call:
- cut them anyway as silent reels, and let the receiver hand them the noise floor;
- invert `--audio-only`: keep the picture, generate or pair a sound (a tone, a carrier, another reel's audio bed);
- drop picture-only sources from the round, which costs this beat its two pre-war sites and all its Tier A.

Everything else in the queue has sound and is unaffected; the Top 5 deliberately contains no silent item, so cutting can start regardless of how this is decided.
