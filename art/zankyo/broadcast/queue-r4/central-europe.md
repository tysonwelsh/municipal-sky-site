## Top 5 — cut these first

1. **https://repozytorium.fn.org.pl/?q=pl/node/11352** — *Rachuj kolego…* (1964): 105 seconds that walk from counting on stones to the Ural 2 mainframe, with a rocket launch in the middle. One item covers the whole of priority 2 and the archive gives you every cut point.

## Site notes

### repozytorium.fn.org.pl — FINA (Filmoteka Narodowa – Instytut Audiowizualny), Warsaw
**The best-documented source I have seen in this project, and the cutter should exploit it.**

- **Search is a GET URL.** The front page's search box POSTs, but it redirects to
  `https://repozytorium.fn.org.pl/?q=pl/search/site/<term>` — fetch that directly, no cookies,
  no form token. Results are `/?q=pl/node/<id>` links with the title, the PKF issue number and
  the release date in the anchor text. An autocomplete endpoint,
  `/?q=pl/fnsearch/autocomplete/autocomplete/all/<prefix>`, returns the phrases the index
  actually holds and is the fastest way to find a title you cannot guess — it is how
  *Komputer prawdę ci powie* and *Elektroniczna perspektywa XXI wieku* surfaced.
- **Every PKF item page carries a frame-accurate shot list** under *Opis sekwencji*, in
  `HH:MM:SS:FF` (25 fps). Every `moments` column above is transcribed from it, not guessed —
  that is why almost nothing here says `timing: audition`. Pages also carry the duration
  (*Czas trwania*), the rights holder (*Prawa*), the sound format, the frame ratio, the colour
  system, the shooting date, named people with one-line biographies, and — for most items —
  **the full narrator's script** under *tekst lektorski*. Read that before choosing windows:
  it tells you what the voice is saying over each shot.
- **The media resolves to a plain file.** Each item page contains one
  `sites/default/files/video/PL<uuid>/<name>.mp4`; prefix it with
  `https://repozytorium.fn.org.pl/`. All four I probed return **HTTP 200 with Range support**.
  **Trap:** the server sends `Content-Type: text/plain` for these `.mp4` files, which is why
  `yt-dlp` on the *page* says "Unsupported URL" and why a naive fetcher may refuse them.
  Give ffmpeg/`make-reel.sh` the direct file URL, not the page URL. Sizes are large — the
  9:47 Antonisz reel is 104 MB, a one-minute newsreel item ~8–10 MB.
- **Some items are fragments.** *Fobia* shows `całość: 10:34, fragment: 01:04` — only the
  1:04 is served. The Antonisz newsreels show only `fragment:` but at 9:47–10:19, i.e. very
  nearly the whole thing. Check that line before planning a long window.
- **One item had no mp4 at all** (node 7301, above). Worth a browser check; the rest of the
  sample was 100 % consistent.
- **Licence, as found.** Item pages state a rights holder — `WFDiF` (Wytwórnia Filmów
  Dokumentalnych i Fabularnych) for the PKF newsreels, `Filmoteka Narodowa` for the animation
  and experimental shelf. There is **no CC mark and no PD statement anywhere**. The Regulamin
  (`/?q=pl/node/9`) says browsing needs no registration and then: *"Prezentowane w serwisie
  … materiały w każdej sytuacji podlegają ochronie prawa autorskiego. Wszelkie kopiowanie
  materiałów jest zabronione."* The Udostępnianie page (`/?q=pl/node/8`) routes all licensing
  to `dokument@fina.gov.pl` for the PKF holdings and notes in capitals that the **online
  commercial model has been closed** — ordering fragments is by phone, mail or in person only.
  Tier B, and the owner should read that sentence himself.
- **Rate and etiquette.** Drupal, no robots hostility met; I ran roughly 45 requests at
  1.5 s apart with no throttling, no captcha and no 429. Do not enumerate node ids.
- **What else is there, beyond this round's theme.** 5 960 film items. The whole Polska
  Kronika Filmowa run 1944–94 plus PAT newsreels from the 1930s; a documentary shelf; an
  **animation and experimental shelf** (`/?q=pl/search/animation`) which is where Antonisz,
  Szczechura, Kondratiuk and the Se-Ma-For studio live; and features. For a future round, the
  PKF's foreign-exchange items (`Wymiana zagraniczna`) are a shortcut to newsreel footage
  from the USSR, the GDR, the FRG and the USA re-narrated in Polish — a second voice over a
  picture the pool may already have.
- **Terms that worked, in Polish:** `komputer` (10 hits), `elektronika` (12), `robot`,
  `losowanie`, `totolotek`, `cybernetyka` (1, and it is the good one), `syntezator` (1, and
  it is the good one), `atom`, `reaktor`, `kosmos`, `telewizja`, `maszyna licząca`,
  `Antonisz`, `Rudnik`, `Bohdan Mazurek`, `eksperymentalny`. **Terms that failed:**
  `ELWRO`, `informatyka`, `mikrokomputer`, `muzyka elektroniczna`, `muzyka konkretna`,
  `maszyna cyfrowa`, `plotter`, `Patkowski`, `Kotoński` (the composer is credited but not
  indexed under his own name — `syntezator` and `Antonisz` are the doors instead).
  `Odra` returns only the river. Search the **subject**, not the machine's name.

### oldradio.cz — Virtuální museum československé historické radiotechniky (Martin Hájek, 1998–2025)
- **No search, no CMS, no player.** Hand-written HTML in `windows-1250`; pipe every fetch
  through `iconv -f windows-1250 -t utf-8` or the Czech diacritics come back as mojibake.
  The site's entire video holding is the ten `.mpg` files linked from `seznamtv.htm` under
  the heading *"Různé znělky z Čs. televize 80. let / Some CSSR TV jingles"* — nothing else
  on the site links a video, and I checked the index, the two monoscope pages, the CRT
  regeneration page and the Tesla factory page.
- **Media resolves with no extractor.** `https://www.oldradio.cz/cst/<name>.mpg`, HTTP 200,
  `Content-Type: video/mpeg`, Last-Modified 2025‑08‑25 for all ten. **Durations are not
  published anywhere and cannot be read without fetching the file** — `yt-dlp -j` on a direct
  MPEG‑1 file returns nulls for duration, codec and dimensions, so every Czech row above says
  `timing: audition`. Byte sizes (50 KB–738 KB) suggest these are seconds, not minutes; the
  cutter will know the moment the source lands in `local-dev/broadcast-src/`.
- **Licence.** There is no terms page, no robots.txt hostility, no copyright line beyond
  `© Martin Hájek, 1998 – 2025` on the index — which covers the site, not ČST's broadcast
  output. The material is Czechoslovak state television recorded off air by a collector.
  Tier B, and the register's "contact-the-owner case" reading stands: there is a `Kontakt`
  link on the index if the owner ever wants to ask.
- **Pool overlap to check by ear.** The pool's `cst-vysilani-znelky` is a YouTube-sourced
  off-air ČST tape whose manifest notes describe the TV2 test card, the ČS monogram
  assembling itself, the announcer Miloš Frýba and a Zprávy TN title. `zahajeni.mpg` and
  `tvnoviny.mpg` are the same *station* and probably the same *era*, from a different tape.
  Audition them against the existing reel before cutting; `pocasi.mpg`, `naddopis.mpg` and
  the Mr Egg adverts have no counterpart in the pool at all.
- **What else is there.** Not video, but worth knowing: an extensive photographic record of
  Czechoslovak receiver manufacture (`vyrobatv/vyroba.htm` — the conveyor lines, the burn-in
  tunnel, the shaking rig, the factory signal exchange, 1958/59), a catalogue of ~150 Tesla
  television models 1953–90 with schematics, the Soviet sets imported in the 1950s–60s, and
  — as the source scout noted — a large radio half that would serve an audio tier.
- **The 1970s electronic monoscope** described on `monoskop70.htm` (recorded off the
  generator in the Tesla museum at Třešť, "nearly two hours", with the circle deforming near
  the end as the generator fails) is exactly what the station wants and is **not downloadable**
  — the page says it is available as DVD files to burn. That is a write-to-the-owner item.

### rtsplaneta.rs — BLOCKED. Widevine/FairPlay DRM on every asset
This site cannot serve this round, and the register row should be corrected. What I found:

- The `.mp4` strings *are* in the server HTML, as the source scout reported — but they are
  DASH manifest URLs under a path literally named **`cenc_vod_dash`** (`cenc` = Common
  Encryption), and each one is accompanied by
  `"drm":{"fairPlayCertURL":"…/drm/….cer","fairPlayURL":"https://rtsplaneta.rs/drm.php/fairplay?token=","isProtected":true,"widevineURL":"https://rtsplaneta.rs/drm.php/widevine?token="}`.
- **25 of 25** video items on the Programski arhiv category page carry `isProtected: true`;
  **zero** carry `false`. The same holds on the item page for *Novogodišnji kolaž 1970*
  (8 of 8). There are **no HLS/`.m3u8` variants** anywhere — 25 `manifest.mpd`, 0 `m3u8`.
  This is site-wide, not per-title.
- The brief's rule is "no logins, no paywalls, **no DRM**", so nothing here was queued as a
  candidate. The passed-over table lists the archive titles I could read so the owner has a
  map if he ever writes to RTS or relaxes the rule.
- **Two further walls behind the first.** The site's search is client-side only — the server
  returns byte-identical markup for `?q=kompjuter`, `?q=racunar` and `?q=Radovanovic`, so the
  Serbian theme terms (`računar`, `kompjuter`, `kibernetika`, `Elektronski studio Radio
  Beograda`, `Vladan Radovanović`, `budućnost`, `loto`, `izvlačenje`) could not be run at all
  without driving a browser. And at least one category returns
  *"Pristup omogućen samo registrovanim korisnicima koji poseduju korisnički nalog"* —
  access for registered account holders only.
- **The companion door is empty too.** `www.rts.rs`'s *Trezor* strand page, the register's
  "map of what RTS has already cleared", fetches clean and contains no media element of any
  kind — it indexes programmes whose video lives on RTS Planeta, behind the DRM. rts.rs's own
  search is likewise client-side.
- **Consequence for the beat:** Serbian contributed no candidates this round. The theme leads
  the brief named for it — the Elektronski studio Radio Beograda (1972–), Vladan Radovanović's
  *Elektra*, the Yugoslav loto draws — were not testable, not disproved. If the owner wants
  Serbian material, the two live options are (a) write to RTS, or (b) relax the native-host
  rule for Jugoslovenska kinoteka and RTSH, which the source scout rejected precisely because
  they publish through YouTube.
