# `latam` — video queue, reels round 4
Scout: Claude Opus 5, 2026-09-14. Sites: https://www.senalmemoria.co/ (the beat's
only Pile A site). Languages: Spanish (Colombia).

**Read the site note first.** Every *video* pieza on Señal Memoria plays through an
`<iframe>` pointing at **RTVC's own "Señal Memoria" YouTube channel** — not at a file
on senalmemoria.co. Two exceptions found in 407 item pages checked (§ Site notes).
The senalmemoria.co page is still the thing worth scouting: it carries the catalogue
code, the carrier (U-Matic / Betacam / 1-inch / 35 mm), the production date and, on the
1950s newsreels, a **numbered shot list** that YouTube does not have. Candidates below
are the *pages*; the media resolution route is stated per row. Whether the cutter may
pull media that resolves through the broadcaster's own YouTube channel is a call for
the owner — flagged in STATUS.md, not decided here.

## Site notes

**https://www.senalmemoria.co/ — RTVC's audiovisual archive (Colombia)**

- **The finding that governs everything: the video layer is YouTube; the native layer is radio.** Of **407 item pages fetched and parsed**: 319 embed YouTube, 80 serve a file from `s3.amazonaws.com/rtvc-assets-senalmemoria.gov.co/`, and 8 did not resolve to any media in the HTML. **78 of the 80 native files are `.mp3`** (the *Memoria Crónica* radio series and a handful of radio piezas). Exactly **two** native `.mp4` files turned up in the whole corpus: `2022-01/omarrayo.mp4` (row 2) and `2022-11/inravision1.mp4` (8 s). Everything else is an `<iframe>` to `youtube.com/embed/…` or `youtube-nocookie.com/embed/…`. The channel is **RTVC's own** (`youtube.com/channel/UCNqIj6lfoAz8mH536f6gBrw`, uploader string "Señal Memoria") — the broadcaster distributing its own archive, not a collector re-upload — which is why this site is not in the register's "mirrors YouTube" rejection list, and why the owner has to rule on it rather than the scout. A third pattern exists on the newest piezas (2024+): a native `.mp4` with the source timecode in the filename, e.g. `2024-08/7. BTCX60 050724 - 0.16.41 - 0.18.51 (1).mp4`. That naming means RTVC cuts these from the master with in/out points — if the site keeps publishing that way, this becomes a native-video source over time.
- **Why the page still beats the YouTube URL.** Only the senalmemoria.co page carries the **código de catálogo** (`C1P-242815`, `UMT-207265`, `BTCX30 014581`, `VR F35mm-851301`), the **soporte** (1-inch tape / U-Matic / Betacam / 35 mm / nativo digital), the **fecha de producción** — often the *broadcast* date, decades before the YouTube upload — and, on the newsreels, the numbered shot list. Cite the page; resolve the media however the owner rules.
- **How its search works.** Do not use it: `/search/resultados?keys=…` is **`Disallow: /search/` in robots.txt**, so this scout never touched it. Everything above came from listing views that robots permits:
  - `/categoria-piezas/<term>?page=N` — server-rendered, 9 items a page. Terms: `retrovisor` (29 pages, 257 piezas), `culturama`, `memoria-cronica`, `especial-balcanes` (119 more). This is the best structured entry point on the site.
  - `/?_wrapper_format=html&page=N` — the **full piezas feed**, newest first, ~12 a page, pager runs to **page 529** (≈6 000 piezas). Pages 0–59 were taken (483 slugs); the rest is untouched and is where a second pass should go.
  - `/contenido?page=N` — articles only, pager to 335. `/colecciones/<slug>` — article collections; *Historia de los medios* is the one to read for context.
  - `/colombia-al-dia` — 12 published DINAPE editions, four listing pages, no descriptions (see passed-over table).
  - `sitemap.xml` exists but contains exactly one URL (the home page). `/jsonapi` and `?_format=json` are off. Drupal 10, Bootstrap Barrio theme.
- **The terms that found things.** `Jacqueline Nova` (via a site-restricted web search — her pieza is not in any browsable category listing). In the slug corpus: `computa`, `electron`, `ciber`, `ciencia`, `futuro`, `tecnolog`, `luna`, `espacio`, `internet`, `milenio`, `antena`, `unidad-movil`, `inravision`, `noticiero-cinematografico`. `noticiero cinematográfico` is the single highest-yield string on the site, exactly as the source scout predicted for the region. `carta de ajuste`, `cierre de emisión`, `sorteo`, `lotería`, `baloto`, `chance`, `cerebro electrónico` and `año 2000` returned **nothing** — see dead ends.
- **Rate limits and etiquette.** No robots obstruction outside `/search/`; no Cloudflare; no geo-block on `www`. It answers a plain desktop UA. It is slow (1–3 s a page) but never rate-limited across roughly 570 requests spaced 0.9–1.5 s apart. One request at a time is enough.
- **Licence.** Every page footer reads `© 2026 Señal Memoria · RTVC Sistema de Medios Públicos · MinTIC · Gobierno de Colombia`; terms and privacy live at `inravision.gov.co`. Nothing on the site asserts PD or CC. **All rows above are Tier B.** The one Tier-A argument worth knowing: the *Colombia al Día* newsreels (rows 11, 15, 16, 17) are **DINAPE** — 1954–58 Colombian state production. Colombia's term for a work authored by a legal person runs 70 years from publication (Ley 23 de 1982 as amended by Ley 1915 de 2018), so a 1957 newsreel clears in 2028, not now. Cut them Tier B; revisit in two years. High-resolution licensed copies are obtainable through "Solicitar material" / the Licenciamiento service (`senalmemoria@rtvc.gov.co`, Carrera 45 #26-33, Bogotá, +57 601 220 0700) — the right door if the owner ever wants a Tier-A path into this archive.
- **What else is here, beyond this round's theme.** (a) **Tandas**: ~16 individually catalogued television commercials, 1950s–80s — Saltinas La Rosa (1950s), Chocolate Sol, Icollantas and Peter Pan (1960s), Pintuco (1962–65), Chispitas Mariposa, Ositos Habladores, Din Don Corazón, Granahorrar, SENA, Federación de Cafeteros, and a Don Chinche commercial about the papal visit. A full ephemera round could live on this list alone. (b) **The radio layer**: 77 *Memoria Crónica* episodes plus HJCK and Radiodifusora Nacional material, all native `.mp3`, all reachable without touching YouTube — the obvious first stop when the audio pass comes. (c) **Indigenous and regional**: *Pueblo arhuaco en el archivo Señal Memoria (1958)*, *Los tejidos de las mujeres paeces*, *Pareja misak*. (d) **Six thousand piezas** past feed page 59 that nobody has looked at.
- **Dead ends on this site.** No test card, no sign-off, no clock, no station ident as a catalogued item — the archive keeps *programmes*, not continuity, and the pool's existing `inravision-mensajes-1987` appears to have come from somewhere else, because nothing like it is published here. No lottery or prize-draw footage at all (`sorteo`, `lotería`, `baloto`, `chance` are absent from the published corpus); the only trace of televised chance on the whole site is the sentence naming *Tele hipódromo* inside the Producciones Punch pieza. No computer-room, mainframe or punched-card footage: the country's technology story as this archive tells it is **broadcast** technology — transmitters, aerials, outside-broadcast vans, control rooms — and that is where rows 3, 6, 9, 10, 18, 19, 20 and 30 come from.
