## Site notes

### filmmirasim.ktb.gov.tr (Turkish, Pile A #1) — **the good site in this beat**
- **Search**: `https://filmmirasim.ktb.gov.tr/tr/search/0/0/<keyword>` (URL-encoded; the
  homepage JS builds exactly this). Plain GET, no session, no VIEWSTATE needed.
  Substring match over title **and** the catalogue description — which is why `atom`
  finds the civil-defence films and `fen` finds Fenerbahçe. **Capped at 20 results**,
  no pagination visible, so narrow terms beat broad ones.
- **Item page**: `/tr/film/<slug>` carries everything the cutter needs — SGM number,
  `Sesli`/`Sessiz` (**with sound / silent** — check this, a third of the archive is
  silent), `Süre :HH:MM:SS`, place, restoration status, year, title and a one-line
  description. **No shot list and no timecodes anywhere**, so every window is an audition.
- **Media**: three plain HLS manifests are in the page HTML at
  `https://filmmirasim.ktb.gov.tr:8443/WEBTV/_definst_/mp4:Publish/<dir>/<file>-{240,360,480}p.mp4/playlist.m3u8`.
  I fetched two 480p manifests as text: both **HTTP 200** with a real `#EXT-X-STREAM-INF`
  and a `chunklist_*.m3u8`. `yt-dlp` generic says "Unsupported URL" from the page URL —
