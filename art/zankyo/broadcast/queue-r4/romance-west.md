## Site notes

### patrimonio.archivioluce.com (Istituto Luce, ~70 000 films)
- **Search works with a plain GET**: `/luce-web/search/result.html?query=<terms>&startPage=<n>`, 20 results a page. It behaves as a phrase/AND match: `cervello elettronico` → 13, `cibernetica` → 5, but `Luciano Berio` → 0 and `fonologia` → 0. **Search single words and two-word phrases; personal names mostly fail.** `Italia 61` matched WWI footage (it read "61" loosely), so quote-like precision is not available.
- Terms that paid: `calcolatore`, `cervello elettronico`, `cibernetica`, `musica elettronica`, `robot`, `automazione`, `lotteria`, `Totocalcio`, `reattore nucleare`, `Ispra`. Terms that failed: `fonologia`, composer names, `Studio di Fonologia`, `Elea`. **Luce has no Studio di Fonologia material findable by name** — the RAI research-centre item (#16) is the closest the archive gets to the Milan electronic studio, and I could not confirm any Berio/Maderna/Nono footage exists here.
- **Detail pages are the prize**: date, duration, b/n or colour, sonoro/muto, production house, and an ordered **`sequenze` shot list**, plus `keywords / temi / persone / luoghi`. No timecodes, but for a 40–100 s item the ordered shot list is effectively a timeline — the estimates in the queue above are (shot index ÷ shot count) × duration and should land within a few seconds.
- Detail pages also list "gli altri N servizi del Cinegiornale" — the other items in the same newsreel, with their own durations. Useful for building a whole-newsreel reel.
- **Media**: `yt-dlp -s` → generic extractor → m3u8, confirmed live on `IL5000085533` (format 983). Nothing special needed.
- **Etiquette**: the subdomain 301s its own robots.txt. I used a 2 s gap and a browser UA; no throttling, no errors across ~40 requests.
- **Beyond this round**: the archive holds the entire *Giornale Luce* (1927–1945) and *Settimana Incom*, so it is the natural home for fascist-era titles, fanfares and announcer voice; the `Panorama Cinematografico` and `Radar` series carry 1960s–70s magazine items. Note the pool already holds `it-giornale-luce-340-1943` — don't re-cut that specific number.

### memoire.ciclic.fr (Ciclic Mémoire, 14 986 amateur films)
- **Search**: the form posts to `/films-d-archives?barre_recherche=<terms>`, plain GET. Result count appears as a bare number near the top. It searches title, description **and the shot description**, which is why `science` returned 42 and `an 2000` returned 31 (mostly false positives on "2000" in other senses — `Reggiane 2000` aircraft, "l'an 2000" in a title).
- Terms that paid: `électronique`, `informatique`, `ordinateur`, `science`. **The find of the site is a cluster of at least six films of the Nançay radio-astronomy station** (`radioastronomie`, `Nançay`) — construction 1952, the 169 MHz "cigar" antennas, the decametric array, a helicopter lobe survey, station extensions — deposited by Jean-Louis Steinberg and by the station's own engineer Christian Couteret, and only put online in **October 2025**. Search `Nançay` and `radioastronomie` directly for the rest.
- **Detail pages** give year, director, place, sound (`Sonore` / **`Muet`**), colour, gauge (16 mm / super 8 / Umatic) and duration, plus a shot-by-shot description that on the Couteret films runs to several hundred words. **Check `Muet` before queuing** — most of the Nançay material is silent, so it is picture-only for a reel.
- **Timecodes exist in two places**: the user *Commentaires* (people annotate "11:10 à 11:16 ce bâtiment…") and a *Captures* tab. The comments are the only real timecodes anywhere in this beat — use them.
- **Media**: the public page URL is not yt-dlp-readable; the embed is. Use `https://memoire.ciclic.fr/embed/<id>` where `<id>` is on the detail page (e.g. 11735 → embed 20377; 4077 → embed `9410_2` — note the `_N` suffix on multi-sequence deposits). HLS + DASH.
- **Etiquette**: `robots.txt` sets `Crawl-delay: 10`. I honoured it; every request was 200 and the site never slowed. Budget ten seconds a page.
- **Beyond this round**: a province's private 8/9.5/16 mm — village fêtes, agriculture, the 1950s road, workshops. The `Trajectoire` series (Union des entreprises de l'Indre, 1988) is a run of regional industrial TV magazines on Umatic, several of them marked "film très abîmé", which is free degradation.

### cinememoire.net (Cinémémoire, Marseille + the former French colonies)
- **The register's sitemap is the small one.** `videositemap.xml` has 631 entries of the curated web selection. **`newvideositemap.xml` has 15 682** — the real sequence-level catalogue, at `https://cinememoire.net/notice?num_seq=<N>`, each entry carrying a `<video:content_loc>` **direct MP4**, a `<video:duration>` in seconds, and a description that is often a full shot list. It is a 24 MB file; fetch it once and grep locally, which is one request for the entire archive. That is by far the cheapest catalogue in the beat.
- Grepping it for theme terms: `exposition universelle` → 7 (three of them **Expo 58 Brussels**, incl. the Atomium), `nucléaire`/`atomique` → 13 (Mururoa, Marcoule, Tricastin), `observatoire`/`télescope` → 18 (incl. a second Nançay film), `robot` → 9, `futur` → 25, `ordinateur` → 25 (nearly all false — the word appears in modern newsroom descriptions). **`science` and `laboratoire` are noise here**; the productive queries are place and machine names.
- MP4s are served directly with `content-type: video/mp4` and a `last-modified` from 2013 — verified with a HEAD, no download. No yt-dlp needed at all.
- **Etiquette**: robots.txt publishes the sitemaps and bans only SemrushBot/BLEXBot/MJ12bot. One sitemap fetch plus a HEAD was my whole footprint on this site.
- **Caveat**: a large part of the 15 682 is post-2000 local TV news rushes (Marseille court reporting, football, fires) deposited by a production company — these dominate keyword results and are almost never on theme. The 1930s–1980s amateur and colonial material is the reason to be here.
- **Beyond this round**: Marseille 1930s (the transbordeur, Prado, the 1956 ice winter), Indochina 1951–53, Cameroon, Congo, Chad, Mali, Madagascar, Haiti — colonial-era footage with essentially no other home. Rights are unresolved there and the owner should rule before any of it is cut.

### rtve.es (NO-DO + RTVE archive) — **the richest site in the beat, and the one that needs a method**
- **Do not use `/play/buscador/`.** It is an Angular SPA whose results are relevance-weighted towards recent, popular programmes: searching `carta de ajuste` returns 30 episodes of a soap called *Cartas en el tiempo*, and `sorteo de la loteria` returns nothing archival at all. The register's 503s did not recur for me — the problem is relevance, not rate limiting.
- **The search API** (read out of `/play/buscador/main.*.js`) is `https://api.rtve.es/api/search/contents?search=<q>&page=1&size=60&context=tve&type=completo&tipology=video&isExpanded=true&isChild=true&useOntology=false`, JSON, no auth, Referer helps. Same relevance problem, so it is only useful for a lucky single hit.
- **What actually works — enumerate the programme.** `https://api.rtve.es/api/programas/50991/videos.json?size=300&page=<1..14>` returns the **whole NO-DO catalogue: 4 064 items**, each with `longTitle`, `duration` (ms), `htmlUrl`, and a `description` that is the newsreel's **full item list in broadcast order, in capitals**. Fourteen requests at a 6 s gap, all 200, no 503. Then grep offline. Every NO-DO candidate above came out of that index, including the Pierre Henry item, which no keyword search on the site would ever have surfaced. **A future scout should pull this index once and keep it.**
- Programme id 50991 = NO-DO. The same `/api/programas/<id>/videos.json` shape will work for *Revista Imágenes*, *Documentales B/N*, *Documentales Color*, *Imágenes del Deporte*, *Archivo TVE* and *Filmoteca* — I only had budget for NO-DO. Get an id by reading `programInfo.id` from `https://api.rtve.es/api/videos/<videoid>.json`.
- Offline grep counts over the 4 064 NO-DO descriptions, as a map for whoever comes next: computers/cybernetics **23**, electronic music **2** (both queued — #1 and #11), randomness/lottery/games of chance **131**, "the future"/año 2000 **132**, nuclear **154**, space/satellite/observatory **220**, automation/robot/electronics **121**, exhibitions and fairs **1 364**.
- **Media**: `yt-dlp -s` uses the named `rtve.es:alacarta` extractor, m3u8 + MPD, confirmed live on video 1487625 (`hls-844-1`). Fine.
- **Timing**: NO-DO items are 9–11 minutes carrying 4–8 reports, and the description lists them **in order** with no timecodes. My `est.` windows are (item index ÷ item count) × duration; treat them as a starting point for `--propose`, not as truth. The two standalone *Nodo — Lotería de Navidad* clips (42 s, 61 s) are the exception: already cut, already reel-length.
- **Licence**: no statement on any item page and rtve.es's aviso legal reserves rights, so I have written every RTVE row **Tier B**. But NO-DO (1943–1981) was produced by the Spanish state and is digitised with Filmoteca Española; if the owner wants to rule it Tier A the argument is strong and it would unlock 40 s windows on the best material in this queue. **Flagging for a ruling, not deciding it.**
- **Note for the pool**: the pool already holds `nodo-noticiario-espanol` from an archive.org mirror. Nothing above is that item — these are specific numbered noticiarios — but the cutter should eyeball the existing reel before cutting #5 or #19.

### pasouoquepasou.crtvg.gal (CRTVG "Pasou o que pasou", Galician)
- **Search**: `/search/node?keys=` is the wrong door — it returns the Drupal page furniture. The exposed-filter field is `combine`: `/search/node?combine=<term>`. Real results come first; **the last nine links on every page are the "O máis visto" sidebar** (Prince in A Coruña, the Rolling Stones in Vigo, Man de Camelle…) and must be filtered out or every query looks like it returned nine hits.
- Category browse works too: `/categorias/<slug>?page=<n>`, e.g. `ciencia-saude-e-medio-ambiente`, `arquivo-historico`, `economia`.
- **Detail pages** give the date, a Galician one-line description, decade, location, named people and sometimes the exact location ("Administración de Loterías Nº 5 'La Favorita'"). **No duration is published** — the two numbers on the card are likes and view count, not time. Everything here is `timing: audition`.
- **Media**: an `.m3u8` is in the page markup, on `ondemand-crtvg-origin.flumotion.com`, e.g. `…/videos/arquivo/7/1/1/4_…/7114_alt_9241_….mp4/playlist.m3u8`. Grep the HTML for `\.m3u8`; the Drupal category pages are not yt-dlp-readable but the node pages hand you the URL.
- **Etiquette**: `Crawl-delay: 10` in robots.txt. Honoured; ~15 requests, all 200.
- **The honest finding**: this archive starts in 1985 and is TVG news cut into single stories. **It has essentially nothing on priorities 1 and 2** — searching `ordenador`, `informática`, `tecnoloxía`, `espazo` returns one item between them (a 1986 printworks). Its science category is the Prestige spill, forest fires and mad cow disease. Its value to this project is **language**: the pool has no Galician at all, and #26/#28/#29 put three Galician voices in it. If the owner wants more Galician, the better use of the next pass is type 1 and type 6 — idents, weather, continuity — not this round's theme. `/content/30-anos-de-tvg` is where the channel's own old idents live, inside a 2015 anniversary montage.
- **Not checked, and worth it**: the sibling `crtvg.gal/tvg/a-carta/etiqueta/arquivo` (a named `Crtvg` yt-dlp extractor exists) holds different cuts, per the source register.
