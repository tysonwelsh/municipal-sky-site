# `far-languages` — video queue, reels round 4
Scout: Opus 5, 2026-09-14. Sites: https://www.isuma.tv/ (Inuktut, Igloolik/Iqaluit/Baker Lake — IBC, Inukshuk Project, Nunatsiakmiut), https://uluulu.recollectcms.com/ (ʻUluʻulu, Hawaiʻi's state moving-image archive — KHET/PBS Hawaiʻi, KGMB, KHON, KITV).

**The one-line finding.** Both theme leads landed. The Anik satellite arriving in the Arctic
is on IsumaTV twice over and from the Inuit side of it: the **Inukshuk Broadcasting System
grand opening (1980, Igloolik, Anik B)** and, two years later, **a whole 1982 programme about
the town voting Yes or No on whether to let television in at all**. Mauna Kea did *not* land
— ʻUluʻulu's astronomy holdings are Hōkūleʻa navigation and NASA-administrator interviews,
not the summit — but ʻUluʻulu paid off somewhere better: a **1971 KHET broadcast of a
university professor demonstrating an electronic synthesizer**, and a 1978 public-access tape
of **Ed Emshwiller's video-synthesizer and computer-animation work**. Those two are the beat's
priority-1 hits. Everything is Tier B: neither archive publishes an open licence.

## Site notes

### https://uluulu.recollectcms.com/ (ʻUluʻulu, Recollect CMS 1.5)
- **Search, the working form.** `https://uluulu.recollectcms.com/nodes/search?keywords=<term>` is the one that filters. `?searchkeywords=`, `?search=` and `/nodes/search/searchkeywords/<term>` all return the *whole* catalogue (302 pages) and look like results — check `data-maxpage` and the first node id to tell. Add `&page_number=N` to page. The form's own POST endpoint `/nodes/definesearch` is **disallowed by robots.txt**; the GET above is not, and works.
- The index searches the Summary field, so plain-English content words pay: `computer`, `technology`, `automation`, `synthesizer`, `experimental`, `televised broadcast`, `news reel`, `computer center`, `virtual reality`. Single rare words beat phrases. `Coraggio` found the synthesizer episode on its own.
- **Item pages give the cutter everything**: `<source src="https://uluulu.recollectcms.com/assets/show/1/<assetid>" type="video/mp4">` straight in the HTML, plus Duration, Color, Sound, Collection, Subjects and a rights line. `yt-dlp -s` resolves these pages with the generic extractor (confirmed on node 47492).
- **Rights are uniform and unhelpful**: every item tested prints *"This work is copyright protected and not available for download. Please contact the archive for more information."* There is no per-item PD or CC statement anywhere I found, including on a 1960 California Academy of Sciences film. Treat the whole site as **Tier B**, and note the archive asks to be contacted — a state archive that answers email is a realistic Tier A route for anything the owner falls in love with (`(808) 689-2740`, UH West Oʻahu).
- **Collection vs item**: a record with `Item Type: Collection` has no player. Check for `<source src=` before queueing.
- Robots allows everything used here. No rate limiting observed at ~1 request / 2 s; the site is small, so stay light.
- **What else is there, beyond this round**: the KGMB ENG Files (~400 hours of raw 1979–80 news), the KITV News Library, `Mānaleo` (hundreds of hours of native-speaker ʻōlelo Hawaiʻi, 1995–2009), `Rice & Roses` (a complete labour-affairs series), `Pau Hana Years`, and the whole `UH Today` run (episodes #17–#52, KHET 1971, film, much of it B&W and several silent). **UH Today is the richest single vein for this project** and I have only sampled it.

### https://www.isuma.tv/ (IsumaTV, Drupal)
- **robots.txt disallows `/search/` and `/?q=search/`** — the site's own keyword box posts to `/search/node` and was therefore not used. `Crawl-delay: 10` is declared and was honoured (one request per 10 s throughout).
- **Use `/keyword/<tag>` instead.** Every item page lists its tags as links; the taxonomy pages are crawlable and are how nearly everything above was found. Productive tags: `technology`, `science`, `television`, `radio`, `internet`, `weather`, `video`, `camera`, `history`. Empty or near-empty: `satellite`, `computer`, `future`, `random`, `broadcasting`. Tag spelling is the uploader's, so try the plain noun first.
- **The catalogue shortcut that made this beat.** IBC published a PDF of ~306 episode descriptions for its Channel 2 — `https://www.isuma.tv/sites/default/files/attachments/180425_isuma-ibc_channel_2_video_descriptions-shareable.pdf` (161 pp, 600 KB), linked from https://www.isuma.tv/ibc-2/ibc-channel-2-video-descriptions. Each entry gives the programme name, producer, host, camera, location, **a numbered segment list** and key words, with the isuma.tv URL. One fetch replaces hundreds. That document is where the Anik B grand opening, the Igloolik television vote, the telephone-installation flight and the Cambridge Bay radio station all came from. The same page says comparable description sets exist for `/ibc` (821 videos, 200 added 2018), `/ibc-3` (200), `/takuginai-educator-series` (161 clips) and `/pituqait` — **I did not chase those; the next scout should.**
- **The channel listings do NOT describe episodes** — every Amitturmiut row repeats the same series blurb — so ranking off `/ibc?page=N` is impossible. Go through the PDF or the keyword pages.
- **How the media resolves (important for the cutter).** `yt-dlp` **fails** on isuma.tv item pages ("Unsupported URL") — the player is built by JS. But the item HTML always contains the poster at `https://s3.amazonaws.com/isuma.video.thumbs.large/<name>.mp4.jpg`, and the SD source is the same name under a different bucket: **`https://s3.amazonaws.com/isuma.video.mp4_sd/<name>.mp4.mp4`** (note the doubled extension). Verified 200 `video/mp4` on the Inukshuk grand opening. `isuma.video.mp4_hd` and the single-extension form both 404. Some pages (e.g. `/artco/zkmp-technologyinternetinuk`) print the mp4_sd URL directly; most do not.
- **Licence, honestly.** IBC items carry a standing note — *"made available on the Internet for your enjoyment through funding provided by the Government of Canada and the Government of Nunavut"* — which is an access statement, not a reuse licence. Isuma's Terms & Conditions are the only site-wide text. The source scout's ruling stands: **Tier B legally, and something stronger than Tier B in practice** — IBC and Isuma operate community protocols on ceremonial material, and nothing in this queue is ceremonial precisely because I kept to broadcasting, technology and school material. IBC asks for `info@inuitbroadcasting.ca` with the URL for copies; that is the right first move if the owner wants any of the top three for real.
- **`/probed-visual` is a third-party uploader**, not IBC — an account posting broadcast fragments (a Weather Network off-line feed, 6–12 s title cards from the Margery Hinds tapes and a 2010 elders series). Provenance is unstated. The title cards are 6 s each, under the 12 s window, so they are only useful stitched; the Pelmorex off-line feed at 2:58 is queueable on its own. Check before cutting.
- **Where the theme is thin, and why.** Priority 1 — chance music, generative art, electronic studios — returns **nothing** on IsumaTV, and that is not a search failure: IBC's archive is hunting, sewing, language, health, politics and community, filmed by the community. The beat's priority-1 hits both come from ʻUluʻulu. What IsumaTV has instead, and has better than anywhere, is **priority 2 told from the receiving end** — the satellite, the telephone, the radio station, the terminal, arriving in towns that were asked whether they wanted them.
- **Two more leads I did not have time to open**: `/isumatvnunavut/tvguide` (an actual TV schedule page, likely the route to Uvagut TV continuity and idents) and `/nitv` (Nunavut Independent Television, the Igloolik station itself). Both are in-beat and native.
