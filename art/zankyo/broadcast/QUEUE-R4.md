# ZANKYŌ broadcast — the round-4 video queue (theme: chance, computers, the future)

*2026-09-14, evening. Eleven Opus video scouts, one per locale with Pile A
sites (`SOURCES.md` §A), about two hours each, on the owner's theme: chance
music and early generative art first, then computers and the history of
technology, science, the imagined future, randomness on air. Nothing is
downloaded yet. The owner picks from this queue; cutting follows in the
reels-3 shape (`CURATE.md`).*

## How to read it

- **One file per locale** in `queue-r4/`, verbatim from its scout: a ranked
  candidate table, the scout's Top 5, a passed-over table so nobody
  re-scouts, and site notes for whoever cuts (how the player resolves, the
  search terms that worked, rate notes).
- **Columns:** `licence as found` is what the item page or site terms say;
  `tier` is the scout's A/B call under `CURATE.md`'s rulings. `theme tags`
  come from a fixed list (aleatoric, generative, computer-music,
  electronic-studio, computer, technology-history, science, space, nuclear,
  future, utopia, dystopia, city-of-tomorrow, randomness, lottery, ident,
  signoff, testcard, clock, weather, jingle, school-tv, industrial).
  `moments` are timecoded where the archive publishes a shot list or chapter
  marks, and `timing: audition` where it does not; one moment per candidate
  is marked LONG when a stretch stays interesting for 18–40 s.
- Southeast Asia sat this round out: it has no Pile A site.

## Decisions for the owner (C)

1. **Señal Memoria plays every video from RTVC's own YouTube channel.** Only
   two native `.mp4` in 407 pages. The Latin American queue (30 candidates,
   incl. Jacqueline Nova and the 1956 control room) is therefore YouTube
   fetches in all but name, from a broadcaster-owned channel curated by the
   national archive. Cut from that path, or drop the beat's video queue?
2. **filmisadocument.jp streams no audio track** (four video-only
   renditions). Seven Japanese candidates would be silent reels — the pool
   has never had one. Allow picture-only reels (the station's own room tone
   under them), or drop the seven? The NARA-sourced AFVN item is the same
   question.
3. **NO-DO's tier.** Spanish state production, but RTVE shows no licence;
   queued Tier B, arguably Tier A. Tier A would allow 40 s windows on the
   Corticalart, the cybernetics competition and the lottery drums.
4. **Daehan News is Tier B as found**, not Tier A as the source scout
   guessed: item pages carry no 공공누리 badge and the copyright route goes
   through KTV's licensing desk. Stays B unless the owner wants to ask KTV.
5. **RTS Planeta (Serbia) is DRM on every asset** (25 of 25 protected, DASH
   only) plus a registration wall on some categories. It leaves Pile A;
   Serbian yielded nothing this round. Note it and move on, or is Serbia
   worth a letter like the five on the roadmap?
