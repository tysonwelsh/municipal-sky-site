THE SOURCE

The Elma "Classic" collet knob is the knob of the Swiss studio: on every Studer A80 and A810 audio module, on EMT plates and Neve desks. A plain cylinder of satin-black anodised aluminium, clamped to the shaft by a collet under the cap (so there is no set-screw and no skirt), with a chamfered top edge and a snap-in plastic cap in a colour that carries meaning across the machine: grey for level, red for the record side, blue and green for monitoring. The pointer is a white line moulded proud on the cap, running from its centre to its edge and no further. The scale is not on the knob: it is a ring of eleven engraved dots on the panel over 270 degrees, with tiny numerals only at 0 and 10, and a DIN-like engraved legend below.

The tells kept here: the pure cylinder, its chamfer catching one bright ring of light at the top-left; the cap a step below the chamfer, with an inner shadow at its top-left and a lit lip at the bottom-right; the pointer line with its own moulded shadow; the anodised body as a matte black with a faint concentric grain and a soft top-left highlight but no specular; the dark contact ring where the body stands proud of the panel; a light band of reflected light on the underside; the engraved dots and legend with a lit edge (A810, light grey) or a shadowed edge (A80, dark grey); a little finger grime below-right. cap picks the colour, diameter (14 / 21 / 28 mm) the size, panel (a810 / a80) the plate. Drag is vertical (up is clockwise), the wheel steps, arrows step and Shift-arrows step by ten; 270 degrees, heavy.

Left out: the collet itself and the knurled locking ring under the cap, the A810's module screws.
