THE SOURCE

The Technics SL-1200 MK2 pitch control is the long black slot plate on the right of the silver plinth: a flat, cool, silver-anodised aluminium cap with fine ridges across it, a white scale printed down the plate from −8 at the top to +8 at the bottom, a longer white centre line at 0, and beside it the small rectangular window of the green quartz-lock lamp that lights only when the slider is exactly at zero. The MK2 has a mechanical click at the centre; the MK3D famously deleted it.

The tells kept here: the matte black plate let into the brushed silver, with the recess shadow at its top-left; the ridged metal cap that is shinier in the middle where thumbs have polished it, standing proud with a contact shadow; the mirror-image scale in a Helvetica-like face, − at the top and + at the bottom; the centre line and the green lamp right beside it, on at 0 and off the moment the cap leaves; the detent — within ±0.3 % of centre the cap is pulled to 0 with a 30 ms click and seats a hair deeper; "PITCH ADJ" printed below the slot. The cap follows the pointer exactly; the wheel steps 0.05 % (Shift 0.5); arrows step 0.05 % (Shift 0.5); Home/End go to −8 and +8.

Left out: the rest of the plinth (strobe, start/stop, the "Technics" script), the reset button of the later M5G, the ±16 % range switch of the MK5.
