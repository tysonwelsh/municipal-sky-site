THE SOURCE

The Tektronix 500-series scope cabinets (535A, 545A, 547) are finished in a specific blue-grey hammertone enamel — a fine all-over pattern of soft round dimples baked onto the steel — with a cream anodised aluminium sub-panel set a millimetre below the surface to carry the controls' engraved legends. It is a swatch other pages can mount instruments on, not a control in itself: `value` is the legend engraved on the cream inset.

The tells kept here: the dimples are a seeded SVG turbulence run through a diffuse-lighting filter and hard-light blended onto the base colour — never a tiled bitmap — so each one gets its own highlight on the upper-left rim and shadow at lower-right from the same light that lights everything else on the page; a blue-grey that reads faintly metallic; the cream inset sunk behind a dark line at its top-left and a light line at its bottom-right; four proud nickel screws at the inset's corners; radiused (4 px) steel corners; and `finish="smooth"` swaps the dimples for the 465's flatter satin orange-peel. `wear` chips a corner to bare steel with a rust halo. A default `<slot>` lets a host page mount other controls on the swatch.

Left out: the true Phillips-screw thread count, the cabinet's carrying handle and rear vents, and any Tektronix wordmark (a plain engraved legend stands in).
