THE SOURCE

The Atari CX40 shipped with the 1977 VCS/2600: a square black ABS base with a moulded texture, an orange-and-black striped overlay screwed to the top, a rubber accordion boot around the stick's base, a black stick with a flared four-flat grip, and one concave orange fire button at the top-left corner. Underneath, four leaf-switch contacts read the stick as eight directions plus centre — there is no analog position, only on/off per axis.

The tells kept here: the square black silhouette with the diagonal orange/black candy-stripe frame; the stick's continuous lean toward the pointer with the rubber boot nudging along with it; the fire button's concave dish, lit on the far wall and shadowed on the near wall, with a 2 px press; the flared grip with its moulded flats and a polished cap where thumbs land; a spring return that snaps the stick to centre a beat after release, matching the leaf switches disengaging. The generic "VIDEO GAME CONTROL" wordmark stands in for the original trademarked overlay text. Drag follows the pointer's direction (angle-follow, not vertical); the reported value snaps to the nearest digital reading per axis unless analog is set; arrows tilt, Space or Enter fires.

Left out: the rear cable and DE-9 connector, the underside's four rubber feet in full relief, the exact leaf-switch click sound.
