THE SOURCE

The Atari VCS of 1977 — the heavy sixer — has six identical chromed slide switches in a row across its black moulded front bezel, above the walnut-print vinyl strip: POWER, TV TYPE, LEFT and RIGHT DIFFICULTY, and the two spring-loaded ones, GAME SELECT and GAME RESET. The chrome tip is the only shiny thing on a black-and-wood front; it travels about 8 mm sideways in its slot and stops hard.

The tells kept here: the small bright chrome bar with its hard horizon line, rounded and slightly tapered, sitting in a black slot with a dark shadow above it; the black bezel as a raised island with a soft radius and a lit top-left lip; the white silk-screened legends — the option words above the slot ends, ON at the left and OFF at the right, the function name in small capitals to the right; the woodgrain vinyl below, drawn as stripes with a repeating darker figure; a scuff on the POWER switch's chrome. The tip slides in 70 ms with an ease-out; the momentary variant (momentary) springs back in 120 ms and emits press, release and activate. Click toggles; drag the tip across the slot's middle; Space or Enter toggles; Left/Right arrows and Home/End pick a side.

Left out: the four-switch and Vader front panels, the cartridge slot, the "Atari" fuji and the model number printed on the bezel.
