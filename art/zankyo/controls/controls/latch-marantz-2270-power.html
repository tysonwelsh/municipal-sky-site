THE SOURCE

The Marantz 2270's POWER button is not a toggle but a push-push latch: a machined aluminium cap with a bright chamfered edge, standing proud of a matching brushed champagne fascia, that you push once to latch in and push again to release. A hint of the dial glass sits just above it, and when the receiver is on it warms to the Marantz blue.

The tells kept here: the cap and fascia as two tones of the same brushed metal, separated only by the chamfer's bright line; the horizontal grain running the same direction across both; the cap sitting visibly lower in its escutcheon when latched, with the dark rim reading thinner above and wider below it; "POWER" engraved above in thin, widely tracked capitals; and a faint fingerprint smudge on the cap's face. The lamp attribute wires the dial strip above to brighten over 600 ms when the button is on and fade over 300 ms when it's off; without it the strip stays dark.

Left out: the rest of the receiver's face, the screws that hold the escutcheon, and the push-push mechanism's own click sound.
