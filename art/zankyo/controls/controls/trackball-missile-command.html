THE SOURCE

The Atari Trak-Ball is the 4.5 in white phenolic ball sunk into the middle of the Missile Command control panel, in a black cast-aluminium bezel with a raised, polished lip. Three chrome-ringed FIRE buttons — red, white, red — sit above it, one for each silo, over the game's red-and-blue control-panel artwork.

The tells kept here: the ball's cap rising well past the bezel's hole with a hot highlight at ten o'clock and a soft core shadow; a speckled surface that visibly turns with the drag so the roll reads, and keeps drifting a little after release before friction stops it; the raised ring's polish on its front, lit top-left and shadowed on the inside of the hole; a grey grime band at the visible cap's equator; a sliver of the black cup peeking at the bezel's bottom-left. Dragging anywhere on the ball rolls it (a delta value, {dx,dy} per tick, accumulating in value); releasing keeps it coasting under friction for about 800 ms; the wheel nudges y; arrows roll with a little acceleration on repeat; each FIRE button is its own focus stop.

Left out: the roller assembly under the ball, the cabinet's full artwork, the 2.25 in Centipede variant.
