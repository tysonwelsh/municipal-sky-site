THE SOURCE

The Minimoog Model D's solid black-walnut end cheeks and base skirt, and the same timber on a Prophet-5 rev 3 or a Clavinet D6: a hand-oiled finish rubbed into the grain rather than lacquered over it, so the wood stays matte and a little dark, with the front edge broken to a soft round that the light catches as one clean line.

The tells kept here: straight long grain with a slight waviness and one cathedral arch built from a stretched noise field mapped through a warm three-stop gradient, so it crowds and curves rather than repeating; short dark pore dashes running with the grain; a deep chocolate ground with warm red-brown early wood, darker than any lacquered veneer; a single soft highlight along the rounded front edge and a paler line of bare wood at the top edge where hands rest over the years; the piece sitting proud of its mounting panel by a couple of pixels, with its own contact shadow. orientation turns the grain to run along the long or short axis, end adds a cut end-grain band with its own ray pattern, and edge="square" drops the round-over for a plain machined edge.

Left out: the cheek's screw bosses and the case-bolt counterbores on its inner face.
