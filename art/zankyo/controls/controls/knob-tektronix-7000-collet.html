THE SOURCE

The Tektronix 7000-series plug-in knob: a small turned aluminium collet knob, anodised the same cool grey-blue as the plug-in's panel but a shade darker, with fine flutes cut into the lower half of the side wall only (the upper half is smooth, where the chamfer runs into it), and a black plastic cap insert with one crisp white hairline sitting inside a thin bright chamfer. Under it, the legend in dark condensed silk-screened capitals: POSITION, VARIABLE, TRIG LEVEL. The VARIABLE knobs of a VOLTS/DIV pair are the same knob shrunk, with a red cap, standing concentric in front of the larger one.

The tells kept here: the 36 flutes on the lower half of the side, placed by angle and projected so they crowd toward the edges and catch the light on the left; the smooth upper half; grey on grey, the knob darker than the panel and both cool; the black cap recessed inside a bright chamfer ring with the white hairline; the panel's horizontal brushed grain; the legend in "Barlow Condensed" caps with a thin printed 300-degree arc, tiny arrow-heads for direction when arrows is set, and a CAL mark at the clockwise end when the inner knob is present (inner="cal" or 0–10 sets its angle; it is display only in this build, the coupled pair proper is the A5.2 selector). Drag is vertical (up is clockwise), the wheel steps, arrows step and Shift-arrows step by ten; 300 degrees.

Left out: the plug-in's release latch and the dots of the graticule illumination, the hex socket in the collet under the cap.
