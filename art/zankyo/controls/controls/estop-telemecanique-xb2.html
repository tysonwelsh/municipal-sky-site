THE SOURCE

The Telemecanique XB2-BS — everybody calls it the coup de poing, the punch — is the 40 mm red mushroom on the front of every French machine tool, lift controller and packaging line since the 1960s, and through Schneider it is still made. The ZB2-BY9330 legend plate is the part that makes it legible from the far end of a workshop: a 60 mm matte yellow aluminium ring with ARRÊT D'URGENCE curved over the top and EMERGENCY STOP curved under the bottom, black on chrome yellow.

The tells kept here: the flattened glossy crown with one hard specular and a highlight ring around its upper-left edge; the deep ribbed skirt beneath — twelve ribs, each shaded by its own angle to the above-left light, so they burn white toward ten o'clock and die at four; the two white moulded arrows turning clockwise on the crown that say turn to release; the chrome collar showing its hard horizon in the crescent the head's overhang does not cover; the yellow plate as a separate part, with a hairline shadow at its edge, a fine anodising grain and two keying flats at the sides; the palm smudge on the crown, because this is the one control on the machine that people hit with a whole hand. Latched, the head sits 4 px lower, the chrome crescent below it closes up while a new one opens above, and the shadow tightens — the state reads without needing a colour change.

Left out: the rear contact block with its NC/NO flags, the 22.5 mm mounting nut behind the panel, and the moulded TIRER POUR DÉVERROUILLER line some heads carry instead of the arrows.
