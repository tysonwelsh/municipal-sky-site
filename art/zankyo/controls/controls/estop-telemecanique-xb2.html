THE SOURCE

The Telemecanique XB2-BS — everyone calls it the coup de poing, the punch — is the 40 mm red mushroom on the front of every French machine tool, lift controller and packaging line since the 1960s, and through Schneider it is still made. The ZB2-BY9330 legend plate is the part that makes it legible from the far end of a workshop: a 60 mm matte yellow aluminium ring, ARRÊT D'URGENCE curved over the top and EMERGENCY STOP curved under the bottom, black on chrome yellow.

The tells kept here: the flattened glossy dome with its one hard specular and a highlight ring around the upper-left edge; the deep ribbed skirt beneath it — twelve ribs, each shaded by its own angle to the above-left light, so they brighten toward ten o'clock and die at four; the two white moulded arrows turning clockwise on the crown that say turn to release; the chrome collar showing a hard horizon line in the crescent the head's overhang does not cover; the yellow plate as a separate part with a hairline shadow at its edge and two keying flats at the sides; and the palm smudge on the crown, because this is the one control on the machine people hit with a whole hand. Latched, the head sits 4 px lower, the chrome crescent below it closes up and its shadow tightens — the state reads without colour.

Left out: the rear contact block and its NC/NO flags, the 22.5 mm mounting nut behind the panel, and the moulded TIRER POUR DÉVERROUILLER line that some heads carry instead of the arrows.
