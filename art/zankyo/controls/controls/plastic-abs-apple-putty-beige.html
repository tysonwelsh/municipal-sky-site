THE SOURCE

The Apple II family's putty-beige ABS shell — carried through the II, II+, IIe and the Disk II — is a warm, slightly greenish beige moulded with a fine leather-like pebble, a row of slotted vents over the power supply, and a small recessed badge. Every surviving example has yellowed unevenly: the plastic's flame-retardant browns under UV, fastest where light actually falls on it.

The tells kept here: the fine pebble grain, matte everywhere with no reflection band; a moulded vent grille of rounded slots each with a dark interior and a lit rib top; a faint mould-seam line with a 1 px step; a recessed rectangular badge holding the legend, dark inside with no ink outside it; and, with yellow, an uneven amber cast that stops sharply at a rectangular patch of the original beige — the shadow of a lid or a decal that shielded the plastic underneath. finish="platinum" swaps in the cooler, darker 1987 case colour, which barely yellows at all. wear polishes the pebble smooth and faintly shinier where hands rested.

Left out: the keyboard well and the rainbow Apple mark (a plain recessed badge stands in for it).
