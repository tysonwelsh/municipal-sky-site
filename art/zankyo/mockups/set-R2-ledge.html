OPTION R2 · 操作段 THE CONTROL LEDGE restrained round

The argument. The screen is the event; everything below it is a servant. So this option changes exactly one thing about the set you already have: the 30 px chin, which was never moulded — it was a row of leftovers — becomes a properly moulded ledge of 63 px. Nothing else moves. The plastic is the same plastic, the corner radius is the same 11 px, the wall is the same 13 px, the crack and the tape are untouched, and a visitor who has seen the live set will not notice that the casing changed at all until they look at the bottom. The bet is that a ledge can be beautiful without being big: that the difference between a control strip that looks bolted on and one that looks injection-moulded is four details worth about eight pixels each — a parting line, a draft angle, a recess with a lit front lip, and button faces that sit below the plastic around them — and that once those are right, sixty-three pixels is plenty of room for five controls and there is nothing to apologise for.

WHAT CHANGED FROM THE LIVE CASING

  • The chin became a ledge. .zk-set2-chin (30 px, display:flex, four things at 7 px gaps) is replaced by .zk-ledge, which runs to the casing's own edges (margin: 0 −13px) and carries the cabinet's bottom radius, so it is one piece of plastic with the cabinet.
  • Four pieces of moulding, all load-bearing. (1) A parting line across the top: 1 px of groove and 1 px of lit lower lip, where the bezel face steps back to the ledge face. (2) A draft angle: 9 px at each end falling away from the viewer, so the ledge reads as a face pulled out of a tool rather than a plane. (3) A recess — the well — whose upper wall is dark because the ledge above casts into it and whose front wall is lit because it faces the room; every control face sits inside it, 4 px below the surrounding plastic. (4) Two ejector-pin witnesses on the face, where the tool pushed the part off the core. Nobody notices them; the plastic looks wrong without them.
  • The controls. 選局 (a 20 px knob) is gone — no rotary on the ledge at all. 受信 survives as one of five, folded into the row. The master volume knob on the console below is now the only rotary on the whole faceplate, and it is the one control where a knob is the honest answer.
  • The tube geometry. Unchanged at every width: 488 × 366 at 960, full width on a phone. The ledge costs 33 px of faceplate height, not one pixel of picture.

THE FIVE CONTROLS, WIRED FOR REAL

  • 輝度 BRIGHT — a 4-step rocker. Drives the shader's black lift and gain ((l − lift) × gain, 25/1.03 → 6/1.70) and the bloom pass's alpha and blur radius together, which is what brightness on a CRT actually was: not a multiply, but how hard the beam is driven and how much the phosphor spreads.
  • 焦点 FOCUS — a 3-step rocker, 鋭 / 中 / 粗. Reallocates the raster live: 192 × 144 → 96 × 72 → 48 × 36. 鋭 is the reels' native size; 粗 is a Paik mush of moving blocks. The source, phosphor, glow and bloom canvases are all resized on the step, so the cost goes with it.
  • 蛍光体 TUBE — a lit cycle button. Swaps the LUT: P39 long-persistence yellow-green (the live tube), P3 amber, P4 blue-white. The crack's own glow, the Paik line, the idle sweep and the decay colour all follow the phosphor, so the swap reads instantly. The button's lens takes the tube's colour, which is how you know which one you are on without reading anything.
  • 受信 RECEIVE — momentary. A real reel, tuned in now.
  • 常時 HOLD — latching, with a red lamp. The picture stops leaving: the hold phase never expires, so the set becomes a continuous television instead of a visitation. Latch it while the tube is dark and it receives at once. Unlatch it and the current picture gets a second and a half of grace, then loses the way it always did.

MOBILE

At ≤ 700 px the two column wrappers dissolve with display: contents and the four things take an explicit order: television → spectrum scope → 段階 progress bar → PLAY / STOP / volume. The tube stays full width and the ledge thins its walls to 9 px with the casing.

At ≤ 430 px the well goes to two rows, and the reason is arithmetic. Five controls across a 308 px well is 62 px each; a rocker that wide has 26 px ends, which is not a thumb. So the well runs on a six-column grid: the two rockers take three columns each (measured 160 px, ends of 48 px) and the three buttons take two each (measured 105 px). Both rows fill the width exactly — there is no spare space anywhere on the ledge at any width, which was the complaint about the last round. The ledge is 105 px on a 390 px phone under a 327 × 246 picture, and every pixel of it is a control; at every width above 430 px it is one row and 63 px.

THE TOUCH TARGET, AND THE MOULDING IT HIDES BEHIND

The moulding needs a 36 px cap — any taller and the ledge stops being a shallow band. A finger needs 44 px. Those are different numbers, so they are different boxes. Every control in the well measures at least 44 × 44 at every width while the plastic it paints stays exactly 36 px (35 on a phone) and does not move a pixel:

  • The three buttons are 44 px tall with margin-block pulled back by the overhang, so the grid row still measures the cap; the painted face is ::before, inset by the overhang, and it carries the background, the shadows, the radius and the 2 px of travel.
  • The four rocker ends could not do that — their cap is 30 % of the rocker, which is 34 px wide at 960, so the target has to be wider than the paint as well as taller. The cap is a plain span in the flex row and the button is an invisible box laid over it at max(44px, 30%), lapping into the indicator window, which is decorative and has nothing to hit. Two of them never meet: 2 × 44 = 88 px against a rocker that is 114 px at its narrowest here.
  • On the phone the two rows lean apart instead of paying for a wider gap. A 44 px target over a 35 px cap overhangs its row; at the 5 px row gap two rows would overlap by 4 px and the boundary between 輝度 and 蛍光体 would be a coin toss. Widening the gap would cost the ledge 4 px of height. So the top row hangs 7 px up and 2 px down and the bottom row 2 px up and 7 px down — both still 44 px, clearing each other by 1 px, and the ledge stays exactly 105 px. The cost is 2.5 px of bias off the cap's centre, which is nothing under a fingertip.
  • :active, the latch and the focus ring are all retargeted at the cap, so the ring hugs the plastic and not the invisible box, and -webkit-tap-highlight-color: transparent stops iOS painting a rectangle over the part of the target that is not the control.
