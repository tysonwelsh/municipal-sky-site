Two tubes, two phosphors

THE SET · P39 green #9ae6ae (core #d6f8de): the old receive-only CRT-9 is a green tube. Its picture is unchanged.

THE SCOPE · colour tube: pink #ff2d55, magenta #ff3df0, violet #676c9d, lilac #e5a6ff, and the cyan echo #16e0e0. The live ZANKYŌ title is back exactly as zankyo.css draws it (white core, pink glow, cyan echo on top, magenta echo below), and so is the visualizer as zankyo-viz.js paints it (RUNNING shows the captured frames). Notes

  • 4b keeps every module from option 4 and drops the rack around them. The chassis (.zankyo-frame) is now one plain plate in the same powder-coat and grain as the panel the lit examples stood on. There are no rails, slots, crossmember or kick plate. The two louvres stay because 逸脱 hides in the bottom one.
  • Two-tube rule. Each tube has its own phosphor: the set is green P39, the scope is a colour tube. The scope's neon is confined to .zk-monitor .zk-screen, where the old --red / --magenta / --cyan names point at the --tube-* tokens, so the live title and glitch rules apply unchanged. Everywhere else those names point at the four panel lamps.
  • One finish. Every module has the same powder-coat, the same 3 px radius, the same alloy captive screws, edge wear and cream screen print. The console is fourteen identical 1U strips with label-tape channel numbers, black anodised preview pushes and 入 · 切 slide switches.
  • Trade-off: the chassis is now the same coat as the modules, so they separate by edge, shadow and screws, not by a step in tone. The 3 px gaps between console strips read as seams rather than slots.
  • No viz port needed. The visualizer keeps its live colours; they now define the scope's family instead of clashing with the panel's.
