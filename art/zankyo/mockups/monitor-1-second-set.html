MOCKUP 1 · 隣 THE SECOND SET

Argument. The signal is a visitor, so it gets its own chair. A second set beside the scope keeps the machine's instruments intact — the scope never stops reading the mix, the bargraph keeps its arc — and gives the broadcast a body the yard bolted on later: an older receive-only tube on a steel strap, its glass cracked and taped, dark for most of the night. The crack is the point: the picture never arrives whole. It refracts across four shards, each slipped a few pixels, and a chip in the lower-left corner shows the black tube behind the phosphor. Idle, the tube is not dead — a faint raster crawls, a test card surfaces from the depth for a second about every twenty seconds, and every so often the whole raster folds into Paik's one line of light and goes out.

Phosphor. P39 — long-persistence yellow-green (black → #061a0c → #1a8a3a → #7dea9a → pale mint at white). Persistence is real: the previous frame decays under the new one, so motion smears and a dropout leaves an afterimage. The main scope keeps its neon; the two tubes are visibly different decades.

Mobile. Under 700 px the bank stacks — scope, bargraph, then the second set at full width on its strap (4:3, ≈ 326 × 245 px at 390 px wide). The tape and bolts stay; the sticker on the CRT-19 goes as it does today.
