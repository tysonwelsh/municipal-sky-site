the argument

There is one television on this machine and Paik would bend it rather than buy a second one. The signal is a visitation, not a channel: for the length of it the scope stops to listen. The spectrum bars slide toward the centre line and lose their height, the waveform flattens, and every trace of light on the tube gathers into a single horizontal line — Zen for TV — that holds, breathing, for most of a second. Then the line thickens, a picture is squashed inside it and rolling, the vertical hold catches on the second roll, and the frame stands up to full height still snowing. Loss reverses it: tear, roll, fold into the line, and the scope grows back out of the line as if it had never left. Nothing is added to the faceplate except the tally and the counter, so the whole cost of the option is the thing that makes it Paik: for five to fifteen seconds the visualizer is gone and the machine is a television.

phosphor

The scope's own cyan gun. No new tube means no new phosphor: the picture is drawn in the same trace the waveform is drawn in (black → deep teal → #16e0e0 → pale cyan-white), and the red and magenta guns that fringe the waveform stay misregistered as the picture's multipath ghost. A P39 green picture would announce a second, different tube inside the first; cyan says it is the same one, bent.

the counter: nixie, not seven-segment

The tally lamp is an NE-2 neon bulb and the two-digit channel counter is a nixie tube, so the whole retrofit is one gas — neon orange, a colour the machine did not have before, sitting between its red and its amber. Seven-segment is an LED and belongs to the bargraph's decade; a nixie is a tube like the CRT, and its stacked cathodes stay faintly visible behind the lit digit, which is the kind of imperfection this yard leaves in. The counter spins while the dial is turning and locks with the vertical hold. Audio-only reels (a platform, a numbers station) never lock: the counter hunts slowly through the hold, because there is no channel.

mobile

Nothing to do. The scope already stacks full-width under 700 px, the fold is the same gesture at 160 px tall, and the neon cluster is 95 px wide on the chin, which still fits beside the brand plate at 390 px. The sticker hides on mobile as it does on the live page.
