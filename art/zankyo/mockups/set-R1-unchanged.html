OPTION R1 · 無改造 THE UNCHANGED SET control experiment

The argument, in one paragraph. The screen is the event and the controls are a servant, so this option spends nothing on the casing and everything on making the five picture controls reachable with a thumb. The cabinet you are looking at is the live one, value for value — the same yellowed plastic, the same 11 px corner, the same 11/13 wall, the same tube radius and inner shadows, the same crack, the same piece of tape lifting at the right edge. The chin is the only thing that moved, and it moved 22 px: from 30 px of leftovers holding a 20 px knob and a 20 px switch, to a 55 px strip holding five 46 × 28 function buttons — each with a 44 px touch target around it — on a 66 px pitch, flush-mounted in the plastic with an even band of it between each and silk-screened underneath the way a late-70s set printed its function bank. Nothing is a bay, a rail, a shelf or a bank; there is no second housing and no new material. If this reads as well as the ambitious four did, it wins on cost alone — and it is the only option whose diff against the shipping CSS is a chin height, five buttons and a deletion.

What changed from the live casing — the whole list. (1) The chin becomes a strip: .zk-set2-chin at ≈ 30 px becomes .zk-set2-strip at 55 px (7 px top pad, a 28 px cap, 3 px, an 11 px print line, 6 px bottom pad — 44 × 27 caps under 430 px). Each cap is painted by a ::before inset 8 px top and bottom inside a 44 px-tall button that is pulled back into the strip with margin-block: -8px: the finger gets the 44 px minimum in both axes, the plastic does not move, and the strip does not grow. Same padding: 7px 6px 6px rhythm, same flex row, same plastic behind it. (2) Five buttons replace two controls: each one is the live .zk-push switch — its exact background, its exact two-ring black surround, its exact 1 px of travel, its exact lens well — widened to fill its cell and given one to four pips. (3) The 選局 TUNE knob is deleted. Its job on the live chin is "scan for a signal", which is what 受信 does; a 20 px knob is the worst thing on the panel to reach for on a phone, and the owner said so. Nothing is orphaned: 受信 is the same switch, at thumb size, and it carries the separate 7 px 受信 lamp's three states — cold, armed, receiving — in its own lens, so the lamp is folded in rather than dropped. That is the entire visual diff. One further change is invisible: the tube's 4:3 now comes from percentage padding rather than aspect-ratio, because WebKit ignores a ratio on a shrinkable flex item and that is how option C shipped a 488 × 274 tube (PLAN §9.1); .zk-set2-tubewrap is a plain block instead of a centring flex row so the zero-height child can live in it. The tube is the same 488 × 366 either way.

Zero rotaries. The brief allows one. This option uses none, and the defence is short: every variable here is a small set of named states — four brightness steps, three rasters, three phosphors, on and off. A control that cycles a named state and shows which one it is on is better than a knob in every way that matters on a phone, and it is also more honest to the machine: a late-70s set's function bank was buttons, and only the picture trims were knobs. The trims are behind the buttons now.
