SET D · 座 THE PEDESTAL SET (variable-first)

Argument. The other three options ask what kind of equipment the set is. This one asks what kind of object it is, and then hands you the four knobs that object actually had. It is TV Buddha, 1974: a spun-aluminium drum on a turned base, standing on the floor of the panel rather than screwed into it, throwing a shadow on the steel behind. Nothing bolts it down — the transport plate stays over with the scope, because you do not fasten the station's PLAY button to a sculpture. And because the cabinet is the whole of the object, the pedestal can carry a control bay that would look absurd on a chin: a quarter-round shelf tipped up toward you with four concentric dual knobs on it. Those knobs are the argument. 焦点 FOCUS sweeps the raster from 192 × 144 — the sharpest the archive holds — down to 48 × 36, where the picture is moving blocks of phosphor and nothing else; it turns the plan's §1 finding into the single most powerful control on the faceplate. 同期 HOLD knocks off its centre detent and the frame rolls, continuously, faster the further you go, with the blanking bar sweeping up the glass. That is Magnet TV with a knob instead of a magnet, and every television ever built had it. 残光 PERSISTENCE takes the phosphor from a fast P4 wipe to a smear where a hand leaves a comet trail. 輝度 BRIGHTNESS makes the other three legible. All nine controls — four outer shafts, four inner verniers, one tube-type strap — are wired to the real shader. None of them is a mock.

PHOSPHOR

Default P39, the live set's own LUT, unchanged: black → #0c4820 → #28985c → #78e292 → pale mint at white, gamma 1.3, and real persistence — the previous frame decays under the new one. The strap exists because the LUT is the cheapest place in the whole machine to change the picture's century: P4 makes it a 1955 monitor, P3 an airport departure board, P1 a radar repeater. The crack survives (pattern A, the upper-right impact, verbatim from zk-set.js) — a Paik set is junk by definition, and the refraction along the fracture is the one effect that reads better on an object than in a bezel.

IDLE

The tube is dark. No raster crawl, no test card, no retrace — the most restrained idle of the four. Every so often a single line of light opens across the glass, holds for a beat and closes: Zen for TV, 1963. It does not run on a timer of its own — it fires on the station's own clock, at the three places the jo-ha-kyū arc turns over (序→破 at 0.45, 破→急 at 0.82, and the fold back to 序), so the dark set is still telling you where the night is. Watch the 段階 bar: the line arrives when the bar crosses a tick.

MOBILE

At ≤ 700 px the pedestal loses its base (the plinth goes; the cabinet, collar and control bank stay, and the object still throws its shadow), and the quarter-round bank flattens into two rows of two knobs with the wafer and the paddle on a third row. The tube stays full width — ≈ 318 px at a 390 px phone, and sharper than today at that size, not bigger. Order is the owner's own: television, spectrum scope, 段階 progress bar, PLAY/STOP/volume, via display: contents plus explicit order.
