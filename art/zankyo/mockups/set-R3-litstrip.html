OPTION R3 · 灯列 THE LIT STRIP restrained

Argument. The screen is the event and everything else is a servant, so this option spends its whole design budget on making the servant disappear when it is not needed. There are no knobs — a knob on a phone is a thing you fight — and there is no bay, no bank and no rail: one shallow band — 87 px tall on the desktop, 71 on a phone — carrying a single row of five square pushbuttons behind smoked lenses. The row obeys a rule borrowed from broadcast machines: a lamp is on only when its key is doing something. Nominal — 輝度 at step 2, 焦点 at 鋭, 蛍光体 at P39, 常時 unlatched — is dark. So when nothing is happening, the strip is five black slots in a black band and the only lit thing on the entire faceplate is the glass; and when you do put the tube out of nominal, the band tells you at a glance, in the colour of the thing you changed. The keys carry the whole control language, so they are built like keys and not like pictures of keys: 3 px of real travel into a moulded slot, a lamp at the bottom edge of the cap that grazes up the face and dies before the top (an edge-lit lens, not a colour swap), a filament model in JavaScript that brings the lamp up over about 100 ms with a small flash of overshoot and lets it die over about 350 ms, and a legend silk-screened in a pale grey that is perfectly readable with the lamp out.

PHONE FIRST

The strip was laid out at 390 px and only then let out to 960. At 390 the frame gives the set 346 px, the cabinet's 9 px walls give the strip 310, and five keys at a 6 px gap come to 58 × 54 px each — square, dense, edge to edge, with no gap anywhere in the row and nothing wrapped, stacked or hidden. At 960 the same row relaxes to 86 × 64 and gains one silk-screened stamp. That is the entire difference between the two widths, and it is why the band does not become a field of blank plastic when the screen gets wide: the keys grow with it instead of sitting in a corner of it. Everything else at ≤ 700 follows the settled order — television → spectrum scope → 段階 progress bar → PLAY / STOP / volume — by dissolving the two column wrappers with display: contents and giving the four things an explicit order. The tube stays full width and gets sharper, not bigger.

IDLE

The tube is dark but never still: a faint raster crawls with a slow vignette and thermal sparks, the MSHI test card surfaces from the depth about every twenty seconds with the hold slipping, and every so often the whole raster folds into Paik's one line and goes out or shrinks to a dot. A signal arrives on its own about every 25 seconds — tune-in, hold, dropouts, loss — and 受信 brings one now. Through all of it, with the set nominal, the strip stays dark.

WHAT IS WEAK

Three things, declared. (1) 受信's tally. Its lamp sits half-lit while a picture is being received, which means the row is not completely dark for roughly a third of the night. It is honest — that is what a tally lamp is for — but it is a compromise against the brief's "the screen stays the only lit thing," and it would be one line to remove. (2) 粗 COARSE is genuinely ugly at 48 × 36 on a 488 px tube — it is Paik's mush, which is the point, but an owner who lands on it by accident will think the set broke. (3) The strip has no room to grow. A sixth control would either shrink the keys below a comfortable thumb at 390 px or force a second row, and a second row is the bay this round rejected. Five is not a starting point; it is the ceiling.
