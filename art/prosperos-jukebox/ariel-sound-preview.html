1. Dry Baseline
Current sound: two detuned sines, gain envelope, nothing else. This is what the chimes sound like right now.
2. + Reverb
A convolution reverb generated from filtered decaying noise. Simulates the sound existing in a physical space. Adjustable decay time and wet/dry mix.
DECAY 2.5s
WET MIX 40%
3. + Stereo Panning
Each note is randomly placed in the stereo field. Creates width and the sensation of chimes scattered across a space. Best heard with headphones.
4. + Inharmonic Partials
Real metal tubes produce overtones at non-integer ratios (2.76x, 5.4x the fundamental) rather than clean harmonics. This makes each note shimmer with a bell-like quality instead of sounding like a pure electronic tone.
PARTIALS 50%
5. + Attack Transient
A tiny burst of filtered noise (2-3ms) at the onset of each note, simulating the metallic "tick" of a striker hitting a chime tube. Gives each note a physical presence the ear can latch onto.
TRANSIENT 50%
