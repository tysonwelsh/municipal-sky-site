Each Library music-box ping drops a glint at its pitch. Four render styles are stacked as lanes (a ridgeline-style layout) and fire the same notes, so you can compare them directly. Pick a direction; we'll wire the winner into the spiral.

Height from sets what the ultimate height encodes: Loudness (per-note velocity — louder = higher; the spiral already shows pitch, so height carries loudness instead), Duration, or Fixed (random). Max rise is the ceiling that fraction scales; Height jitter adds variation; Spin/Shimmer tune the sparkle.
