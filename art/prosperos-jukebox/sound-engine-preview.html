0. Current Sound (Dry, Mono)
How each track sounds right now. No effects, no spatial processing, mono center. This is your reference point.
1. Reverb
A convolution reverb on the master output. Each track gets a different room character: Library = warm intimate study, Sycorax = dark cavernous cave, Ariel = open airy outdoors. This single change affects every sound.
35%
2. Stereo Panning
Individual notes and events are randomly placed in the stereo field. Drones stay centered for anchoring. Melodic and ambient events float left and right. Best with headphones.
70%
3. Waveshaping (Warmth / Grit)
A subtle waveshaping curve on the drone layers adds harmonic richness. Library gets soft-clip warmth (analog tape feel). Sycorax gets harder saturation (menacing overtones). Ariel gets gentle tube warmth.
40%
4. Delay / Echo
Short feedback delays on melodic and ambient events. Creates depth and space. Library = warm slapback on melody. Sycorax = dark trailing echoes. Ariel = bright ping-pong shimmer.
35% 320ms
5. Master Compression
A gentle compressor on the master output glues all layers together and prevents peaks from spiking. Makes the overall mix feel cohesive and polished rather than a collection of independent sounds.
6. Custom Waveforms (PeriodicWave)
Instead of basic sine/triangle/sawtooth, custom waveforms designed from harmonic coefficients. A "bell" wave for chimes, a "reed" wave for the hum, a "glass" wave for ghost tones. Each has the overtone fingerprint of a real material. Compare each pair: basic oscillator vs. custom wave.
