design notes ✦ mockup 3 · the celestial atlas

  • Engraving, not phosphor. No glow, no additive blending, no alpha fades anywhere on the plate. Depth on the rotating coil is carried by line weight + palette step (near limb: bright silver, 2px; far limb: deep-silver hairline) — the §3 ink-squash rule translated to metal. Fill under the FFT contour is engraver's hatching (radial burin strokes, density ∝ magnitude), the light-on-dark analog of the codex's Bayer ink-wash.
  • The frozen FFT is honest-fake: authored dB peaks (breeze root+fifth low, whistle at G5 under the plumb-bob, chime cluster aloft, aeolian mid) summed as gaussians over 7 octaves × 96 samples, then pushed through the real v1 projection (yaw/pitch, painter-ordered) with slow auto-rotation. Data positions are never grid-quantized; only textures and stamps snap to U=2px.
  • Chart furniture means the music: the two authored asterisms are the two Lydian homes (I printed quiet in old gilt, II lit bright — the current chord; the margin's chord panel is the same asterism repeated). The ♯4 is one lone gilt star that only the pale-sky halo rings — it lives in melody and halo, never in the pad. The dotted gilt flight-line climbs the coil leg by leg (scene legs), with paired wingbeat chevrons (answers) and last evening's line ghosted beneath in deep silver (the returning bird). The horizon rules the plate's foot and sinks through the release.
  • Plate texture is scratches + burnish, not stains: per-art-pixel value noise across the three plate blues, a diagonal burnish sweep, ~50 seeded 1U scratch strokes with occasional deep-silver glints — density reduced inside the baked "plate zone" where the coil lives so texture never fights hairlines.
  • Typography: VT323 everywhere; headers are letter-spaced small caps with gilt star interpuncts (✦) — engraved captions, no blackletter on this plate. Numerals always VT323.
  • Contrast: all three required pairs measured live in the dev box against the LIGHTEST plate tone (#22345a, worst case) — silver data ≥ 6.5:1, VT323 ≥ 6.5:1, gilt ≥ 5.9:1, all ≥ 4.5:1 with no palette adjustment needed. The deep silver #6e8aa4 measures ~3.4:1 and is therefore demoted by rule to depth-cue hairlines, hatch shading, and aged-ink log lines — it is never the sole carrier of a data mark.
  • Carries to implementation: L0 plate / L1 furniture (printed asterisms, cartouche, horizon, schema, border) / L2 data (coil, flight-line, plumb-bob) / L3 event gilding, per §6. The sea change re-gilds star points one by one toward the new tonic's angle; lift/float glyphs stamp at boundary t on the flight-line.
