design notes ✠ mockup 1 · the alchemical codex

  • What to look at first: the spiral. It is the real v1 geometry — 7 octaves × 96 samples, one turn per octave, honest 3D projection — rotating at ~1 rpm in front of its own parchment page (§3 primary treatment). Depth is carried by ink weight, never alpha: the near limb is dark iron-gall and slightly wider; the far limb is a pale hairline. The page never moves; only the ink does.
  • The toggle (top-right of the plate) switches to the §3 alternative: the inked object. The backdrop page vanishes from the plate region and the parchment maps into the coil itself — paper tone fills the coil’s quads from a world-space texture, and the contour’s ink darkens where it crosses fibers. A curled parchment strip spinning in the void. The margin keeps its own paper (bounded version — losing the page entirely would lose the apparatus’s home).
  • What is real: the parchment (all §1a layers, procedurally baked once at load on the U = 2 CSS px art-pixel grid — value-noise mottling, seeded blue-noise stains, 1U fiber streaks, Bayer-dithered radial edge darkening, deckled border); the 3D projection and depth sort (ported from v1’s spiral, seeds the eventual renderer); the Bayer ink-wash fill under the contour (screen-grid-aligned pattern tiles, chunky by design); the seven planetary-metal sigils as authored 12×12 art-pixel stamps on the in-key degree ticks of the base ring; the measured contrast numbers in the dev box (sampled from the baked pixels, not from the palette table).
  • What is fake: the FFT is a frozen, authored spectrum (drone C2–G2, hum F3, a harpsichord phrase, music-box pings at C6/E♭6) — plausible, static, honest in its mapping but not live audio. All margin data (tide 0.62, fire 0.38, chord IV, the genealogy, every log line) is frozen plausible telemetry per §4. Transport, seal, lamp, and mixer are dressed but not wired.
  • The margin apparatus (§4 Library column): Conjunctio folio heading with rubricated Jacquard 12 initial and its authored 16×16 operation emblem; the moon-phase tide volvelle; the athanor with the four degrees of fire; two quills aloft of an air-limit of three; chord IV with Venus’s copper sigil (IV of C Dorian is F — F is Venus in the §1a metal mapping, so the readout is self-consistent); the genealogy tree with roman-numeral generations, one dotted ancestor tether (gen IV → gen I), and the ghost branch in an earlier, paler hand; a ten-line scribal log with three steps of ink aging and a Transmutatio entry; the density footer — solid ink past, pricked pounce-hole forecast.
  • Chrome strip (§5): the shelf with Library lying open and the two idle spines keeping their own palettes; brass pushplates with 1U bevels and a gilt lit state (no glow); the wax-seal seed; the lamp wick as master volume; three legend rows — authored 12×12 voice sigil, VT323 name, dithered slider fill with an unquantized thumb, and v1’s pixel mute square kept verbatim.
  • Contrast (risk #1): see the dev box — all three measured ratios hold ≥ 4.5:1 against the worst-case (darkest-decile) baked paper in their zones. Two adjustments inside the palette’s spirit were needed: (1) the plate zone and the text columns are baked at reduced mottle amplitude — clamped to the two lightest paper tones — exactly the §6 “plate zone” rule, extended to wherever ink must carry data; the darker ramp steps live in the borders, stains, and deckle. (2) The rubric’s body-text duty always uses rubric-0 (#8e3b2c); rubric-1 (#b0553c) is reserved for aged tags and accents at display sizes. The far-limb hairline (ink-3) is deliberately below 4.5 — it is a depth cue, and the same datum returns at full contrast every half revolution.
  • Also per rulings: no CRT scanlines anywhere (§8 Q1); the paper is fully static — all motion is ink (§8 Q2); the page is genuinely parchment-light, floating on the void (§8 Q3); headers are Jacquard 12 (§8 Q4); the log stays text (§8 Q5). Out-of-key degrees are bare pale ticks — the in-key tinting relocated from color into symbol weight.
