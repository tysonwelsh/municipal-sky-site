Design notes — mockup 2 · grimoire (for the family record)

  • The woodcut inversion, resolved: on soot paper the ink mass cannot carry data (near-black on near-black), so the block prints in negative discipline — the ink band is the coil's body (visible as a darker-than-paper mass with rough squashed edges), and every data-bearing edge is a bone-white gouge: the FFT contour is the longest gouge on the block, relief hatching rides inside the band, and the baseline is a bone hairline. Bone carries all the data; ink carries only weight and depth.
  • The 25%-ink hush is a palette step, not an alpha fade: dropping bone marks to 25% alpha would sink them below 4.5:1, so the hushed print drops one locked ramp step (bone #d8cfc0 → #b3a68e, still 6.9:1) and quiet contour stretches thin to hairlines. The ink layer's visual floor is held at ~0.4 rather than the literal 0.25 — at a true quarter the block stops reading as a block on soot paper; the cutGain drop is encoded in the live/hush CONTRAST, not the absolute. The live voices — proto-drum bump (lowest turn) and the waterphone's six inharmonic spikes — keep full black ink and full bone, so the hush reads as the plate holding its breath around two living marks.
  • Witch-light is spent in exactly one place: the waterphone apparition's stamp (and its two log tags). Data-carrying witch-light uses the bright ramp step #c4b5fd (9.0:1); the dim step #9b87b8 (5.2:1) is reserved for its halo. v1's lavender survives as one accent, per §2.
  • Blood never carries data: #93392b is 2.3:1 on this paper — decorative rubrication only (the cut tag's field, the seal, sigil frames, the fire's glow). Everywhere blood appears, the adjacent glyph or text is bone.
  • Depth on dark paper: v1's alpha depth-fade dies here, so the far limb steps down the bone ramp (bone0 → bone2) and thins, per §3's ink-weight rule. The near limb also gets ink squash — the band bleeds sideways at amplitude peaks.
  • In-key tinting relocated into symbol weight (§3): the floor schema's 12 degree ticks — the seven sycorax-field degrees print long and bright with seated diamonds, the five foreign degrees are bare pale stubs. The keening notch at degree 1 is the schema's one double-cut tick.
  • Pose sigils are bind-runes with shared anatomy: all five stand on a center stave; coil wraps it, sting barbs and falls off it (the keen's ground), hollow opens an empty eye on it, veil drapes across it, smoke breaks it into drift. At 12×12 they survive down to the 36px margin stamp.
  • Frozen-data honesty: the spectrum is authored in dB (floor −75 / ceil −10) — quieted gurdy partials on the sting pose {0, 1, 5}, chant residue, proto-drum fundamental, six waterphone inharmonics — normalized by the same map the live engine will use, projected by v1's verbatim yaw/pitch quad pipeline, depth-sorted back to front. Nothing in the coil is a texture.
  • For the build: paper (L0) bakes once at art-pixel resolution with a quieted plate zone; the slash + scar + emblem belong on L3 (illustrations) with dither-mask decay; the pulse mark should beat with the real proto-drum. Open for the owner: should the slash heal on screen over the afterimage (scar fading in as the slash fades), and does the treeline strip want its fire to gutter with intensity or stay a steady station-mark?
