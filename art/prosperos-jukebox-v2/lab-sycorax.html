how this page works

The real rite, unmodified, plus five candidate voices for the register Sycorax does not have. Turn one on, let the evening run, and move the knobs while it plays — every knob is read at the moment the next sound is scheduled.

You never have to wait. "SOUND ONE NOW" plays the voice on the current pose immediately; presence ×3 makes it enter constantly; the hush chips leave the gurdy alone so you can hear the candidate naked against the seam.

The cut owns the room. No voice here may start from 4 s before the cut lands, through the hush and its return — the horn's law. Anything already in the air is killed at the cut itself, in 0.25 s. Stops — the same gestures in new clothes

how a stop works, and what a shadow cannot carry

A stop is a variant of a body the engine already owns. Switching one on mutes that engine layer and re-renders every note it plays through the variant here, at the engine's own times — its phrasing, new clothes. Switching it off unmutes the layer and stops the shadow. One stop per layer.

Notes arrive about a quarter-second before they sound, so the shadow lands where the engine's note would have. The notes a stop does not change are re-rendered through a copy of the engine's own body, so the layer keeps everything it had.

What a shadow cannot carry. The prototype pours into the bus through its own room, so it cannot reach the engine's private sends: the shadowed rebec and waterphone do not feed the seasick delay or the cave's throat, shadowed drum strokes do not excite the throat or pulse the trompette, and every shadow takes the page's room instead of the scene's room blend. A shadow also cannot read the engine's private darkening window, so a shadowed horn or gurdy does not close its filter the extra 15 % at an arrival. What a shadow does carry is the cut: the gurdy's and the horn's shadows go through the page's own duck, dipping and returning on the cut event's own numbers, because the engine ducks those two layers too. The percussion shadows and the cut's waterphone apparition stay open — the engine routes them around the dip, and the hush is theirs. Judge the STOP's colour here, not the layer's whole place in the cave.
