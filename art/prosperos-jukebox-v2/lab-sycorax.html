how this page works

The real rite, unmodified, plus five candidate voices for the register Sycorax does not have. Turn one on, let the evening run, and move the knobs while it plays — every knob is read at the moment the next sound is scheduled.

You never have to wait. "SOUND ONE NOW" plays the voice on the current pose immediately; presence ×3 makes it enter constantly; the hush chips leave the gurdy alone so you can hear the candidate naked against the seam.

The cut owns the room. No voice here may start from 4 s before the cut lands, through the hush and its return — the horn's law. Anything already in the air is killed at the cut itself, in 0.25 s. The line under the meter says where the cut is; while it says HUSH, even SOUND ONE NOW refuses, and that refusal is the feature. The hush belongs to the heartbeat and the apparition.

Each card shows its last two decisions. Tap KNOBS ▾ to open one instrument's knobs; opening one folds the others away.

Stops — the same rite, a different instrument

the test a stop has to pass, and what a shadow cannot carry

A stop replaces a body the engine already owns. Switching one on mutes that engine layer and re-renders every note it plays through the variant here, at the engine's own times — its phrasing, its notes, its loudness, a different instrument. ENGINE puts the layer back.

The test. These are not shades. A listener who knows the layer should name a DIFFERENT INSTRUMENT within two seconds: the wheel becomes voices, the horn becomes a shell, the cantor growls, the fen becomes a cave. Each variant carries one line naming the change so you know what to listen for. What a stop never changes is WHAT and WHEN — the engine still decides that — or HOW LOUD: A and B are matched to the engine's own layer within ±1.5 dB on the same phrase.

Notes arrive about a quarter-second before they sound, so the shadow lands where the engine's note would have. Every card has PLAY A PHRASE NOW: play it under ENGINE, then A, then B, on a hushed bus, and you have the comparison in six seconds. The row above the cards flips the whole rite at once.

The rite's laws still hold. The gurdy, the horn, the sky, the bullroarer, the overtone chant and the pot all sum into the engine's cutGain, so their shadows dip with the night on the cut event's own numbers. The engine ENDS the five (bullroarer, overtone, jaw harp, blade, cauldron) at the cut's tB, so their shadows are ended there too, in the same anchored 0.25 s. The PROTO-DRUM is rendered faithfully in all three positions on the percussion card — the hush keeps its heartbeat whatever the stop says. And the gurdy is the seam: every fade on that card is the cycle's own 3–5 s, so nothing there can startle.

What a shadow cannot carry. The prototype pours into the bus through its own room, so it cannot reach the engine's private sends: a shadowed rebec or waterphone does not feed the seasick delay or the cave's throat, shadowed drum strokes do not excite the throat or pulse the trompette, the gurdy's buzzing dog lives on the grit bus and is gone, and every shadow takes the page's room instead of the scene's room blend. A shadow also cannot read the engine's private darkening window, so a shadowed horn or gurdy does not close its filter the extra 15 % at an arrival. Judge the STOP's colour here, not the layer's whole place in the cave.
