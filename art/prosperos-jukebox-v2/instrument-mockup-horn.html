This page prototypes the Sycorax track's under-voice — a far low horn (aurochs horn / bronze lur: dark, breathy, ceremonial) that gives the hurdy-gurdy drone a fundament now and then. The full Sycorax engine plays unmodified; the horn is synthesized page-side from the engine's own live telemetry (pose, scene, intensity, boundary events) and fed into the engine's master bus through its own long dark reverb. It enters on the rite's movement — pose changes, the organum arrivals, the processional and invocation — never continuously, never a melody, and it falls hard-silent through the cut's hush. Judge it against the gurdy: darker, not louder, is the track's law.
