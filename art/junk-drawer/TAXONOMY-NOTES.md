# Taxonomy notes — presenting and sharing the rubric

The owner's working notes for explaining the Junk Drawer's grading taxonomy
to other people: exemplar responses that make an axis legible, framings that
land, caveats worth saying out loud. Repo-only (deploy excludes `.md`); this
is a notebook, not servable data. Add entries at the top of their section
with a date.

## Exemplars, by axis

### Layering

- **2026-08-30 — GPT-5.1's oil-painted eyeball (googly-eyeball rerun) is a
  great example of a CRITICAL failure on the Layering axis.** An otherwise
  great SVG that is poorly layered — and the filed ratings say exactly that:
  the owner's blind scores were top marks on every other axis
  (Understanding Assignment 4/4, Structural Coherence 3/3, Je ne sais quoi
  3/3, overall Select) against Layering 1/3, big problems. Useful when
  presenting because it shows the axes are genuinely independent: a drawing
  can grasp the brief, hold together structurally, and still fail purely on
  stacking order — which is why Layering earns its own axis instead of
  folding into coherence. (Item `2026-08-01-googly-eyeball`, rerun
