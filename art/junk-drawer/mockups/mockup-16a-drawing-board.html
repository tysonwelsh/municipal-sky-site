Mockup 16A — The Drafting Table

The darkroom as a square sheet of graph paper seen from above. Four drawing instruments work at scattered spots — a pencil sketching loops, an ink nib tracing a line, a compass sweeping an arc, a typewriter carriage ratcheting across — each keyed to a blind slot, A–D. Arrivals freeze mid-gesture with the stroke completed; a failure snaps the tool and scratches the work out in red. Self-demo: A lands at 3s, C at 6s, B at 9s, D fails at 12s, then holds. prefers-reduced-motion → no animation runs; pending is a static half-drawn state.
