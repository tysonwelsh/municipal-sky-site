Please Wait

The Junk Drawer · mockup 32 · the message, in 24 languages

The rain already says nothing in seventy-three scripts. Here it occasionally says one thing — that you are waiting — in whichever tongue comes up. Every one of these falls the way the rain already falls: down a column, a character to a square. Scripts that cannot be split that way — Arabic joins its letters, and Devanagari, Thai, Tamil, Bengali, Lao and Khmer reorder and reshape theirs — are left out rather than set sideways across the rain or, worse, split into text that is wrong.
