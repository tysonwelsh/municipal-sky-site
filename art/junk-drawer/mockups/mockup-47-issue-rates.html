    How it counts

    • Understanding Assignment has four levels. Mostly Understands counts as a small problem; Somewhat or Barely Understands counts as a big one.
    • Structural Coherence and Layering map directly: Small problems and Big problems.
    • Je ne sais quoi measures what goes right, so it is counted the other way: the hit rate is Has it plus Just a hint, with Has it in the dark segment.
    • The printed figure is the whole bar. The dark segment at the base is the severe (or best) share of it.
    • Whiskers are 95% Wilson intervals on the whole bar. At about 45 drawings a model they span roughly ±14 points, so bars whose whiskers overlap are not a real difference yet.
    • Every panel uses the same 0–100% ruler, so the categories can be compared with each other, not only within themselves.
