The Transport Bar

mockup 42 · replay as a strip under the print · 2026-09-16

The replay leaves the photograph and becomes a transport bar: a slim strip printed directly beneath the plate, the plate’s full width, outside the artwork — play/pause at the left, a scrub track ruled like the card’s own grade gauge in the middle, and a mono readout at the right (elapsed / total, with mark N of M beneath it, so a moment on the track names the element the pen is on). Drag the red-pencil caret, click anywhere on the track, or focus it and use ← → (Shift for coarse steps, Home/End, Space to play) to hold the drawing at any instant and read its layering. Inside the track a faint ink-density profile — how much drawing lands in each slice of time, from the same weights the engine schedules with — shows where the busy stretches are; the elapsed portion prints the same profile solid, so “how far” and “how busy” are one reading. It sits beneath the plate rather than in the .rc-plate-btns corner because the corner is on the print (the owner’s objection), because a scrub track is only useful at the plate’s full width and the print’s bottom band already belongs to the margin notes, and because a track the width of the photograph makes time read left-to-right across the picture it describes. DOWNLOAD SVG stays where it is on the print with the file number under it — it is a file operation, not a viewing control, and the row is one button shorter now. Trade-offs: the card grows about 40px taller (on a phone that is a real cost against the 52dvh plate clamp), and the bar keeps the engine’s inline animations parked instead of stripping them after the run, so the DOM is no longer “exactly what was rendered” while the card is open.

Notes for the build

  1. jd-record.js: plateBtnsHTML drops the .rc-draw button (keep .rc-dl); cardHTML appends a new transportHTML() after the plate inside .rc-col-l. A small shared module (JD_transport(svg, host), proposed for jd-core.js beside the engine) builds the bar, arms the animations and owns play/seek/readout; the card calls it on open and on every response flip, and calls its .destroy() (which strips the svg) on close. The click/key handlers’ .rc-draw exemptions become .mk-bar exemptions.
  2. jd-turn.js: plate() with opts.replay emits the bar after the .jd-turn-art div (before the figcaption) instead of the .jd-turn-draw--icon button; the delegated data-act="replay" handler goes, the module handles its own presses. SKETCH_ICON retires.
  3. junk-drawer.css: the .mk-bar block on this sheet, renamed (.jd-tbar), plus the two token maps (.jd-record-scrim .jd-tbar, .jd-turn-scrim .jd-tbar). Delete .rc-draw and .jd-turn-draw rules and their zoom-fig variants. The enlargement (.rc-zoom-fig) should carry the same bar in its bottom band in place of REDRAW — same module, its own svg.
  4. Engine contract: the bar relies on JD_drawOn’s inline animations being seekable through getAnimations(), and it parks them by setting svg.__jdDrawSeq = -1 so the strip timer skips. Cleaner: give JD_drawOn an opts.hold that suppresses the strip and returns the schedule ({secs, items:[{el,start,dur,w}]}) so the bar does not re-measure every element for the profile.
  5. Reduced motion: the bar always arms with force:true but does not autoplay under JD_reduced(); it parks at the end (drawing complete) and scrubbing is the explicit request. This sheet autoplays once on load otherwise, as the card does.
  6. Open questions: (a) Phone height — the bar adds ~40px under a plate clamped to 52dvh; accept, or shrink the plate clamp to 48dvh when the bar is present? (b) The mark count says “mark N of M” (elements begun); should it count elements finished instead, or name the element’s tag (“path 27”)? (c) Should DOWNLOAD SVG move to the bar’s right end on wide cards, leaving the print bare but for the notes and file number? (d) The profile is the engine’s weight per slice; a per-element tick row (one hairline per start time) is the more literal layering map — worth a variant?
