THE JUNK DRAWER — labelling the models on the grid (round 28)

Four ways to say “Model A” on the darkroom and Results frames, replacing the lone pencilled letter. Each card shows both faces — top row still drawing, bottom row arrived — since the label has to read on each. The models stay anonymous until the unveil, so the label is always the letter, never the name; whichever wins, the later cards’ copy (“grade drawing A”, “Drawing A”) would follow it to “Model A”.
