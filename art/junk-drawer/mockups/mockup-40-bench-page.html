49 prompts · 200 drawings in the drawer · 4 models on the panel

How it works

  1. 1A prompt goes to every model on the panel verbatim, one shot each.
  2. 2The SVG comes back and is filed as written. Nothing is cleaned up.
  3. 3Each drawing is graded blind on the rubric, then the set is ranked.
  4. 4The record keeps model, version, tokens and cost. Visitors take turns too.

How grading works

The author grades every drawing without knowing which model drew it; the names come off only after the grades and the ranking are filed. The rubric is versioned. When an axis is retired its old scores stay on the record, marked as such, and are never surveyed again.

Visitor turns are graded on the same rubric by the visitor and stored separately from the author’s grades. The two populations are never pooled without saying so.

Why this exists

The author runs evaluations like this for a living, under NDA. This is the public version of that discipline: an instrument, a rubric, and a record anyone can inspect. About the author →

Limitations

One rater. A small visitor sample. Drawing an SVG is one narrow capability, not a verdict on a model.
