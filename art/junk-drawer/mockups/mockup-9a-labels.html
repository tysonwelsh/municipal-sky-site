Junk Drawer · Mockup 9A · Label Tasting

The owner picked 9A — the Credit Window but not its placeholder wording. Every tile below is the real 9a asset — same lens, same backlight, same press — with only the printed lines changed, so what is being tasted is the label AS IT WOULD SHIP: two silhouette lines under the credit bars, width-pinned to the lens. All amber; colour is a separate decision. Every tile presses.

NOTES — line two carries the label (it is the big line); candidates were chosen so line two stays a short fat word — JUNK, PROMPT, DRAWER, THING, PLAY, TURN, PILE. Six-letter second lines (PROMPT, DRAWER) drop to a smaller size to keep the letterforms from crushing; they are the thinnest at drawer scale. The recommendation is A · PUSH FOR JUNK — it borrows the exact grammar of the reference hardware (“PUSH FOR CREDIT”), says press-me in words as well as light, and lands the site’s register.
