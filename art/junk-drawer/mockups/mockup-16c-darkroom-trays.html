Junk Drawer · Mockup 16C · The Darkroom

The §2 waiting card, re-lit: a square, near-black card under a breathing red safelight. Four developing trays sit in a loose arc — A rocks, B is stirred by tongs, C hangs dripping from a clip line, D just deepens. Each print's ghost image blooms up while its machine is still drawing; on arrival the print lifts, a clip bites, and the caption ticks to ✓ arrived; on failure the print fogs black — ✗ didn't survive in ink red. Slot letters are chinagraph rings on the prints, so they survive the fog. Self-demo: A lands ~3s, B ~6s, C ~9s, D fogs at ~12s, then holds. Replay is the small control in the card's bottom corner. Reduced-motion users get static half-developed prints and instant state flips.
