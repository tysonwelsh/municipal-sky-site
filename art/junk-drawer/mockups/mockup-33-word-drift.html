Word Drift

The Junk Drawer · mockup 33 · english only

No character pool, no other scripts, nothing generated. Every letter on the sheet belongs to a word — a column is a word, spelled downward one letter to a printed square. The letters fall, strike the bottom rule, are thrown, and come to rest in a heap. For a wait indicator the heap is the useful part: its depth is how long you have been waiting.
