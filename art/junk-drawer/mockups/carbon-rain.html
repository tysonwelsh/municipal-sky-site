The Carbon Rain

The Junk Drawer · one wait indicator · round G · 78 scripts, 5987 characters · built the way the app builds

Same rain, rebuilt on the mechanism the app already uses. Nothing repeats. A stream fires animationiteration at the instant it wraps — the one moment every square in it is off the sheet — so that is where its characters are drawn again, from all 5987 of them across 78 writing systems. The change is never seen and the supply never runs out. The word a stream carries is re-drawn with it, so no word is welded into one column for the length of a wait. About one square in fourteen turns on its own centre as it falls, and a few squares a second re-strike in plain view.

Construction. Every glyph occupies one 9×9 cell, so characters sit inside printed squares and columns fall down printed rules. Each script carries its own font-size — measured, not guessed — so Cherokee at 8.9px, Deseret at 9.2px, Devanagari at 8.9px and Chinese at 6px all land on the same advance as Latin at 10px. The inventory is generated, not typed: each script enumerated from its full Unicode block, then sieved by ink density, shape-tested for missing-glyph boxes, filtered against unicodedata, de-duplicated, and checked for bitmap-identical glyphs. Ink weight is set once per column and never varies down its length. Reversal applies only to scripts a reader recognises and only to asymmetric glyphs. Motion is one transform per stream; the only per-square animation is the pinwheel. Under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce the streams never animate, so animationiteration never fires and the sheet holds the page of type it was first dealt.
