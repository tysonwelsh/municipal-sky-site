THE JUNK DRAWER — the darkroom's wait slip goes broadcast (round 26)

CLAUDE'S TAKE

Three replacements for the pencilled “please wait…” slip laid over the four swatches — each one a PLEASE STAND BY card in the manner of the television test patterns, printed on the office's own paper. The swatches, the indicator deal, and the arrival states are untouched; only the centre slip changes. The slow-timer line keeps its seat under the slip's main matter in all three.
