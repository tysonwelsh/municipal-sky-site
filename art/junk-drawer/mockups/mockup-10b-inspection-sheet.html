Notes · what I committed to, what implementation must watch

1 · The premise carries the chrome, never the interaction. The survey is a paper form because the taxonomy already talks like one — the stamps, punch boxes, CONDEMNED and FILED are all fiction laid over completely ordinary controls: every scale is a native <input type="radio"> group inside a role="radiogroup" (Tab enters, arrows move, exactly the semantics production’s pills have today), every option is a ≥44px label, focus rings are real (:focus-within on the chip, as production does), and selection is never colour alone — the stamp gains its rotation + double-ring emboss, the punch box gains its ✕, the n/a slot its clerk’s dash. Everything renders from a JS const holding the taxonomy.json subset verbatim (grades + the five live axes; defunct axes excluded), so production wires this to the payload exactly as ratePanel() does now. Titles only, everywhere: the retired pill-row’s inline descriptions are gone.

2 · One footnote system — the a11y contract. Three doors, one set of definitions, no competing UIs: (a) hover, on hover-capable fine pointers only, shows a transient tooltip for THAT option; (b) keyboard focus shows the same tooltip (a modality flag distinguishes keyboard focus from the focus a tap causes, so a plain tap only ever selects); (c) the superscript footnote mark — the explicit touch affordance, its hit area padded to ~44px by a ::before — is a real button (aria-expanded/aria-controls) that unfolds the whole scale’s definitions as an IN-FLOW typed slip under the line: it pushes the form down rather than covering it, so it can never block rating; Esc or a tap outside folds it, one slip open at a time. Screen readers don’t depend on any of that: each radio carries aria-describedby to a hidden definition registry, so the definition is announced with the option natively; the visual tooltip stays aria-hidden. Definition text sits in data-def on the chip — no duplication drift.

3 · The comparison is now a five-stop Likert — contract addition, flagged not invented. Wording: A decisively better · A slightly better · dead even · B slightly better · B decisively better, A anchored left and B right on the comparator and in the chip row, both exhibits visible as plates beside the gauge. Filing would extend C1.3’s comparison object to {"winner": "a"|"b"|"tie", "strength": "decisive"|"slight"|null} (strength null exactly when winner is tie) — winner keeps its exact current meaning so existing rows and the required-before-filing rule are untouched; strength is one new nullable column. The office-use panel at the sheet’s foot prints this payload live. The needle is SVG drawn on the page (no images), rotates via transform-box: view-box, and is aria-hidden apparatus — the chips are the instrument.

4 · Blank-form economy. Per-axis remarks collapse behind a small “remarks —” disclosure so ten empty ruled lines never dominate the sheet; the CONDEMNED write-in (“this response is broken or offensive” keeps the current wording) reveals the same way. Implementation must keep production’s mutate-in-place discipline here: rate is the one long scrolling state, so slips, remarks and the flag line must toggle hidden on nodes already in the DOM — never re-render the state — or the visitor is thrown back to the top of the survey (the comment above ratePanel() is the precedent).

5 · Motion and stamps. The stamp thunk, CONDEMNED slam, FILED hit and the needle swing are the only motion; all are killed under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce (the still form is the complete form — the inked pose, not the landing, carries the state). The FILED stamp is page fiction: production files ONE batch to jd-rate.php and proceeds to unveil; nothing else on this sheet changes that flow, and the single-exhibit degraded turn simply omits item 2 and replaces item 3 with a printed “no comparison — sole exhibit” line (comparison: null stays legal there, per contract).

6 · Floors and namespacing. Nothing here needs more than the iOS 16 floor: no popover attribute, no anchor positioning, no :has() — the tooltip is one fixed-position element placed by script, the slips are plain in-flow toggles. The exhibits are minted from templates with ids rewritten per copy (id=, url(#…), href="#…"), because both real SVGs use colliding ids (grain, glass…) — production already owns this via JD_svgInst.

7 · The mobile pass (2026-08-11) — a 375×667 baseline with a 320 floor, touch-first, and one mechanism: the card is a size container (container: sheetcard / inline-size, Safari/iOS 16+, same floor as everything else here) and every phone rule is an @container rule on the card’s width — which is why the stage-width toggle above the instrument shows the genuine phone form on a desk, and why production should hang the container on .jd-turn itself (the scrim stays viewport business). What changes under 560: sheet padding slims and the ✕ clearance narrows so content gets the pixels; the five-across stamp shelf becomes a 3-across rack (2-across under 350) so no grade ever orphan-wraps; punch boxes fall into two labelled columns of one 44px row each; the exhibit plates trim to ~230px so an exhibit plus its grade line fits a 667px screen together; the comparator keeps its needle by growing — the gauge goes full-width above the two thumb plates (it renders LARGER than the desk inline version, so the engraving and the A/B anchors stay legible) and the five Likert stops stack as full-width 48px rows, A-top → B-bottom with 8px mis-tap gaps — direction is carried by the words and by the gauge directly above, and FILE THIS REPORT was already full-width.

7a · The touch definition contract. On a phone there is no hover and no keyboard, so of note 2’s three doors only the footnote mark stands — it therefore stops being a superscript with invisible padding and becomes a true 44×44 inline-flex button with a bigger glyph, sitting clear of the option row beneath (no overlapping ghost hit areas to steal a stamp’s top edge). The slips it unfolds move to ≥12px type at phone widths (0.75rem — small print as fiction, not as hardship), stay in-flow so they never cover an option, and now fold on a completed outside click rather than pointerdown, because on touch a pointerdown is how a scroll starts — a visitor scrolling while reading a slip must not have it fold under their thumb. Screen readers still get every definition via aria-describedby; tooltips remain a desk-only echo.

7b · What implementation must watch on real phones. (i) Write-ins are 16px at phone widths — via the container rule AND a max-width: 768px media mirror of production’s .jd-turn-input guard — so iOS never focus-zooms the fixed modal half off-screen; production must keep that guard on these fields. (ii) The real modal scrolls internally (.jd-turn-scroll): verify on-device that focusing a remarks write-in scrolls it above the keyboard inside that region (the mockup’s page-flow staging can’t prove this; visualViewport is the fallback if WebKit misbehaves). (iii) The banner band: production’s scrim already reserves --banner-h under html.jd-chrome — nothing on this sheet may re-solve that. (iv) The stamp/condemn rotation transforms overhang a few px at grid edges; sheet padding absorbs them, so don’t slim it below 10px. (v) No horizontal overflow exists down to a 304px card (320 stage minus scrim padding) — keep it that way: any new form line must wrap into the two-column punch grid or stack, never scroll sideways.
