Design Notes · 12B

The move. Today’s tag is a luggage label; this is the drawer’s paperwork. Cream card stock (#f2ead0, one shade lighter than the app’s manila #e8d9a8, as catalog cards are against their envelopes), the classic doubled red top rule, faded blue ruling at a fixed --acc-lh: 1.2rem pitch, a pre-printed masthead, and typed entry lines. The blue ruling is the one non-ink hue on the card — period-correct office stock, kept at 0.34 alpha so it reads as paper, not as UI.

The stamp is the verdict. The grade block is a rubber stamp set 2° off square, inked in the rank’s own color (the same five-color table the gauge already uses), with the segmented bar gauge riding inside it as the calibration strip — gauge dividers switched from manila to this card’s paper color #f2ead0 so they read over the fill. One ink per card: the stamp word, border, tint, and gauge all agree. UNGRADED swaps the stamp for a square, dashed, pencil-weight placeholder box (no rotation, dim ink, hollow gauge at 0.55/0.3 ink alphas) — a slot the curator hasn’t stamped yet.

Anchor unchanged. The punched hole + reinforcement ring sits at the left edge’s vertical center — the exact anchor of today’s grommet, so pick()’s seat/rope math is untouched. (The catalog-authentic spot would be bottom-center, where the drawer rod goes; not worth re-deriving the elastic for.) The hole is painted, not cut: a radial-gradient punch + ring on one absolutely positioned span, ~17px from the left edge to its center vs today’s ~9.5px — the rope endpoint constant in junk-drawer.js needs that one-number nudge. A real cutout would need mask/clip-path: path() and buys little at 22px.

Steady height, downward growth. The SPECIMEN line keeps production’s exact clamp mechanics: two lines reserved (min-height: 2×--acc-lh), -webkit-line-clamp: 2. MODEL stays breakable (the long refine label wraps); FILED’s date is white-space: nowrap. The foot is flex-wrap: on the 17rem tier the two buttons drop to their own row under the stamp, and the card only ever grows downward — pick() already measures tag height at runtime, so nothing else changes.

Integration notes for the real build. (1) .jd-itemtag loses its clip-path polygon and gains border-radius: 7px — the drop-shadow filter works the same; keep cursor: grab, touch-action: none, buttons the only pointer targets (they sit at a higher z-index than the grain overlay, which is pointer-events: none). (2) The grommet ::before becomes the hole span’s gradient recipe; the elastic stub here is page dressing — production keeps its Verlet rope, re-aimed at x≈17px. (3) meterSVG() is reused verbatim except the divider stroke constant (#e8d9a8 → #f2ead0); the tag build also stamps a g1…g5/pending class on the stamp box from the same rank it already reads — one code path, same ranked switch. (4) The masthead and field labels (“MODEL”, “FILED”, “GRADE”) are static pre-printed chrome in the template string; all specimen values stay escaped plain text exactly as today. The masthead is white-space: nowrap; overflow: hidden with an ellipsis fallback, and the 17rem tier shortens it (drop the “Junk Drawer ·” prefix, keep “Municipal Collection”) in the same ≤768px block that sets --jd-tag-w. (5) If typed lines are added or moved, keep every body line-height and vertical margin a multiple of --acc-lh or the type drifts off the ruling; the ruling phase lives in one background-position value on .bd.
