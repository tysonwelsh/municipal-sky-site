Design notes — 13B “Form 27-B”

The idea

The old card was two fictions stapled together: office paperwork above and below, a neon exhibition plate in the middle. 13B commits to one: everything is Form 27-B, a single sheet of cream form paper. The artwork stops being a stage and becomes evidence — a photograph attached to the record. The display container is a photo-lab print backing: paper a shade lighter than the form (#f8f3e2), ruled with a faint engineering grid in a pale blueprint dilution of the form ink (rgba(74,98,138, .17/.07), 45px majors over 9px minors) — graph paper, not neon. Four kraft photo corners hold the print; a small drop shadow (0 4px 10px -3px) floats it a millimetre off the sheet, and a faint rotated FILED stamp (opacity .30, mix-blend-mode: multiply) overlaps its lower corner. That shadow and stamp are the only depth cues — no dark plate anywhere, bold in character, restrained in palette.

Structure (all shipped content, nothing added)

  • Header band: FORM 27-B box · department line · REV — then the specimen title as the form’s subject of record (letterspaced serif caps on a rule).
  • MODEL / PROMPTED / PROCESS / SIZE as one row of four ruled fill-in boxes, label printed in the box corner, value typed (2×2 on phones).
  • Sec. 1 — the photograph (the enlarge handle, role="button", ⤢ hint bottom-right, hint ink-coloured now that the ground is paper).
  • Sec. 2 — the prompt, verbatim, mono, quoted, with the fold (below).
  • Sec. 3 — annotations table, Axis 47% / Verdict 53%, 3-segment gauges + hand-inked verdict words in the earned pencil colours; OVERALL GRADE footer row with the 5-segment gauge.
  • Sec. 4 — other models, same prompt (hidden when there is one response; 2-up visitor variant in demo 3). Compact evidence chips — thumbnail left, model label + coloured grade word right — one ~38px row instead of the old two-deck stack (vertical room is the game). Phones re-stack the chips vertically.
  • Foot: Download SVG ⤓ · No. <id>. Close ✕ absolute top-right, never scrolls away.

How the fit contract is met (the reason round 13 exists)

  • Scrim reserves the banner: .jd-record-scrim { top: var(--banner-h) } (56px) instead of inset: 0. The banner can no longer draw over the title or ✕.
  • Card cap: the scrim keeps padding: clamp(4px, 1.2vh, 14px) 14px and the card is max-height: 100% of it with box-sizing: border-box — i.e. calc(100dvh − 56px − 2×pad). Use dvh in production so phone URL bars are honest.
  • vh-aware photo box: height: clamp(200px, 34vh, 340px) replaces the width-driven aspect-ratio: 10/9 plate. At 680px viewports the photo is ~231px (was ~465px) and the artwork letterboxes inside it; at tall desktops it stops at 340px.
  • The fold: the prompt <p> clamps at 3 lines (-webkit-line-clamp: 3); a hidden checkbox + label in the Sec. 2 head row (“show full prompt ▾ / fold prompt ▴”) releases it. No nested scroller — the old max-height: 7.4em; overflow-y: auto box is gone. When expanded, the card’s ONE scroller absorbs the excess (demo 2, second frame).
  • Result: at 1280×680 the whole form — header to footer — is visible with no internal scrollbar for a normal prompt, three-model strip included (verified by headless screenshots at 1280×800 and 1280×680; note headless --window-size=1280,680 actually yields a 593px inner viewport — ~87px goes to phantom browser UI — so the fit was proven at 593px usable, i.e. with margin to spare on a real 680px screen).

Integration watch-outs

  • Expander is pure CSS (checkbox-label, no JS): cardHTML should emit the checkbox + label only when the prompt overflows 3 lines — measure after render (p.scrollHeight > p.clientHeight) or approximate by character count (>≈230 chars at desktop width). A label on a never-overflowing prompt is a dead control.
  • The checkbox must precede the Sec. 2 head in DOM order — the CSS uses the general sibling combinator (.rc-fold:checked ~ …).
  • floorSVG() is orphaned in the card, but the ENLARGEMENT layer (.jd-record-zoom, a body child above the banner) still uses it — decide whether the zoom keeps the vaporwave floor or gets a big graph-paper mount to match. This mockup assumes the zoom is restyled to match, but did not redesign it.
  • Hint/exhibit colours flipped from paper-on-black to ink-on-paper — the shipped .rc-plate-hint colour values don’t survive the move.
  • Stamp + corners are decoration: aria-hidden, pointer-events: none (corners sit inside the role="button" mount, so clicks still enlarge).
  • Alt-strip caption is one line (model + grade, ellipsized) to buy vertical room; on phones it re-wraps to two lines by class — in production keep that inside the existing @media (max-width: 480px) block.
  • Mockup vs production geometry: frames here use position: absolute + 100vh sections to fake viewports; production keeps position: fixed and 100dvh. The phone demo is a 390px class-based frame (headless Chromium refuses sub-500px windows), mirroring the 480px media query.
  • Type sizes tightened (~0.44–0.62rem across the furniture) to buy the fit — worth an owner squint on a real monitor before adoption.
  • The table-strut trap: the annotation rows only got short after (a) td { line-height: 1 } and (b) making .rc-verdict a block-level flex with height: 12px; overflow: visible — an inline-level verdict span lets the td's inherited 1rem strut set every row to ~21px, and the hand font's fallback metrics make it worse. Keep both when porting or the card silently grows ~25px.
  • Grain/mottle data-URIs are carried over verbatim (stitchTiles="stitch" — never reseed).
