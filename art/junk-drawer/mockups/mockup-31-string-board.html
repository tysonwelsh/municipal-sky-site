junk drawer — rank the drawings: THE STRING BOARD (round 31)

Designer's note. Four plain outlined ranks down the left, the drawings down the right as prints with their names pencilled underneath, and the answer is the drawer's own red elastic tied straight from a rank's edge to a print's — it crosses freely, because the crossing IS the answer. Click a rank or click a drawing, in either order, or drag the string across by hand; two strings on one rank is a tie, 1st takes exactly one, and a skipped rank simply closes up when the call is filed. Three survivors prints three ranks and two prints two, and since the board is a fixed grid rather than a growing stack the whole call fits a phone without scrolling at any count.
