Mockup 7e · The Catalog Card — PLAN-RECORD §3 E

The record as a Library of Congress printed card. The model is the author — main entry heading, entry element in caps (CLAUDE OPUS 5, artist.). The verbatim prompt is the title statement, set with the classic hanging indention (first line at the second indention, runovers back to the first) and closed with the statement of responsibility / drawn by <model>. — never truncated, it simply wraps. Then imprint ([s.l.] : Anthropic, 2026.), collation (1 art reproduction : SVG, col.), the typed notes, and the tracings at the foot — arabic numerals for subject headings, roman for added entries, derived from the title, the tags and the generation mode.

The rubric, compressed — E squeezes hardest, which is honest to the genre: the four axis verdicts become one typed Condition: note (“prompt fidelity, partial; geometry, minor; color, clean…”), with a skipped axis typed as “not assessed”. The overall grade is the librarian’s pencil, circled at the top right with the graded date beneath; the taxonomy’s own description of that grade is typed as the last note. A regrade shows as an eraser ghost — the superseded circle rubbed out but still legible under the new one. Every grade label, grade description, axis label and value label on this page renders from the baked taxonomy.json object; nothing is hardcoded, and axes the taxonomy grows later render generically.

The card’s furniture — cream 3×5 stock, light foxing, a penciled call number in the left margin (derived from the id: JD-27 / a Cutter number off the subject word / the year), and the punched rod hole at bottom centre with the drawer’s brass rod showing through it and a wear halo rubbed into the stock around it. The card sits proud of a few neighbouring card edges — the drawer implied, never built.

The flip-through — the strip is the drawer. The other responses to the same prompt are the cards filed behind, each showing its author heading (and penciled grade) on its peeking top edge, under a guide card whose tab reads FILED UNDER THE SAME TITLE. Clicking one riffles it forward with a paper-shuffle and swaps the lifted SVG in place at the same scale; the title statement — the prompt — is identical on every card, because they are all the same title. Hairball has a single response: no cards behind, no guide tab.

TWO SYNTHETIC OVERLAYS (nothing in items/ carries these states yet; both are stated here and never on the card itself). (1) Subway rat r1 is given a mock grade_history entry — {grade:"choice", date:"2026-07-27", taxonomy_version:4} — so the eraser-ghost supersession renders. (2) Subway rat r3’s color axis is treated as skipped, so the condition note types “color, not assessed”. Everything else — prompts, notes, grades, annotations, models, the artwork — is baked verbatim from items/ and taxonomy.json.

Mobile — at ≤768px the card is the PLAN-MOBILE bottom sheet: lifted SVG in the top 45svh, the card rising out of the drawer below it with the filed-behind edges peeking above its top edge. Peek shows the call number, the author heading, the whole title statement and the process note; tapping the handle expands to full, which adds imprint, collation, condition, provenance, the grade description and the tracings in an inner scroll region (overscroll-behavior: contain). The rod hole stays pinned at the card’s bottom centre in both detents. ?mock-state=peek|full drives the detent (that is how the verification screenshots are taken); ?item=<id> picks the item.

Deviation — the card runs 552px wide rather than the contract’s 420–460px. Below ~520px the typed measure drops under ~60 characters and the record needs more typed lines than a 3×5 can hold at legible size. The 3×5 proportion is enforced as a floor (min-height), so a short record — hairball — sits at exactly 12.5 : 7.5; a long one grows taller, the way a real card overflows onto a second card.
