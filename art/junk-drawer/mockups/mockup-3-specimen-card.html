Mockup 3 · The Specimen Card — the two-step annotation display

TWO STEPS (owner-decided). Step 1 — the scorecard: clicking an item lifts it (the production lift grammar) and the familiar pick-slip at the drawer’s front edge grows into a grading slip — visibly the same paper object, unfolded in place: title · model chip · process chip (ONE-SHOT / REFINED ×N), the overall grade as a red-pencil stamp, the four axis verdicts as compact chips (axes the record skips render muted as — · NOT ASSESSED), and the alternatives strip. No notes at this stage. Clicking another item swaps the slip; clicking wood or Esc folds it back to nothing. Step 2 — the full record: FULL RECORD → (or clicking the lifted item again) opens the annotation sheet over a ~40% scrim — lifted SVG large at left, paper record at right: header, the verbatim prompt (scrollable past ~8 lines), the full rubric with the annotator’s note under each verdict, the overall grade stamped large with its taxonomy description, curator notes, and the alternatives strip again with flip-through (the displayed SVG swaps in place). ✕ / Esc / scrim returns to the scorecard — the selection survives. Every rubric string on this page renders from the baked taxonomy.json object; nothing is hardcoded.

GATE G3 — grade register: Stamps are hand-circled red-pencil marks — two offset open passes with a touch of turbulence displacement, deliberately not vector-perfect circles. Typed is plain mono red, no rings. The toggle re-registers the scorecard and the full record at once.

MOBILE — this same two-step is the bottom sheet of PLAN-MOBILE §4: the scorecard is the peek detent (232px + safe-area — header, overall stamp, alternatives strip) and the full record is the expanded sheet; dragging between detents replaces the button. Nothing to build here — the correspondence is the point.

ALTERNATIVES — no real alternatives are filed yet, so the skeleton key carries two clearly-marked mock thumbnails (“mock — for layout”) beside its primary; flipping to one swaps in a labeled placeholder. The mock records also carry deliberately skipped axes so the NOT ASSESSED rendering is demonstrable. Scissors and rubber band show the empty-strip state.

DEEP LINKS — in production, #<id> and the inventory’s item links open step 2 directly (the scorecard is the in-drawer route; the record is the shared artifact). This mockup honours #2026-07-26-skeleton-key and ?mock-state=score|full (+&g3=typed) the same way — which is also how the verification screenshots drive it.
