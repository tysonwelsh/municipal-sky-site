The failure phrase is per-code (FAIL_WORDS, junk-drawer.js l. 4071): the machine didn’t answer (provider_failed) · the drawer refused the drawing (sanitizer_rejected) · lost on the wire (network) · out of turns for now (rate_limited) · the drawer is resting (drawer_resting) · already at work (slot_in_progress) · anything else: came back blank.

Mobile

a 390px device frame · the live ≤768px scrim rule and ≤600px masthead rules re-stated in scope · a second turn’s deal, seeded “mockup-37-phone” ·

There is no darkroom-specific breakpoint: the card is width: min(560px, 100%, calc(100vh − 110px)); aspect-ratio: 1/1 and every swatch, well and foot is %-sized, so a phone simply gets a smaller square. What DOES change at phone widths: the scrim pads 10px/8px and top-aligns the card (≤768px), and the PLEASE STAND BY band steps down to 0.8rem/0.16em tracking (≤600px). The scatterword resizes via container units (7.893cqw), the drift measures its well at mount, and the mesh re-deals itself on resize.

Fidelity notes · all darkroom CSS/JS verbatim (build-assembled) except: (1) the word-mesh iframe loads /art/kimis-take/mini.php live — here the same document (kimis-take.css + kimis-take.js + mini.php’s bootstrap, params words=1 n=32 stubborn=100 speed 2.8–5) is inlined via srcdoc so the file needs no server; (2) webfonts are not fetched (no external requests) — Courier Prime falls back to Courier New; Iowan Old Style / Snell Roundhand / Bradley Hand are system faces on macOS; (3) the phone frame re-states the phone media rules in scope, and pins out the card’s 100vh term, which would read the browser viewport; (4) specimen swatches are re-seated at fixed size and their model labels hidden. The ✕ on the cards is inert here. Reduced-motion honours the live rules: with “reduce motion” on, every swatch shows its static base pose.
