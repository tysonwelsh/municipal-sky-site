(report-card / alternative-flip candidate)

Draw time 3.0s
WHAT THIS IS: the social video's stroke-draw technique running live on the real SVG DOM — no video file involved. Stroked paths draw themselves over their measured length (in document order — the order the model emitted them); filled shapes fade in; pre-dashed strokes and unstrokeable elements fall back to a fade.

WHERE IT COULD LIVE IN THE DRAWER: (1) when a report card's photograph enlarges — one item, full attention, cost is one-time; (2) when flipping to an alternative response — the incoming specimen draws on. NOT recommended for the initial pile load: dozens of SVGs animating at once is expensive and reads as noise, and the pile's charm is walking in on the mess already in progress.

GUARDRAILS FOR REAL INTEGRATION: honor prefers-reduced-motion (this page does — it skips straight to the finished art); measure lengths once per SVG and cache; strip the inline animation styles after the run so the art returns to its untouched state.
