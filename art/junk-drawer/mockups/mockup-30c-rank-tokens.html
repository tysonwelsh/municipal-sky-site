JUNK DRAWER — RANK THE DRAWINGS: THE LOT NUMBERS (round 30, option C)

Designer's note. The prints never move and never shrink — the artwork stays big enough to actually judge, which on a phone means no thumbnails and no squinting. What moves is a small gummed lot number: drag a 1, 2, 3 or 4 out of the tray and press it onto a print's corner. There is exactly one 1 slip in the drawer, so first place can't be shared — the 2s, 3s and 4s come in pads, which is how ties happen.

Ranks are filed dense, and the ledger says so out loud rather than quietly rewriting the visitor: write 1, 3, 3, 4 and it prints the filed row 1, 2, 2, 3 with one line of plain English. Fewer survivors never shrinks a print — three keeps the same 2-up measure with the odd one centred beneath the pair and pulls the 4 pad off the tray; two leaves a single row and a tray holding only the 1 and a pad of 2s, at which point the unique-1 rule alone makes it the old better/worse call, with a tie still impossible at the top.
