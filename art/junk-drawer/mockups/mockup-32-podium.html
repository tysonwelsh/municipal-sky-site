junk drawer — rank the drawings: the podium (round 32)

Designer's note. Four steps, tallest at the left, each one rank lower than the last — the height alone carries the order, so the only words on the card are the ordinals and the button. The drawings wait in a row underneath and are dragged (or tapped) up onto a step; dropping one on a taken step swaps the two, so a visitor can reorder without ever having to clear a place first. Three survivors builds three steps and two builds two. One print per step here — ties would need a second print standing beside the first, which this shape can't hold at phone width, so say if ties still matter and I'll fit them.
