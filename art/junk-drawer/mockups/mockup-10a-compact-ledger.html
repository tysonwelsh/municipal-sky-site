Junk Drawer · Mockup 10A · The Compact Ledger

10A — THE COMPACT LEDGER. The smallest redesign that satisfies the brief: the survey keeps the production modal’s paper, brass and pill grammar, but every pill drops its inline definition and becomes a title-only chip, every question becomes one aligned row of a ledger, notes fold behind “+ note”, and the comparison stretches to a five-point Likert strung between the two artworks. Definitions never left — they moved into hover/focus tooltips and a tap-to-open ⓘ legend card. MOBILE PASS: the sheet now answers to its own width (container queries, Safari 16+); below is the desk stage, then the same live instrument clamped to 375px — the phone layout, on your monitor.

1 · The instrument — in the modal frame, fully live

the touch contract — a plain tap on a chip only ever selects; ⓘ is the touch path to the definitions (one legend card per question, tap-outside or esc to close) · on a pointer, hovering any chip floats that level’s definition; with a keyboard, arrow through a row and the tooltip follows the focused chip · the response header — thumbnail and letter — stays pinned while its rows scroll

2 · The narrow proof — the same live instrument, clamped to 375px

the phone treatment (≤430px container; 375×667 baseline, 320px floor) — chips settle into a two-column grid with 8px gutters, every target ≥44px · ⓘ grows a dashed border and the word define; its legend opens IN FLOW as a full-width sheet that pushes the ledger down — no overlay, nothing to trap focus in · the verdict turns 90°: A’s thumbnail anchors the top, B’s the bottom, five full-width stops between them · the pin shrinks to a 42px thumbnail · the brass runs full-width

Notes · what I committed to, what implementation must watch


  
1 · What this is. The minimal-delta rework: the production
  .jd-pill grammar (real radio inside a label, tick + border for
  selection, :focus-within ring, colour never the only signal)
  survives at chip size, stripped of the inline .jd-pill-d
  description that made rate a wall of text. One question is one
  ledger row on a shared label rail; the overall grade is the headline row
  (larger chips, ink-dark label); skip is a dashed, de-emphasized chip; the five
  per-axis note fields fold behind “+ note” (open state, and a
  red ✓ when a folded note has text); the flag/report keeps
  the production checkbox + revealed note verbatim, restyled to the ledger. The
  whole instrument renders from the real taxonomy.json subset
  inlined as data — grades and the five live axes verbatim, defunct axes
  dropped — so a taxonomy edit re-skins the survey with no markup change,
  same as production. Rate and compare collapse into ONE scrolling state; the
  state machine loses a step, the persisted work shape keeps
  ratings + a winner and gains a strength.


  
2 · The tooltip / popover a11y contract. Three routes to every
  definition, none of them the browser title attr: (i)
  pointer — hovering a chip positions the one shared styled tooltip
  (fixed-position, clamped to the viewport, pointer-events:none so
  it can never intercept a rating); (ii) keyboard — the same tooltip
  follows :focus-visible as arrow keys walk the native radiogroup,
  and Esc dismisses it; (iii) touch — a plain tap on a chip only
  ever SELECTS (tap-focus is not :focus-visible, so no tooltip
  flashes); the ⓘ button is the explicit touch affordance, opening an
  anchored legend card listing the question’s full scale (axis description
  as its subhead), aria-expanded/aria-controls wired,
  one card open at a time, Esc or tap-outside to close with focus returned to
  the ⓘ. For screen readers the definitions are not tooltip-dependent at
  all: every radio carries aria-describedby to a visually-hidden
  copy of its level’s definition, so the tooltip layer is pure
  presentation (aria-hidden).


  
3 · The comparison contract (flagging, not inventing). The
  five-point Likert (A · by a mile / A · on
  points / dead even / B · on points / B · by
  a mile) maps onto C1.3’s comparison.winner
  ("a" | "b" | "tie") plus a field the
  contract does not yet have: strength: "decisive" | "slight",
  omitted on a tie. That is a PLAN-USER-PROMPTS-CONTRACTS addition the backend
  owner must ratify (validation, the jd_comparisons row, and what
  degraded single-response mode does — this instrument would simply omit
  the verdict section there, filing comparison: null as today). No
  endpoint changes are designed here; the live payload above only shows the
  proposed shape.


  
4 · What implementation must watch. (i) A note typed under an
  axis whose level was skipped has no home in C1.3 — an axis note rides an
  axis rating entry, which requires a value; the
  payload preview marks these orphans. Decide: prompt for a level, file-and-drop,
  or extend the contract. (This is latent in production too; the ledger’s
  folded notes just make it visible.) (ii) The sticky response pin lives inside
  .jd-turn-scroll and cancels its top padding with a negative
  top — both now read one --scrollpad custom
  property on the scroller, so the narrow band retunes them together; keep any
  future padding change on that var, and mind the banner-band padding math on
  the real fixed scrim. (iii) The
  legend card at desk width is absolutely positioned inside its row, inside the
  scroller, so it scrolls with its question and can overlap the next row —
  acceptable because any outside pointer-down dismisses it first; do not give it
  focusable content without adding a focus trap. (In the phone band it is
  static, in flow — no overlap, no trap needed.) (iv) Tooltips hide on
  scroll (their anchor moves under fixed positioning). (v) Motion is additive:
  the base stylesheet has no transitions or keyframes; they exist only inside
  prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference. (vi) Notes keep
  MAX_NOTE 500 via maxlength; the file action stays
  disabled until the verdict exists, mirroring the production
  winner-before-filing rule when both responses survive.


  
5 · The mobile pass (2026-08-11) — what changed. The
  instrument now responds to its OWN width: .stage-clamp is a
  container-type: inline-size container and every narrow rule is a
  @container query (the sibling mockups’ pattern; Safari 16+
  floor) — the old viewport @media block is gone, so the
  page can stage the same live sheet at desk width and at 375px side by side.
  Two bands. ≤560px: the label rail folds (label above chips, ⓘ
  pushed to the row’s right edge). ≤430px, the phone treatment:
  chips leave flex-wrap for a deliberate two-column grid (grades 2×3
  with skip, axes 2×2, “+ note” on its own full-width
  row) with 8px gutters against mis-taps; every interactive element —
  chips, ⓘ, note, Likert stops, flag row, close — meets the
  44px floor; the pin spends less of the screen (42px thumbnail,
  smaller type) and its sticky offset tracks --scrollpad; the card
  height is svh-based (vh fallback line first), never
  a 100vh trap; text inputs get font-size: 16px (kills iOS
  focus-zoom) plus scroll-margin so a focused field clears the
  sticky pin and the keyboard; the brass files full-width. The Likert turns
  90°: A’s thumbnail anchors the top end, B’s the bottom,
  and the five stops run full-width between them with captions at reading size
  (0.64rem, up from five ≈60px slivers at 0.46rem); the decisive
  ends’ heavy border rotates with the axis (top toward A, bottom toward
  B), so direction still reads before the labels do.


  
6 · The touch definition contract, restated for phones.
  Hover does not exist there, so the ⓘ path carries the full weight:
  in the phone band it grows a dashed border and a visible define label
  (44px target, right-aligned on every question row — a findable place),
  and its legend card opens in flow as a full-width sheet that pushes
  the ledger down inside the card’s own scroll — comfortable at
  320px, impossible to clip, nothing floats over a tappable chip, tap-outside
  and Esc still close it. A plain tap on a chip still only ever selects.
  Tooltips remain pointer/keyboard-only garnish; the aria-describedby
  definitions remain the screen-reader path. Nothing anywhere depends on
  hover.


  
7 · What implementation must watch on real phones. (i) The
  container-query floor is Safari 16 / Chrome 105; production currently uses
  viewport media queries for the modal — port these bands either by
  adopting the clamp-container inside .jd-turn or by translating
  to @media, but pick ONE mechanism there too. Since the real
  modal is near-fullscreen on phones, container width ≈ viewport width
  and the bands translate 1:1. (ii) iOS focus-zoom keys on the input’s
  computed font-size at focus time in the VIEWPORT — the 16px rule must
  actually apply on phones (in production, guard it with the same band that
  is active there; the mockup’s narrow container is only equal to the
  viewport on a real phone). (iii) The virtual keyboard: the note inputs are
  focused with preventScroll then scrollIntoView
  (block:'nearest') and carry scroll-margin, but iOS
  resizes the visual viewport, not the layout viewport — verify the
  field stays visible on a device, and keep
  overscroll-behavior: contain + production’s
  html.jd-turn-open{overflow:hidden} so the internal
  scroll never chains to the page. (iv) svh (Safari 15.4+) with
  the plain-vh fallback declaration FIRST — do not reorder
  the two height lines. (v) -webkit-tap-highlight-color is
  cleared on chips, stops and ⓘ because selection/expansion states are
  drawn explicitly; leave the ghost highlight off anything with its own
  pressed state. (vi) A tap that closes an in-flow legend reflows the ledger
  under the finger; the pointerdown-then-click ordering means the close
  happens first and the click lands on the moved layout — production
  should re-hit-test (as here) rather than cache the target.
