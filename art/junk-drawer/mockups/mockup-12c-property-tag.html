Design Notes · 12C

The move. The current tag is a label; this one is hardware — a municipal property/evidence tag. Three things carry that: the stenciled two-deck header (MUNICIPAL PROPERTY over BUREAU OF SPECIMENS, closed by a double rule, letterpress-debossed into the stock), the ruled requisition fields with micro-labels (ARTICLE / ISSUED BY / DATE), and the grade struck as a rotated double-ring certification seal with the shipped gauge inside it and “PER SCALE JD-5” in microtype. The foot (“REMOVAL BY REQUISITION ONLY” · FORM JD-14 · REV. 7/26) is static chrome, identical on every specimen — the bureau being too serious about a rubber duck is the joke doing the theming.

Stock & die-cut. Kraft #dcc186 — one warm step below the app manila #e8d9a8, so it reads as heavier card in the same family (the app manila reappears as the gauge divider color's sibling: dividers are cut in the TAG's paper #dcc186, per the gauge spec). The blank is die-cut: production's angled wire end at left (point depth grows 14→20px to seat the bigger washer) plus 9px chamfered corners on the free end — polygon(20px 0, calc(100% - 9px) 0, 100% 9px, 100% calc(100% - 9px), calc(100% - 9px) 100%, 20px 100%, 0 50%). Texture is one turbulence grain + a machine-direction fibre streak + an edge vignette, all CSS.

The eyelet. Attachment stays at left edge center — rope-math parity with today. The 7px painted grommet becomes an 18px zinc washer (pure radial-gradient, the hole painted near-black so the wood reads through). Hole center sits at (13px, 50%) vs production's (9.5px, 50%): the rope's tag-anchor offset shifts +3.5px right — one constant in pick()/the Verlet chain, nothing else moves.

Skeleton parity (integration). Same contract as .jd-itemtag: fixed --jd-tag-w 20.5rem / 17rem at ≤768px, max-width: calc(100% - 16px), grows downward only. NAME keeps the two-line reserve (min-height: 2.64em at lh 1.32) and 2-line clamp; the model cell is the wrappable one (flex: 1; min-width: 0), the DATE cell is nowrap; the seal+buttons row keeps flex-wrap as the overflow valve (at 17rem the buttons drop to their own row, shown in ④). Only .pt-name, .pt-v values, the grade word + gauge, and the buttons are injected (escaped text, exactly today's fields); everything else is template chrome.

Watch-outs for the real build. (1) Shadow/clip split: here the drop-shadow lives on a wrapper and the clip on the inner element, so the shadow always follows the die-cut; if the build keeps production's single-element approach, re-test the is-dragging stale-shadow workaround since the filter is now two drop-shadows. (2) meterSVG changes: divider stroke #e8d9a8 → #dcc186; keep per-instance clipPath ids (production already mints them). (3) Seal rotation is transform-only (no layout shift); drop it for the ungraded dashed state, as shown. (4) Stencil font: add Stardos Stencil (700) to the Google Fonts request; fallback chain lands on bold Courier Prime, which still reads as a typed department header — acceptable degrade, no layout dependence on the stencil metrics since the header is nowrap at sizes that fit both fonts at both widths. (5) Cursor contract unchanged: cursor: grab on the tag, pointer only on the two buttons; the seal, header, and foot are inert. (6) The narrow tier hides the foot's small-print line (keeps the form number) — the one content-free concession at 17rem.
