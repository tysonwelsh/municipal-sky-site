THE JUNK DRAWER — RANK THE DRAWINGS: THE LEDGER (ROUND 30, OPTION B)

Designer's note. One vertical ledger, one row per print — thumbnail, pencilled label, drag handle — and the rank numerals written by the visitor's own hand in the margin. Rows arrive in arrival order with the numerals only faintly pencilled, because A-B-C-D is not a judgment: the visitor must either move something or sign off on the order as it stands before the brass button will light. Ties are made with the pencil, not the office's ink — a dashed rule between two rows closes when tapped, the pair pulls together onto a shared ground under one numeral and a brace, and dragging either row out breaks it again; the boundary under first place is printed in ink instead, and says so.

Fewer survivors. Nothing special is needed: with three rows the ledger keeps two boundaries — the locked one under first place and one live tie below it, so 1-2-3 and 1-2-2 are both reachable. With two rows the only boundary that exists is the locked one, so the ledger degrades honestly into a straight this-over-that call, still filed as ranks 1 and 2. Use the switch below to see it.
