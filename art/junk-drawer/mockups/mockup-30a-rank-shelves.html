junk drawer — rank the drawings: THE SORTING RACK (round 30, option A)

Designer's note. The call becomes a piece of shelving: the arrived prints wait in a tray, and under it a rack whose top shelf is singular — deeper paper, a brass lip printed goes in the drawer — takes exactly one print (a second is shaken back with a printed refusal), while every shelf below it holds as many as you like. The graphite numeral on each shelf plate is the dense rank that actually gets filed: an empty shelf is skipped and the count closes up, so the rack can read 1 / 2 / — / 3. Three survivors builds a three-shelf rack and two builds a two-shelf one, so the rack is never taller than the answer it can hold — the bench switch shows both.
