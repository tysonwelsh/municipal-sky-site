# Listening list for v0.34

*About ten minutes. This build is the Kolob 2 branch with the round-2
engine, the polish and the new staff. Open a link and press PLAY; times are
mm:ss from PLAY. Every seed plays a different Sunday than on the live site.*

**How much is new, honestly:** the trombone sound is the one big thing to
judge. The varied openings are big for variety over many visits, but small
within any one. The rest are small fixes you'll notice only at their
moment.

1. **The trombones, reworked (the one to judge).** Each note is now
   tongued, the tone gets brighter as they play louder, notes are gently
   separated, the far choir is no longer muffled, and the near choir is
   about 5 dB louder. They now print on the staff: the far choir pale, the
   near choir full green.
   - http://127.0.0.1:8107/art/kolob/?seed=1847 at 0:06: far choir from
     the left, the near choir answers from the right at 0:17, both together
     at 0:41, and the house returns about 0:50.
   - http://127.0.0.1:8107/art/kolob/?seed=7 at 0:05: a brighter Sunday.
     It now ends open, on a minor chord.
2. **Every visit wakes differently.**
   - ?seed=3: the drone alone, then the field sounds, and the organ not
     until 0:16.
   - ?seed=15: a "brush arbor" morning with no organ, bare fifths on
     strings at 0:07, then the clarinet.
   - ?seed=1: the steeples call the valley in at 0:01.6.
3. **The old tune: quieter, every note re-struck, and on the staff.**
   - ?seed=16 at 0:20: "The Spirit of God" (ASSEMBLY), faint and far off,
     printed as a pale remembered melody. The organ lets go as it comes in.
4. **Also in this build:** fewer fast Sundays (about 15 % of first visits,
   down from 36 %); the organ and strings let go when the marching bands
   arrive (?seed=2 at 4:03); and the engine's round-2 fixes (the
   accompaniment follows the choir, a clean stop and restart).
