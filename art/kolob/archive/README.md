# archive — the build's record

Everything here is superseded and kept only as the record of how the app was built: the layered
contract, the plans, the crews' handoffs and the critics' reports. Seeds, odds, versions, file names
and line numbers in these documents no longer match the code. Nothing here governs.

| folder or file | what it was |
|---|---|
| `SCORE-layered.md` | the contract as a base and five later-wins layers, consolidated into `../SCORE.md` on 2026-10-01 |
| `plans/` | the composition, execution and engraving plans; what they proposed is built, declined (`../OWNER-RULINGS.md`) or open (`../OPEN-WORK.md`); the staff's rules live in `../STAFF.md` |
| `handoff/` | every handoff a later round superseded, and two listening packets whose seeds no longer play those meetings |
| `critic/` | the enrichment briefs and the review panels; every proposal was ruled on |
| `handoff-second-look.md` | an independent re-review of the split, the Question and the v0.31 engraving |
| `mockups/` | page plans and mockups from before the engraving; the three engraving mockups the owner chose from stay in `../mockups/` |

The current documents are `../README.md` (the map), `../OWNER-RULINGS.md`, `../OPEN-WORK.md`,
`../SCORE.md`, `../STAFF.md`, and the kept handoffs in `../handoff/`.
