The one with character: a framed board with a name-plate for the day, five recessed slots with ivory cards hung in them and small brass labels beneath, the direction pinned across the corner as a gilt ribbon (absent when nothing fires), and the seed line as the brass plaque at the foot. Typographic and quiet: the day’s particulars printed on a paper programme card (a hymnal’s own idiom), the direction as a gilt rubric on the same line as the mode and meter, and the three numbers as one printed line rather than hanging cards. The green board holds only the card and the seed line; the two columns and the verse beside are kept.
