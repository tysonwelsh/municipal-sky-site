## voices (branch kolob-voices)

- 1 (medium, fix before CAST uses it). A singer's timing habit and a desk's lag delay the voice but not the mouth. In kolob-voices-vocal.js the throat timeline is built from the nominal t: events at :275-283, consonant windows at :363, vowel-gate switches in the syllable walk, and the inhale at :387. The lag is applied only to each person's onsets, via `lag: D.late + ...` at :571 and `D.late += spec.lag` at :626. Mock (r1/mock.js), old bass with timingHabitMs 95: the 's' of 'sol' sounds at 1.762-1.887 s and the 'oh' bank opens at 1.857-1.887 s, but his pitch leaves 98 Hz only at 1.906 s and lands at 2.057 s. Audio (r1/inpage5.js): the fricative peaks sit at 1.80-1.83 s both with timingHabit 0 and with 95, while his pitch crossings move from -0.02/0.00 s to +0.07/+0.08 s after the beat. So the late man says his consonants and vowels on the beat and moves his pitch about 90 ms later. The same happens to every desk (desk lag 0-70 ms). Fix: shift the throat's whole timeline (ev[].s, consonants, gates, breath) by P.late, and keep only the within-desk spread as per-person lag.
- 2 (medium). Single notes honk up to +8 dB because a harmonic lands on a narrow +16 dB F1 peak. The causes are the fixed F1 gain at :158 and the register key at :322: its `Math.round` reuses an untuned bank when f0*1.08 is only just above F1. On a scale on one vowel, one person's level varies 5-11 dB, with jumps between adjacent notes of up to 8.6 dB. Examples (r1/inpage2.js): soprano 'oo' at 367 Hz reads -14 dB against -21/-23 for its neighbours; tenor 'ee' at 294 Hz reads -15 against -24. On the lab line, through the master chain, the harmony alto's G4 'ah' is +7.8 dB over its neighbours (-20 vs -27/-25), and the soloist's G5 'ah' is +7 dB. 'oo' also comes out as loud as or louder than 'ah' (bass at 98 Hz: oo -21.9, ah -25.7), which is the reverse of real voices. The v0.30 quartet is just as uneven (5-10 dB, r1/inpage4.js), so this is not a regression. But it is the main thing between these voices and 'people', especially the soloists. Fix: give each bank a make-up gain fixed at birth, computed with getFrequencyResponse over the note's first ~10 harmonics weighted by the source tilt, and add an intrinsic-level table per vowel. Tune F1 whenever f0*1.08 > F1 (ceil, not round).
- 3 (medium-low, latent). The envelope collapses into 0.5 ms steps when a note or an ornament piece shorter than about 0.17 s comes before an f/s syllable. `pt()` at :517 pushes colliding breakpoints to lastT+0.0005, the hold point at :551 is max(s5+0.08, ...), and the dip is at :537. Mock (r1/mock2.js), at the lab's own tempo (beat 0.85 x 1.1) on the shape syllables: the precentor drops 0.952->0.059 in 0.5 ms 2-4 times per line, for seeds 1-6. The lined reply on the notes has 22-24 such steps. Any note of 0.16 s or less before fa/sol does the same, for example the short note of a gospel dotted figure at beat 0.6. In the audio (r1/inpage9.js) the formants smooth it into an abrupt, glottal-stop-like cut: HF is +6-8 dB and the d2 ratio is 2.5-3, below any click threshold, so it is not a measured click. No lab demo reaches this path, because lining and the soloists sing vowel-only words. Fix: floor every level change at about 8-10 ms, and pull the hold point in ahead of the next dip.
- 4 (low-medium, lab). The child sings the soprano melody an octave up (voices-lab.js:256 and :288, `oct: 2`), which puts her at D5-D6 (587-1175 Hz). The result is flute-like: f0 dominates and the spectral centroid is 1216 Hz, with confidence 0.55. The plan's 'octave up' is relative to a tune in the tenor. With the tune in S, the child belongs at pitch (oct 1): untrained children top out around G5.
- 5 (low). `born = t - 0.45` (:283) means any call with t < 0.45 s schedules parameter events at negative times (79 params in the mock). Real Web Audio throws RangeError for those, so born should be clamped at 0. The header comment (:52-54) says the inhale leads by ~0.35 s, but the code uses 0.42 (born 0.45). Match it to the handoff.
- 6 (low). Young desks beat more regularly. Five desks score 0.27-0.44 for periodicity; desk 2 (alto, young) scores 0.56, above the lab's own 0.5 pass line (r1/inpage6.js). Young voices get only 2.5 cents of drift (:247) and less vibrato. Consider a floor on drift and vibrato for desks.
- 7 (nit). The handoff voices-1.md is stale. It omits demos 2a/2b (8076fea) and the child change to 1.1x solo and brightness 0.6 (8761428), and its tables predate both. The page heading says 'The six demonstrations' over nine cards. The status line prints 'desks 6 x 3' for 2a/2b, which ignore the knobs.
- 8 (info for CAST). The handoff's 'v0.30 quartet clicks' at 1.534 and 3.586 s are detector false positives. A >11 kHz zoom (r1/zoom.png) shows the band-limited sawtooth's regular edge, once per period, and no discontinuity. The CAST crew should not chase them.
- 9 (info). The CPU figure could not be reproduced under current load. budgetTable(true) gave 6 desks + 2 soloists at 1.8-2.2x real time (8 desks 1.4-1.7x).
