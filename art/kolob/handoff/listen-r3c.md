# Listening packet: round 3c

*For the owner. Round 3c brings this round's new guests into the real
meeting: the Nauvoo band, the handcart company, the gulls, the organist's
variations, change ringing, the gift of tongues, the far ward, the Hosanna
and the Social Hall, and the testimony-bearers who now speak. They share
one guest budget, so about six meetings in ten have a guest. Nothing has
been published, and VERSION is unchanged. Nobody has listened to any of
this yet: everything below was measured silently.*

## How big is this, honestly

- **Ten new things to hear, but each one is rare in natural play.** Apart
  from the band, every guest comes in about one to five meetings in a
  hundred (the table below). A 15-minute meeting will usually have one
  guest or none, and most often it is the band. So the seeds below take you
  straight to each guest. The owner's own 36 % for the band was kept, so the
  band is about half of all the guests you'll hear.
- **The biggest new sounds:** the Social Hall (a fiddle dance instead of
  the postlude, about 90 seconds), the Hosanna (Easter and dedications: a
  shouted "Hosanna" three times, then "The Spirit of God" by the full ward
  and organ, about two minutes), and the organist's variations (two to
  three minutes).
- **The biggest change you'll hear across visits:** the testimony now has
  people in it. In about six meetings in ten, two or three of the ward rise
  in turn and "speak". It's a speaking voice with no words, and the
  harmonium or the clarinet plays their phrases back as a tune.
- **Small ones:** the gulls (about ten seconds), the far ward (a second
  congregation across the valley, quiet, a line behind ours), and change
  ringing (a far tower's bells, about a minute).
- **Every seed now plays a different meeting from round 3b whenever a guest
  changed.** The guests' odds are new, and the band that marches replaced
  the looping fife. From here on a seed plays the same meeting every time.

## How to listen

The local server is running. Open a seed and press PLAY:

> http://127.0.0.1:8175/art/kolob/index.php?seed=22

- The times below (mm:ss) are the page's own clock, shown beside each line
  of the clerk's minutes. They count from when you press PLAY. They were
  read from the test harness; the browser plays the same seed the same way.
- `&latin=1` shows the minutes in English.
- **The guest switch (for listening only):** add `&guest=<name>` to ask for
  that guest in every meeting of the visit. For example,
  `…index.php?seed=9&guest=gulls`. The names are `bands`, `handcart`,
  `gulls`, `variations`, `changes`, `tongues`, `farward`, `hosanna`,
  `socialhall` and `testimony`. A switched meeting is not the natural one,
  so its times differ from the table below. The switch can't add a rite: the
  gift and the bearers need a testimony, and the Hosanna needs Easter or a
  dedication. The seeds below need no switch; each guest comes there on its
  own.

## How often each guest comes (the guest budget)

About **six meetings in ten** have a guest. There are never more than two,
and never two side by side. Measured over 1,000 first meetings, two samples:

| guest | how often | busiest Sunday |
|---|---|---|
| the Nauvoo band | 35–36 % | Pioneer Day, 3 in 4 |
| the trombones at dawn | 4–5 % | Easter, Christmas, funerals |
| the handbells | 5 % | Christmas, Easter, weddings |
| the old tune | 4 % | fast Sundays, funerals |
| variations on a hymn | 4 % | Conference, Pioneer Day |
| the Hosanna | 3–5 % (Easter about half, a dedication almost always) | only those two |
| the Social Hall | 3–4 % | Pioneer Day and weddings, about 1 in 5 |
| the steeples (about half of them ring changes) | 3 % | Christmas |
| the singing school | 3 % | ordinary Sundays |
| the far ward | 3 % | fast Sundays, Conference |
| the gift of tongues | 2.5 % | fast Sundays, dedications |
| the gulls | 2 % | Pioneer Day |
| the handcart company | 1.5 % | Pioneer Day |
| *(the testimony-bearers: not a guest)* | *63 %* | *fast Sundays, nearly always* |

**To change how often a guest comes,** edit one number in
`kolob-calendar.js` → `GUEST_ODDS`. There's one row per guest and one
column per Sunday, and each number is the chance that guest is asked to
that Sunday. The rules (two at most, one showpiece, never side by side)
are in `GUEST_BUDGET` just below it. Changing a number moves nothing else
in the meeting.

## Things left for your ear

- **The band's share.** It is the band 35 % of the time and every other
  guest together about 25 %. If you want the new guests heard more often,
  lower the band's row.
- **Where the band crosses.** In the real meeting the band crosses a hymn's
  verse 7 times in 10 and the doxology's 3 in 10. It leaves the doxology
  whenever the doxology has another payoff (the partner hymn, the refrain,
  the withheld tune) or the Hosanna. It is seated there first 7 times in 10.
  To keep it out of the doxology altogether, change its seats
  (`GuestBands.SEATS.usual`, critic A's lever).
- **The Hosanna's shout.** How bright it is (`SHOUT_EFFORT`), and whether it
  should give way to a guest beside its doxology (`YIELD`; today it
  doesn't).
- **The far ward's voices.** It sings with 24 throats of its own; eight
  "pews" of three would cost less.
- **The testimony.** Whether a speaking voice without words reads as a
  person bearing testimony.
- **Levels.** Every level was set by measurement against the organ, not by
  ear.
