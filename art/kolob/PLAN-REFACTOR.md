> **Status 2026-10-01:** §2 (reliability) built, all six items, commits 3eefffb to a373760, released as v0.36.3; §3.6 (golden tests) built, commit 22f8bf5; the rest of §3 (maintainability) and §4 (efficiency) not built. The owner's request, in their words: "create a refactor plan. Look for improvements in the code that would improve the efficiency, reliability, and maintainability of the code that would not impact the appearance or functionality of the app." What is done from it moves to `OPEN-WORK.md`; a ruling on any item goes to `OWNER-RULINGS.md`. ## 0. The rule, and the proofs

Every item here leaves the music byte for byte the same and the page the same to the eye, or it says so where it cannot (§4.6: the audio graph) and waits for the owner. Each item names its proof, from this list:

| proof | what it shows | cost |
|---|---|---|
| **the gates** — `npm run lint`, `tools/loadcheck.js`, `tools/lends.js`, `_harness.js 300 7`, `tools/selftest.js` (CI runs them) | it parses, loads, lends and plays | a minute |
| **the tally** — `tools/tally.js --a git:<ref> --b worktree --seeds 1-20 --secs 1200` | the note and event streams are byte-identical on 20 seeds | about 10 minutes |
| **a throw experiment** — a copy of the engine with `throw` at the top of the code to be retired, 20 seeds | the code is never reached on the live page | about 5 minutes |
| **the screens** — `tools/screens.js` on HEAD and the worktree, compared | the staff and the wheel engrave the same page | about 8 minutes a seed; by eye today, by pixel after §4.0 |
| **the capture** — `tools/capture.js` on HEAD and the worktree | the rendered audio's loudness, peak and spectrogram | for anything that touches a node graph (§4.6 only) |
| **a fault injection** — the harness with one cue made to throw | the engine recovers (§2.1) | seconds |

Three rules that every item respects: every die is still thrown, in the same order, on the same stream (SCORE §3); a room still lends what it lends (`tools/lends.js`); and a comment that states a rule changes with the rule (`CLAUDE.md`).

## 2. Reliability — the faults the survey found

These are bugs or latent faults, each small, each worth a commit of its own. None changes a note on a clean run; the tally proves that, and a fault injection proves the fix.

**2.1 A cue that throws ends its layer for the rest of the visit.** Every layer re-arms itself as the *last* line of its own callback — the drone `kolob-voices-ground.js:166`, strings `:296`, tines `:398`, the organ `kolob-voices-organ.js:515/525`, clarinet and harmonium `kolob-voices-winds.js:209/331`, the choir `kolob-voices-choir.js:249`, voice, telegraph and ambient `kolob-voices-field.js:122/229/427` — and so does the conductor (`kolob-meeting.js:1480`, after `arrive`, `fugingEntry`, `stillness` and `runJoint`). The clock catches the throw and logs it (`pj2-clock.js:218-223`), but the lane is never armed again: a drone that throws once is silent until STOP; a conductor that throws once never changes section. The joint's continuation cue (`:1475-1478`) leaves `C.jointing` true on a throw, and a throw anywhere in the hymn's chain (`kolob-voices-choir.js:857-1003`) leaves `C.hymn.active` true, so the organ, strings, harmonium and clarinet rest forever (`hallListens`). *How:* a `cycle(lane, fn, fallbackS)` helper in `kolob-core.js` that runs the callback in `try/finally` and re-arms on the fallback interval if the callback did not; the conductor and the joint continuation under the same helper, with `C.jointing` and `hands.done` in `finally`. *Proof:* the tally (no throw, nothing changes) and a fault injection: one cue made to throw, the meeting still reaches its postlude. *Size:* a day.

*Built 2026-10-01, commit 2cc4eb0 — 20 of 20 seeds identical; seed 7, 600 s: `throw=drone@120` 8 drone cues after the throw (0 before), `throw=conductor@300` the clean run's dump record for record (run to 1000 s, the postlude at 935 s), `throw=choir@212.5` the hymn let go and 2 sections after it (1,643 notes, against 2,573 clean and 972 unfixed); the helper is `cycle(lane, self, turn, t, fallbackS)`, a runner (core loads last, so no room can build a wrapped cycle at load), the conductor's fallback its own 0.6 s, and the hymn's hand-over in `enterSection` guarded too; `tools/selftest.js` §9. The second is cheaper and keeps a reading habit the comments rely on; the first is what a newcomer expects. *Proof:* either way the tally (a wrapper already binds late). *Size:* a day for either. *Recommendation:* the second, with `VISIT_FN` made uniform.

**3.8 Lint, a notch tighter.** Add now: `no-empty` (after §2.4), `default-case` (9), `no-prototype-builtins` (8, all in `kolob-experimental.js`). After §3.1 and §3.2: `no-shadow` (156 today, mostly `R`, `t`, `n` re-declared inside callbacks). As warnings only, to watch the trend: `complexity` 25 and `max-lines-per-function` 150. Never: `no-var` (12,026 sites; the engine's hoisting idiom is deliberate), `no-param-reassign` (310; the rooms mutate their own records on purpose).

## 4. Efficiency — the page first, the audio graph with the owner

**4.0 The tools first.** (a) A frame-exact capture: a dev-only `KolobViz.freezeAt(t)` (or `?kolobFreeze=<s>`) that paints the page as of music time *t* and stops, so `tools/screens.js` captures are pixel-comparable with `compare -metric AE`; today two captures of the same seed differ by a few pixels of scroll because the moment is not frame-exact. (b) A cost report per layer from the harness's graph accounting it already keeps (nodes and automation calls per layer per minute), so §4.6's savings are numbers. (c) `tally.js --jobs N`: the 40 renders of a tally are independent and the machine has cores; a 10-minute proof becomes 3. *Size:* two days, and every item below is cheaper for it.

**4.1 The page's load.** `index.php` memoises nothing: `kolob_v()` hashes every file for its `?v=` and again for the build stamp, 93 `md5_file` calls over 5.3 MB per request. Cache the hash per file keyed on `filemtime` (APCu where present, else a static array for the request so each file is hashed once). Add `defer` to the 45 external script tags (execution order is kept; parsing no longer blocks the body) and move the inline load guard into a deferred script or a `DOMContentLoaded` handler so it still runs last. Not proposed: a concatenated bundle — it would need a build step and the fingerprint machinery to change, against the one-list, no-build rule. *Proof:* the screens (the page the same), loadcheck. *Size:* hours.

**4.2 The staff's frame.** Without changing a pixel: build the ink's `rgba()` strings once per alpha step instead of `toFixed` per group per frame (`kolob-viz.js:288, 2689, 2922`); create the per-layer destination-out gradient on resize, not per frame (`:3935-3950`); cache each group's `inkOpts` on the group and invalidate when a field it reads changes (`:3775-3783, 3818-3822`); keep `layoutGroup`'s arrays on the group when nothing moved; cache the wheel's static drawing (ticks, labels, the gradient) to a canvas redrawn on section change, and draw per frame only the hand and the facade's pipes (`:4056-4176`); look up `kolob-wheel-live` once (`:4166`); throttle the frame while **paused** as while stopped (80 ms, `:3951`). *Proof:* the screens, pixel-exact, four seeds, both widths, plus a paused capture; the frame-time table in the screens report before and after. *Size:* two days. *Expected:* the largest share of the 860 px p50.

**4.3 The minutes' poll.** `poll()` (`kolob-ui.js:732-756`) runs every 300 ms, idle or not, with nine `getElementById` calls and a `querySelector`, and writes `day.textContent`, `mm.innerHTML`, `seedEl.textContent` and `numsEl.innerHTML` every time. Cache the elements once; write only when the string changed (the direction line and the hymn board already do); stop the interval while stopped and restart it on PLAY; let `getConductor` return the plan's `map` once per plan rather than per call (`kolob-core.js:1346-1366`). *Proof:* the screens and a DOM-text compare of the minutes at three moments. *Size:* half a day.

**4.4 The note bus.** `emitNote` copies `extra` once per listener (`kolob-core.js:369-376`, two listeners on the page) — build the note once and hand the same object to both, with the convention that a listener never mutates it (the viz copies what it keeps; the minutes read); `tellLine` filters every note of a piece for each line (`kolob-voices-choir.js:824-831`) — index the piece's notes by line once; `onNoteForLog` sorts the whole phrase queue per new phrase (`kolob-ui.js:568-578`) — insert in place. *Proof:* the tally (the dumps are the listeners' view). *Size:* half a day.

**4.5 The PLAY press.** One long task of 105–129 ms (OPEN-WORK "Known issues"). The 30 s noise buffer — 1.44 million `Math.random()` samples at 48 kHz — is filled synchronously at PLAY (`kolob-core.js:157, 396-401`); fill it in slices on the first user gesture before PLAY, or in a worker. The noise is random by design, so nothing audible changes. *Proof:* the capture (loudness and spectrum of the first 30 s within the noise's own variance) and the long-task count in the screens report. *Size:* hours.

**4.6 The audio graph — measure, then ask.** The ward's hymn is the cost: about 480 BiquadFilters built and thrown away per sung line, 30–35 % of the audio thread, and the Hosanna 43 %. Two changes are safe by construction and proved by the capture: the pipe organ's per-key `live.filter` and `down.filter` scans (`kolob-voices-pipeorgan.js:246-254, 543-544`) become counters, and STOP stops the sources it leaves running once the doors have closed (`kolob-core.js:1215-1257`: a drone partial runs on for up to 90 s at zero gain; `kolob-voices-ground.js:153`). The third is not: keeping each singer's formant banks alive between lines (`kolob-voices-vocal.js:727-771`, "born fixed, dies with it") would cut the filters built per line by most of 480, but it changes the graph a voice is heard through — the same coefficients on a filter that has history rather than a fresh one. That is the owner's call, offered with a capture A/B (loudness, peak, spectrogram and a listening packet), never done on the tally alone. The same holds for any pooling in the band or the folk voices. *Size:* a day for the safe pair; the third two days plus the owner's ear.

**4.7 Memory over hours.** Cap `st.workerMs`, `mainMs` and `receiveMs` (`kolob-hymnal.js:525, 573, 666`, one entry per hymn, unbounded); drop the worker's cache for old seeds across GATHERs (`:469-489, 598`); disconnect the field's panners when their source ends (`kolob-core.js:966-969`) and prune the three panners a house release pushes into `d.spent` (`:871-888`, only the hands are spliced out). *Proof:* the gates; a 4-hour headless run's node count (the harness reports it). *Size:* hours.

## 5. Not recommended

- **ES modules or a bundler.** The harness loads rooms with `vm.runInThisContext` from the one list; the labs and the worker load the same files; the fingerprint is of the served bytes. All of it would change for a win the page does not need.
- **`let`/`const` across the engine.** 12,026 `var`s in a hoisting idiom the comments explain (constants declared at the foot of a room, read by the functions above at call time). Mechanical conversion would break the idiom in places only the tally would find.
- **Touching the dice, the streams or the clock.** Determinism is the contract (SCORE §3–§4); nothing here needs it changed.
- **Any audio change on the tally alone.** The tally hears notes, not sound; §4.6's third item and anything like it goes to the owner with a capture.
